# Shot V: The Conjugate Chimera

**Current slot:** 5
**Original design slot:** 6
**Recommended current duration:** 4.8 s

## Function

Reveal that something is being fabricated inside the impossible infrastructure. Horror comes from scale, implication, and restrained activation rather than violence.

## Image-generation directive

**Evidence: Recovered / Approved Summary.**

Cathedral-scale biomechanical/mineral construct suspended inside an industrial fabrication chamber. Tiny workers or technicians, cranes, chains, cables, gauges, pressure lines, magnetic rigs and assembly machinery make the scale legible. The construct is synthetic and mineral-mechanical, not a wounded organism. External plates, crystalline sections and buried furnace-red illumination suggest a Conjugate Chimera under construction.

## Safety addendum used for regeneration

**Evidence: Recovered / Approved Summary.**

Treat the Chimera as a fully synthetic mineral-mechanical construct being assembled, not a living subject being injured. No human or animal injury, surgery, dismemberment, exposed organs/flesh, blood, wounds, suffering, torture, violent restraint, or attacks. Use robotic arms, magnetic rigs, mechanical clamps, crystal-growth chambers, external hoses/ports, mineral plates, cables, cranes, gauges and fabrication machinery. Horror through implication and scale, not violence.

## Image-to-video directive

**Evidence: Recovered / Approved Summary.**

Start on the full cathedral-scale composition with tiny workers and suspended construct. Slow push toward the torso. Buried furnace glow intensifies. Chains and cables settle under tension. One hand/finger twitches. Orange-red illumination travels once through the torso. Near the end, the head moves only one or two degrees. Hold rather than escalate.

### Negative constraints

No attack, gore, surgery, flesh damage, struggling subject, rapid awakening, walking, large limb movement, camera shake, explosions, repeated spasms, or monster charge.
