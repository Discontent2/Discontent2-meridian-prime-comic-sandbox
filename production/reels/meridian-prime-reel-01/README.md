## Core visual grammar

Crushed blacks, midnight navy, cobalt or spectral-blue illumination, tiny ember-red accents, monumental scale, tiny humans, restrained motion, and hard cuts. Shot IV is the deliberate warm rupture into deep crimson, ember orange, soot, oxidized metal, and mineral heat.

## Editing grammar

- immediate opening image
- hard cuts only
- no dissolves or transition effects
- nearly motionless tableaux with slow push-ins
- recognition or pressure event once per shot
- no logo, glitch overlay, film-damage effect, or decorative outro
- hard cut to black/silence at the end
