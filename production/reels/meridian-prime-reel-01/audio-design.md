## Sound philosophy

The soundtrack is background score, not a song showcase. No lyrics. Effects should behave like evidence from the environment: pressure changes, distant machinery, breath-like architecture, cable tension, mineral resonance. Avoid trailer-boom overload or constant horror stingers.
