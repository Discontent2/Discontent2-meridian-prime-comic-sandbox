## Canon findings relevant to the shot

The Thunder Egg / Stone Heart is already main canon.

Naming:

- World Works / WWISSaRD classification: **Thunder Egg**
- Common Meridian translation: **Stone Heart**
- Aeonolacertian sacred sense: **the heart that remembers pressure**

Location hierarchy:

`Anticlinal Craton -> Inner Anticline -> primordial swamp -> Thunder Egg / Stone Heart`

The Inner Anticline is a half-exposed geode within the larger Craton, roughly one-eighth its size. Its collapsed top forms a huge cave-like domed basin containing primordial swamp. The Stone Heart stands at the center.

The Stone Heart is a **freestanding node the size of a mountain**. It is visually, culturally, politically and scientifically important. Canon permits competing interpretations such as sacred geography, geological anomaly, WWISSaRD research target, World Works resource interest, mythic landmark, political leverage object, spectacle magnet, possible planetary pressure node and local mystery.

### Protected mystery guardrail

Do NOT establish the Stone Heart as a proven:

- supernatural device
- Core mechanism
- gateway
- machine-control source
- metaphysical master key
- source of Aeonolacertian origins
- source of all Meridian Prime crystal growth

Its power in the reel comes from significance without explanation. Everyone behaves as though it matters; the image should not prove why.

## Sandbox-only possibilities worth preserving as rumor / visual pressure

Earlier sandbox development floated interpretations including pressure node, storm resonator, old ritual-engineering focus, planetary mineral heart, WWISSaRD research obsession, World Works resource target, mythic survivor symbol and arena-calendar anchor. The sandbox also contains a deliberately unresolved Red Glass story idea in which forbidden Craton-era modifications appear to react to the Thunder Egg, plus the pitch **Thunder Egg Silence**, where every machine in a sold-out Geodeum kneels toward the Stone Heart at once. These may inspire unease but must not be treated as proof of machine-control canon.

## Shot IV visual thesis

A vast humid primordial swamp inside the Inner Anticline. Black water, reeds, crystal outgrowths, mist, pressure haze and immense geode walls establish depth. Through the fog, the viewer gradually recognizes a mountain-sized freestanding geologic node: the Stone Heart.

Desired feeling:

- ancient
- sovereign
- geologic
- sacred
- politically charged
- ominously still
- important without explanation

The Stone Heart should initially be readable as distant landscape. Scale recognition is the reveal.

## Still-image directive

Vertical 9:16 cinematic Meridian Prime realism. Inside the Inner Anticline, show a primordial swamp contained by a massive collapsed geode basin: black water, wet reeds, crystal roots/outgrowths, humid mist, pressure haze and high curved geode walls fading into darkness.

At the center stands the Thunder Egg / Stone Heart, a freestanding geologic node the size of a mountain. It must look ancient, mineral and monumental rather than mechanical, magical or polished sci-fi. Keep it partly obscured by swamp mist. Use tiny distant boats, figures, ritual platforms or restrained infrastructure only as scale cues.

Lighting should be dim and storm-heavy, using indirect glints, reflection, silhouette and occasional storm-light rather than obvious internal energy. Scientific instruments, if present, remain visually secondary.

No text, logos, portal, energy beam, floating object, literal heart anatomy or obvious supernatural mechanism.

## Image-to-video motion thesis

**One event: the environment registers a pressure change around the Stone Heart.**

Suggested approximately five-second behavior:

1. Camera drifts / pushes very slowly through swamp mist and over black water.
2. Reeds, wet vegetation and haze move subtly and naturally.
3. Fog shifts enough to reveal more of the Stone Heart's true mountain-scale silhouette.
4. Near the end, one restrained pressure event passes through the environment: faint ripples move across the water, mist shivers, nearby vegetation trembles, and a brief geologic glint or reflected storm-light travels across one mineral facet.
5. Hold briefly after the event.

The Stone Heart itself should remain mostly still. The landscape reacting is more unsettling than making the object visibly perform.

## Negative motion constraints

No:

- portal opening
- energy beam
- literal heartbeat / flesh pulse
- floating or levitating rock
- large-scale morphing
- obvious machine behavior
- repeated pulses
- explosive lightning strike
- creature emergence
- camera orbit
- rapid zoom
- spectacle-heavy VFX
