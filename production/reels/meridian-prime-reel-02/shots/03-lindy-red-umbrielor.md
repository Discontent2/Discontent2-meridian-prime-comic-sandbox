## Approved still

The approved source still shows Lindy beside the front blade of Red Umbrielor at night under cold moonlight. The machine fills most of the background, with wet dark ground, crystal formations, fog/steam, rusted industrial surfaces and multiple warm work lights.

The still is treated as the desired **end-frame composition** for the animation.

## Owner-directed motion concept

The animation should begin **closer to Lindy from behind**, with the back of Lindy's jacket dominating the foreground. Lindy walks away from the camera toward Red Umbrielor, approaches the front blade, turns slightly and leans against it. The animation should resolve naturally into the supplied still composition.

Lindy's movement is definite and readable: a short purposeful walk, natural jacket and hair movement, then a quiet settling action against the blade. Her performance should remain guarded, tired, competent and alert rather than theatrical.

Red Umbrielor remains stationary but **idles heavily for the entire shot**. The tracks do not rotate and the vehicle does not advance. Suggested idle behavior:

- low mechanical vibration through hood, cab, blade, pipes and exhaust stacks
- intermittent exhaust vapor
- faint hose / exposed-plumbing tremor
- ground mist and condensation shivering near the tracks
- steady work lights with only minute engine vibration

## Camera thesis

Start behind Lindy at medium-close distance and track slowly with her as she walks toward the machine. Ease into the final reference framing as she reaches and leans on the blade. No orbit, whip, jump zoom or dramatic reframing.

## Continuity constraints

The generated animation was judged usable with **CapCut clipping/trimming** rather than requiring another generation. The important retained beat is: back-of-jacket foreground entry -> Lindy approaches machine -> Lindy leans -> reference still becomes the end-frame composition.
