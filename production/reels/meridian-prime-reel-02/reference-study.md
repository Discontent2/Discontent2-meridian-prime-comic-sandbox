## What makes the reference work

### 1. It is a dream itinerary, not a conventional trailer

There is no obvious dialogue scene, exposition card, plot explanation or action montage. The viewer travels through discrete images that feel related by atmosphere rather than geography.

### 2. Most images receive enough time to become uncomfortable

After the short opener, the reel settles into roughly 6.4-7.6 second holds. That is substantially slower than conventional social-video cutting. Each image gets one readable action or atmospheric event.

### 3. Alternation prevents visual fatigue

The sequence oscillates among:

- place
- character/creature
- beauty
- empty place
- threat
- human action
- destination
- ritual/activation
- rest
- shock

This is more important than copying any individual subject.

### 4. Saturated color is structural

Cobalt / electric blue functions as the base dream color. Green fog/fire creates a second supernatural channel. Red/orange appears as small high-value interruptions: candles, torch, foreground omen, final fire.

For Meridian Prime, preserve the **color-role logic** while using our own palette and materials.

### 5. Motion is singular

Each tableau is mostly static, but one thing changes enough to justify video:

- cloud or atmospheric drift
- eating / hand movement
- gentle contact
- fog advance
- watcher gesture
- torch flare
- push toward foreground object/destination
- activation / lightning
- sleeping or breathing stillness
- abrupt final face

The lesson is not 'more animation.' It is **one motion thesis per shot**.

### 6. The final stinger changes the memory of the reel

The long quiet ninth shot appears to offer closure. The approximately 1.4-second final image breaks that contract. It does not need explanation; its job is to infect the previous 58 seconds retroactively.

## Meridian Prime adaptation principles

Keep:

- 9 full tableaux + 1 short stinger
- hard cuts
- 59-ish second runtime
- one continuous music bed
- one dominant base palette with two controlled accent families
- alternating human / nonhuman / empty / monumental images
- one motion thesis per shot
- false ending before the stinger

Do not copy:

- unicorn
- wizard
- castle
- troll/orc
- fantasy knight
- exact color treatment
- exact compositions
- literal magic circles or spellcasting

Translate through Meridian Prime materials: failed routes, old infrastructure, crystal storms, signal culture, survey objects, industrial ritual, isolated stations, strange fauna, route memory, field rumor, and local anomalies.
