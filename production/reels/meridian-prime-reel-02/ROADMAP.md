## North Star

Reel 02 should feel like the viewer has found a route-memory recording from Meridian Prime: beautiful, mundane, industrial, lonely, and increasingly wrong. It should not explain the route. It should make the route feel remembered by something other than the traveler.

Reel 01 was compressed monumental horror. Reel 02 should be **longer, more human-scale, more dreamlike, more varied, and deceptively gentle before the final contamination.**

|---|---:|---|---|
| I | 4.0 s | Landmark / invitation | show a distant Meridian landmark or obsolete infrastructure under impossible weather |

## Palette plan

Do not clone the reference reel's candy-blue look. Translate its color hierarchy.

### Base family

- black / graphite
- polar midnight blue
- dirty cyan / instrument blue
- low-saturation ice and mineral surfaces

### Accent family A: living/anomalous

- spectral mineral green
- oxidized teal
- sick auroral green

Use sparingly in Shots III, IV or VIII.

### Accent family B: human/industrial heat

- sodium orange
- emergency red
- furnace amber

Use as interruptions in Shots II, VI, VII and/or X.

Never allow all three families to peak in the same shot.

---

## Motion law

Each shot gets **one motion thesis**.

Examples:

- one cloud layer moves against another
- one machine repeats a mundane action
- one animal turns its head
- fog advances toward camera
- one watcher raises or lowers an object
- one flare catches
- foreground marker slowly enlarges during a push
- one signal pulse crosses equipment
- one sleeping figure breathes
- final image appears and is gone

Avoid multi-action chaos, frantic morphing, constant camera movement, combat coverage, or a different effect every second.

## PHASE 3 — Still-image production

**Status: WAITING**

Generate one shot at a time.

Workflow per shot:

1. write image-generation directive from repo source
2. generate still
3. evaluate composition before animation
4. correct still if needed
5. save approved prompt + negative constraints
6. move to next shot only after approval

Do not use animation to repair a weak still.

Target still grammar:

- vertical 9:16
- readable silhouette on phone
- strong foreground / middle / background depth
- one dominant focal event
- enough negative space for atmosphere
- no fake UI/text/signage unless diegetically necessary

## PHASE 6 — Music and sound design

**Status: WAITING**

Use one continuous music bed across the full reel.

Meridian Prime music rule:

- instrumental only
- no lyrics
- heavy synth foundation
- late-1970s / 1980s influence
- dark fantasy rather than synthwave nostalgia
- slow-to-mid pulse
- enough negative space for environmental sounds
- no giant EDM drop

Sound design should be sparse. Candidate event classes:

- route/wind/world tone
- one mundane close sound in Shot II
- one beautiful/nonthreatening environmental sound in Shot III
- threshold pressure/fog sound in Shot IV
- tool/flare/relay sound in Shot VI
- activation event in Shot VIII
- near-silence in Shot IX
- abrupt contaminated hit or impossible vocal-like texture in Shot X

Do not build a trailer-boom orchestra on top of the music.

# Anti-convolution rules

1. **Roadmap beats chat drift.** If later discussion conflicts with this roadmap, explicitly revise the roadmap before acting.
2. **One phase at a time.** Do not jump from farming directly to animation.
3. **One shot function per slot.** A cool image does not belong if it duplicates a neighboring dramatic job.
4. **Repo source required.** Phase 1 candidates must cite a source path or be labeled clearly as production-only invention.
5. **No mystery solving.** A reel may imply. It may not answer protected lore questions.
6. **Lock decisions visibly.** Use `LOCKED`, `APPROVED`, `REJECTED`, or `RECONSTRUCTED` in shot records.
7. **Preserve rejected useful ideas.** Do not silently recycle rejected shot concepts into later slots.
8. **Update current-status after every major phase.** That file is the shortest handoff for future sessions.
