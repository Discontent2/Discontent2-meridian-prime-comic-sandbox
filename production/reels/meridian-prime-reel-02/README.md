## Working concept spine

**Provisional title:** `THE ROUTE DREAMS BACK`

Build a Meridian Prime dream-itinerary reel that feels less like Reel 01's compressed cosmic-horror trailer and more like a sequence of discovered tableaux along a failed route. The reel may brush against 409-style failed-map / pressure-system ideas, but it must not define what 409 truly is or solve protected mysteries.

## Translation rule

Borrow the reference reel's cinematic grammar, not its literal fantasy nouns.

Do not make a Meridian unicorn, wizard, castle, troll, or knight reskin. Translate each reference shot by **dramatic function**: landmark, mundane impossibility, false beauty, empty threshold, watcher, human agency, destination/omen, activation, lull, contamination stinger.
