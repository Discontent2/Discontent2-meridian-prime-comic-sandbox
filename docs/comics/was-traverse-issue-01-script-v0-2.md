
### Traverse Crew Visual Rule

Routine Traverse crews do not normally wear helmets during travel, load checks, or refueling.

Default visual language:

```text
goggles
route shades
work glasses
collar mics
cold-weather field gear
breather scarves or masks when needed
chest lamps, tool lamps, cab lamps, vehicle lamps, and work lamps
helmet racks visible but unused
```

Helmets are reserved for crevasse training, rescue, rope work, vertical descent, known surface-collapse response, or hard-shell hazard operations.

### III-Era Mixed Crew Rule

This present-day crew includes, at minimum:

```text
Human Rookie Lead Driver
Saurasapien Traverse Lead
Antisapien Signal Tech
Robot Load Tech
```

Species and culture should appear through silhouette, posture, hands, voice, equipment, and work role. Do not stop the incident to explain species politics.

### Rookmask Jack Rule

Issue #1 does not explain what Rookmask Jack is.

It may hint:

```text
servo-stillness
metal foot under robe
birdlike cowl as service housing
staff contact node
relay responds as if Jack has credentials
machine reflection overlays Mark Aggressive's human personnel identity
```

Core hidden principle:

```text
Rookmask Jack is not Mark Aggressive's body.
Rookmask Jack is what Mark Aggressive can still reach.
```

## PAGE 1
### The Lead Tractor Sees the Road
**4 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Large horizontal opener. Interior of the lead tractor cab, looking out through a fogged windshield. Headlights tunnel into black-blue mineral mist. Wet frozen flats glitter under the light. A neat line of black route flags leads forward into the dark.

A helmet rack sits behind the seat, unused. The cab feels more like a route worker's machine than a spacecraft: thermal coat, route goggles hanging from a hook, collar mic cable, battered cup, taped hazard map.

**CAPTION:**  
The approved route was clean.

**Panel 2**  
Closer inside cab. The HUMAN ROOKIE LEAD DRIVER watches the flags with tired focus. Helmet off. Work goggles pushed up on his forehead. Dashboard glow paints his face cyan and amber. Wiper streaks cut the glass. Condensation gathers at the edges.

**CAPTION:**  
The flags were cleaner.

**Panel 3**  
Close on the route monitor. The approved GPS route line bends slightly left. The physical flags seen through the windshield continue straight.

No one is looking at the monitor.

**SFX:**  
tik  
tik  
tik

**Panel 4**  
Tight reflection in the windshield. For one instant the cab reflection looks older than the cab itself: a different dash shape, a different seat silhouette, a different route label underneath the modern display. It is too faint to read.

**CAPTION:**  
That was the first problem.

## PAGE 22
### Final Splash: The Old File Opens
**Full-page splash**

Composite page. Rookmask Jack stands outside by the relay mast, reflected in cab glass, goggle lenses, route shades, fuel-cache screens, and wet black metal. The Traverse train sits trapped in fog and work lights around the fuel cache.

Jack's reflected body reads as machine now if the reader looks closely: robe over route-service limbs, cowl as service housing, staff as authentication tool.

On the central awakened relay display, an old WISSARD file finally resolves:

```text
W.A.S. TRAVERSE
CORE TRANSIT INCIDENT
PERSONNEL STATUS: UNRESOLVED
```

Below it, a corrupted human personnel ID clears just enough:

```text
MARK AGGRESSIVE
TRAVERSE LEADER / LEAD SCIENCE OFFICER
```

Jack's machine reflection overlays the screen without explaining why.

At the bottom, another line appears:

```text
PHASE TWO AUTHORIZED
```

The crew came for fuel.

**CAPTION:**  
They found proof the road had done this before.
