## Visual decisions to carry forward

- Gothic graphic-comic finish: hard black masses, economical contour lines, flat cold cobalt/slate-violet planes, graphic fog, isolated dirty amber work light, sparing cyan route instruments. Reserve signal red for a later story beat.
- Machinery is lived-in heavy Traverse equipment, with levers, worn gauges, cab glass, and a physical route; it is neither a spaceship cockpit nor a train.
- Preserve the readable geography: physical black flags **straight**, approved route line **left**. Future pages may change camera angle but should not invert the mistake.
- The rookie is alert, tired, and credible. His mistake comes from plausible reliance on the cleaner physical flags. He does not yet know that old route memory is present.
- The old-cab reflection is deniable and contains no readable W.A.S. personnel name, C.A.S.E. material, Flagstaff, Jasper, Jack, or relay tower.
- Page 1 establishes the visual baseline for the rest of Issue 01; later high-drama pages may increase red, black-sky pressure, and cryptid silhouettes without making this opening retroactively explicit.
