## Script Guardrails

```text
The present-day crew lives the incident.
The W.A.S. crew haunts the evidence.
```

This script keeps C.A.S.E. buried for later, reveals W.A.S. only on Page 22, and uses bleedthrough as equipment ghosts, reflections, route logic, old telemetry, warning cadence, and corrupted signal rather than full flashback.

Production exclusions for Issue #1:

```text
No full W.A.S. explanation before Page 22.
No readable W.A.S. personnel names before Page 22.
No C.A.S.E. reveal in Issue #1.
No casual exterior wandering.
No crew approaching cryptids because they are curious.
```

# Full Issue Script Draft v0.1

## PAGE 1
### The Lead Tractor Sees the Road
**4 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Large horizontal opener. Interior of the lead tractor cab, looking out through a fogged windshield. Headlights tunnel into black-blue mineral mist. Wet frozen flats glitter under the light. A neat line of black route flags leads forward into the dark.

**CAPTION:**  
The approved route was clean.

**Panel 2**  
Closer inside cab. The ROOKIE LEAD DRIVER, suited but helmet off, watches the flags with tired focus. Dashboard glow paints his face cyan and amber. Wiper streaks cut the glass. Condensation gathers at the edges.

**CAPTION:**  
Clean flags. Clean weather window. Clean convoy status.

**Panel 3**  
Close on the route monitor. The approved GPS route line bends slightly left. The physical flags seen through the windshield continue straight.

No one is looking at the monitor.

**SFX:**  
tik  
tik  
tik

**Panel 4**  
Tight reflection in the windshield. For one instant the cab reflection looks older than the cab itself: a different dash shape, a different seat silhouette, a different route label underneath the modern display. It is too faint to read.

**CAPTION:**  
Clean lies best.

## PAGE 2
### Galley SITREP
**6 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Inside the galley module. Narrow, warm, functional. The TRAVERSE LEAD sits strapped at a fold-down station with a rugged tablet, half-eaten ration pouch clipped nearby. Cabinets vibrate softly.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
SITREP block four. Fuel reserve?

**Panel 2**  
Radio panel close-up. Multiple crew channels flicker.

**REAR DRIVER, RADIO:**  
Rear tractor holding at sixty-three percent.

**FUEL TECH, RADIO:**  
Bladder pack stable. No pressure drift.

**Panel 3**  
Traverse Lead enters data. The interface briefly offers an obsolete template underneath the active one, then clears.

Tiny flicker, barely visible: ROUTE STATUS: LEGACY...

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
Load strain?

**Panel 4**  
Interior of another module. Crew member braced in a narrow aisle as the train sways.

**LOAD TECH, RADIO:**  
Within green. Couplings singing ugly, but green.

**Panel 5**  
Back to lead cab. Rookie driver watches the route flags slide toward him in the headlights.

**TRAVERSE LEAD, RADIO:**  
Lead, confirm route visual.

**Panel 6**  
Rookie's thumb hovers over the radio switch. His eyes stay on the flags.

**ROOKIE:**  
Flags are clean. Holding course.

## PAGE 3
### Odd-Page Reveal: The Old Survey Drone
**5 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Rear tractor windshield POV. Fog and headlight glare. The train's own tracks vanish behind them.

**REAR DRIVER:**  
Hold on.

**Panel 2**  
Side camera feed on a small monitor. A yellow-white survey drone stands half-shrouded near the flag line they just passed. Tarp scraps. Broken antenna. One arm holding a bent flag staff.

**REAR DRIVER:**  
Was that a survey drone?

**Panel 3**  
Monitor distortion. The drone's head turns a fraction, or maybe the feed stutters.

**LOAD TECH, RADIO:**  
Out here?

**Panel 4**  
Rear tractor cab. Rear Driver leans closer to the screen, unsettled.

**REAR DRIVER:**  
Late model.

**Panel 5**  
Lead cab. Rookie finally glances at the GPS monitor.

The route line and the physical flag line do not match.

## PAGE 4
### The Rookie Notices
**5 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Close on Rookie's eyes, lit by the GPS. Fear enters quietly.

**Panel 2**  
GPS monitor. Approved route bends away behind them. The train has followed the old flag line far enough to matter.

**ROOKIE, small:**  
No.

**Panel 3**  
Rookie's gloved hand reaches for the radio switch.

**Panel 4**  
His finger stops short. In the radio speaker, the Traverse Lead is still calm, mid-SITREP.

**TRAVERSE LEAD, RADIO:**  
Weather block confirms black-blue mineral mist, low lateral wind, no surface break reports.

**Panel 5**  
Rookie looks through the windshield again. The black flags continue ahead, neat and confident. For one flicker, the GPS overlays an older route line that matches the flags.

**CAPTION:**  
Shame entered the cab.

Minor rough-ground adjustment. Holding safe speed.

Move!

### The Traverse Lead Takes Command
**5 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Galley module. Traverse Lead switches to all-channel. His face hardens. No panic, just procedure.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
All stations, shut up and listen.

**Panel 2**  
Lead cab. Rookie flinches at the voice.

**TRAVERSE LEAD, RADIO:**

Rear tractor. Rear Driver steadies the train.

**Panel 5**  
Traverse Lead, all business.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
Stop ops in one mile.

**SFX:**

Traverse Lead sees it. His gaze shifts from the flag to the GPS drift on his handheld.

He looks toward the lead cab where Rookie is still sitting in silence.

**TRAVERSE LEAD, RADIO:**  
Lead driver. Remain in cab.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
We'll talk after the train can't kill us.

## PAGE 16
### The Relay Tower Option
**5 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Galley module map table. Traverse Lead has patched the GPR feed into the convoy network. Routes glow in layered colors.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
Options.

**Panel 2**  
Map close-up. Reverse route is red. Crossing attempt is red. Continuing forward shows an old relay tower and fuel cache twenty-nine miles ahead.

**CAB TECH, RADIO:**  
Old record shows a bridged path near the tower.

**Panel 3**  
Data panel. The relay/fuel cache listing shows scrubbed institutional residue: blocked fields, project-family markers, old hardware classification. Nothing clean enough to explain.

**Panel 4**  
Traverse Lead looks at the options. He hates all of them.

**TRAVERSE LEAD:**  
We go forward.

**Panel 5**  
Lead cab. Rookie absorbs that his quiet mistake has become a twenty-nine-mile problem.

**TRAVERSE LEAD, RADIO:**  
Not because forward is safe. Because everything else is worse.

## PAGE 21
### Odd-Page Reveal: Rookmask Jack
**4 panels**

**Panel 1**  
Large reveal. Rookmask Jack stands at the relay mast. Hooded black robes. Birdlike plague-mask cowl. Crooked staff. Old Traverse backpack. He is not attacking.

He is watching.

**Panel 2**  
Crew reaction. Helmet lights tremble. Fuel line still attached. No one moves closer.

**FUEL TECH, small:**  
What is that?

**Panel 3**  
Jack lifts the crooked staff and taps the mast once.

**SFX:**  
TOK

## PAGE 22
### Final Splash: The Old File Opens
**Full-page splash**

Composite page. Rookmask Jack stands outside by the relay mast, reflected in cab glass and helmet visor glare. The Traverse train sits trapped in fog and work lights around the fuel cache. Screens across the page show fragments of the same old file finally resolving.

On the central awakened relay display:

```text
W.A.S. TRAVERSE
CORE TRANSIT INCIDENT
PERSONNEL STATUS: UNRESOLVED
```

Below it, a corrupted ID fragment clears just enough:

```text
MARK AGGRESSIVE
TRAVERSE LEADER / LEAD SCIENCE OFFICER
```

At the bottom, another line appears:

```text
PHASE TWO AUTHORIZED
```

**CAPTION:**  
The crew came for fuel.

**CAPTION:**  
They found proof the road had done this before.

**CAPTION:**  
And someone had already decided to do it again.
