Page 1 is the cold open.

It must communicate:

```text
The crew is professional.
The route appears clean.
```

# Panel 1 - Wide Cab / Windshield Opener

## Function

Establish the lead tractor cab and the apparently clean road.

## Composition

Interior of the lead tractor cab looking out through a fogged windshield.

Required elements:

```text
fogged windshield
wiper arcs / streaks
headlight cones entering black-blue mineral mist
wet frozen flats outside
neat black route flags vanishing forward
unused helmet rack behind the seat
route goggles hanging from a hook
collar mic cable
battered cup
thermal coat or route-worker gear in cab
 taped hazard map
cab dashboard edge in foreground
```

## Visual Read

The cab must feel like a working route machine, not a spaceship cockpit.

Use:

```text
practical industrial controls
worn switches
rubberized handles
cold-weather grime
scuffed panels
field repairs
dashboard glow
```

Avoid:

```text
sleek starship bridge
luxury cockpit
airplane cockpit polish
clean sci-fi glass cockpit
too many holograms
```

## Lighting

Main light sources:

```text
cold blue headlight spill through windshield
cyan / amber instrument glow
small dirty amber cab practicals
black silhouettes around the cab frame
```

Do not use broad white fill.

## Caption

```text
The approved route was clean.
```

Place the caption in dark cab negative space, not over the key flag line.

# Panel 2 - Human Rookie Lead Driver

## Function

Show the human driver trusting the physical flags.

## Composition

Close-medium on the **Human Rookie Lead Driver** in the cab.

Required cues:

```text
human face
helmet off
work goggles pushed up on forehead
thermal route coat or cold-weather field jacket
collar mic
cyan and amber dashboard glow on face
wiper streaks / condensation in surrounding glass
eyes focused past the route monitor toward the physical flags
```

## Character Read

The Rookie should look:

```text
tired
competent enough
under routine pressure
not stupid
not panicked yet
```

Avoid making him look like a careless fool. The danger is that the world gives him convincing bad ground truth.

## Caption

```text
The flags were cleaner.
```

---

# Panel 3 - Route Monitor Truth

## Function

Make the route disagreement readable.

## Composition

Close on the route monitor.

Required visual information:

```text
approved GPS route line bends slightly left
physical flag line reflected or visible through windshield continues straight
monitor is readable at thumbnail size
no one is looking at it
```

SFX:

```text
tik
tik
tik
```

## Screen Design

Use the issue bible's interface rules:

```text
practical utility display
limited colors
cold green / cyan / amber only as needed
blocky old route UI language
no floating hologram spectacle
no random fake text clutter
```

Text should be minimal and functional. Do not invent readable lore labels here.

## Critical Readability Rule

The reader must understand this immediately:

```text
screen route bends left
physical flags go straight
```

This is Page 1's story engine.

---

# Panel 4 - Reflection Strip

## Function

Introduce W.A.S. bleedthrough without explaining it.

## Composition

Full-width bottom strip, tight on windshield reflection.

Required elements:

```text
present cab reflection
older cab shape faintly overlaid
different dash silhouette
different seat silhouette
unreadable route label under modern display
wiper streak cutting through the ghost image
condensation at the edge
```

## Bleedthrough Level

This must be deniable.

The reflection should feel like:

```text
the cab briefly remembered being another cab
```

It should not read as:

```text
literal ghost standing in cab
clear flashback panel
readable W.A.S. evidence
time portal
crew apparition
```

## Caption

```text
That was the first problem.
```

Place it in black negative space, ideally bottom right.

# Pass / Fail Checklist

Approve a Page 1 art test if:

```text
the page reads clearly as a comic page
Panel 1 establishes the cab and trustworthy-looking flags
Panel 2 shows a human rookie in goggles/route gear, helmet off
Panel 3 clearly shows route monitor vs physical flag disagreement
Panel 4 gives a deniable older cab reflection
caption placement does not cover key visual information
style matches the issue bible's heavy-black / cold-blue / dirty-amber language
cab feels industrial and used, not sleek sci-fi
the reader senses trouble before the Rookie admits it
```

Reject or revise if:

```text
it looks like a spaceship cockpit
it looks like a train cab or locomotive
routine crew are wearing helmets
route disagreement is not instantly readable
reflection is too literal or too explanatory
W.A.S. text appears too early
Jack, Jasper, Flagstaff, relay tower, or C.A.S.E. appear
lighting becomes clean white daylight
image is beautiful but not sequentially readable
```
