# Panel Direction

## Panel 1 - Relay Site Establishing Band

**Function:** show the set, the crawler convoy, and the black-sky / pale-cloud language.

Required elements:

```text
frozen relay tower / fuel cache
heavy tracked Traverse crawler convoy
fuel tanks / utility boxes / cable runs
red relay beacons
dirty amber work lights
black frost and snow crust
black sky with pale blue cloud mass below it
vertical black sky-drips / ink-stalactite shapes
```

### Traverse Crawler / 409-Type Scout Rig Rule

If the lead/scout tractor is visually based on **409**, it should resemble a compact Antarctic-style tracked groomer / crawler carrier, not a truck and not a train.

Required visual cues:

```text
red compact tracked snow-groomer cab
wide snow tracks
front blade or front chassis assembly
long forward-mounted GPR boom extending ahead of the blade
round / sled-like GPR antenna pod at the boom's leading end
industrial field modifications
cold-weather route lights
no rails
no locomotive
no passenger cars
```

Functional logic:

```text
The GPR boom exists to scan hidden crevasses before the vehicle weight crosses the hazard.
The boom gives the crew reaction distance.
The radar operator reads real-time subsurface returns inside the cab.
```

For Issue #1, do not label the rig as 409 unless a later script revision explicitly says so. The visual lock is:

```text
Traverse crawler convoy, not train.
Tracked scout tractor with forward GPR boom, not locomotive.
```

## Panel 2 - Cab Reaction Band

**Function:** prove the mixed crew and separate the Saurasapien Traverse Lead from Rookmask Jack.

Required crew read:

```text
Human Rookie Lead Driver: goggles / route-worker gear, anxious but functional.
Robot Load Tech: utility robot silhouette, work optics, industrial cab glow.
Saurasapien Traverse Lead: saurian person, route authority, goggles or work lenses.
Antisapien Signal Tech: blue-skinned / dark-eyed / work glasses silhouette, signal focus.
```

### Critical Character Separation Rule

The Saurasapien Traverse Lead in the cab is **not** Rookmask Jack.

Do not give the Saurasapien Lead:

```text
red glowing eye
bird beak
black hood
plague mask
Jack silhouette
relay staff
machine-body posture
```

The Saurasapien Lead should read as a saurian person in route gear, not as a bird-masked omen.

### Cab Lighting

Use warm amber cab light and hard black silhouettes. The cab should feel cramped, practical, and procedural.

Do not let this panel become a fantasy party portrait. It is a reaction strip inside working machinery.

# Rookmask Jack Visual Lock

## Core Read

```text
Rookmask Jack is a black-alloy humanoid robot wearing a black bird-beak mask under black robes.
The beak is equipment, not anatomy.
The red eye is a lens, sensor, or machine glow, not an animal eye.
```

## Profile Rule

When Jack is shown in profile, he should have **one visible red eye only**.

Do not show a second eye through the far side of the mask.

Approved profile cues:

```text
one dim red eye
long black beak mask
hood swallowing the skull line
black alloy jaw/face hints inside the mask
robe mass hiding torso construction
black alloy robot hand
black mechanical feet
```

## Robot Hands and Feet

Hands and feet should be visibly mechanical if the panel gives room:

```text
black alloy fingers
jointed machine knuckles
hard mechanical wrist under sleeve
black segmented feet / bootlike machine feet
servo ankles
metal toe structures
```

These details confirm that Jack is not a bird-person or a simple ghost.

## Forbidden Jack Drift

Do not turn Jack into:

```text
an organic bird creature
a bird-person
a feathered humanoid
an animal monster
a fantasy plague doctor
a wizard
a superhero villain
a clean robot
a normal person in costume
```

Feather-like shapes are allowed only if they come from:

```text
robe rags
cable shreds
mask ridges
black silhouette tears
weathered fabric edges
```

They must not read as organic plumage.

# Sky and Atmosphere Lock

The approved sky language is critical.

The sky should have extreme graphic contrast:

```text
deep black upper sky
pale blue cloud bank beneath it
vertical black ink-drips descending into the blue
thin blue linework inside black masses
hard negative space
no soft sunset realism
no smooth painterly gradient as the main sky event
```

The black sky should feel like:

```text
ink hanging over the world
storm ceiling
visual pressure
route-memory darkness
```

The blue cloud mass should feel like:

```text
cold polar vapor
mineral fog
hard-edged comic cloud shape
not fluffy daylight cloud
```

This sky treatment is now part of the Page 22 visual lock.
