## Core Decision

The W.A.S. Traverse is the original disaster traverse.

The C.A.S.E. Traverse is the follow-up / cleanup / sealing / evidence-control traverse.

```text
W.A.S. opened the route.
C.A.S.E. closed the file.
Neither stopped what came back.
```

---

## W.A.S. Traverse

**Full Name:** `World Aperture Survey Traverse`  
**Short Name:** `W.A.S. Traverse`  
**Story Function:** Original Core disaster / cryptid-origin traverse  
**Era Placement:** Tenet Nocsid Jr. era candidate, pending later canon review  

The W.A.S. Traverse is the mission that enters the Core too early and creates the Core-returned cryptid pattern.

Current transformation pattern:

```text
Mark Aggressive -> Rookmask Jack
Richard Jameson -> X / Isopod_X
"Buck" -> Ol' Jasper
Mara "Sparky" Cairn -> The Cairn Hound / Sparky
Flagstaff / The Flagger -> The Last Surveyor
```

W.A.S. should remain the tragedy-source. It is the traverse whose people become folklore because the institution refuses to admit what happened to them.

### W.A.S. Story Role

Use W.A.S. when the story needs:

```text
first Core disaster
cryptid-origin event
Mark Aggressive command failure
Richard Jameson becoming X
Buck becoming Ol' Jasper
Mara Cairn becoming Sparky / Cairn Hound
Flagstaff becoming the Last Surveyor
bad route folklore source
buried WISSARD personnel file
original sin of the arc
```

---

## C.A.S.E. Traverse

**Full Name:** `Core Aperture Stabilization Expedition`  
**Short Name:** `C.A.S.E. Traverse`  
**Story Function:** Follow-up / cleanup / recovery / sealing / containment / evidence-control traverse  
**Era Placement:** After W.A.S., still within the broader Tenet Nocsid Jr. era candidate space unless later canon review revises it  

C.A.S.E. is not the mission that creates the cryptids.

C.A.S.E. is the institution returning to the wound with stabilization language, containment logic, file discipline, and the false promise that the disaster can be packaged into procedure.

### C.A.S.E. Story Role

Use C.A.S.E. when the story needs:

```text
institutional follow-up after W.A.S.
cleanup language
containment teams
recovery order
route sealing attempt
evidence-control traverse
black-file bureaucracy
institutional cover-up layer
second secret beneath the first secret
```

C.A.S.E. should feel less like discovery and more like paperwork arriving with teeth.

---

## Relationship Between the Two Traverses

```text
W.A.S. = the wound
C.A.S.E. = the bandage, the stitch, the label, and the lie
```

W.A.S. is tragic expedition horror.

C.A.S.E. is institutional horror.

W.A.S. creates the cryptids.

C.A.S.E. tries to classify the cryptids, seal the route, recover useful evidence, and prevent the public record from naming the people who came back wrong.

---

## Issue #1 Usage

Issue #1 may still open with a present-day Traverse crew encountering the wrong-route incident.

The present-day crew should discover the W.A.S. layer first:

```text
W.A.S. TRAVERSE
CORE TRANSIT INCIDENT
PERSONNEL STATUS: UNRESOLVED
```

C.A.S.E. should not replace W.A.S. as the Issue #1 mystery reveal.

Instead, C.A.S.E. should become a later-file reveal, showing that WISSARD or its institutional predecessor knew enough to return, contain, redact, and misname the disaster.

Recommended escalation:

```text
Issue #1 reveal:
The monsters have W.A.S. personnel names.

Later reveal:
There was a C.A.S.E. Traverse after W.A.S.
The institution came back.
They did not rescue the crew.
They stabilized the story.
```

## Tenet Nocsid Jr. Era Note

Current development preference places W.A.S. in the Tenet Nocsid Jr. era rather than Tenet III's present-day era.

This keeps W.A.S. as inherited damage: older than Tenet III's current Traverse work, but recent enough to remain buried inside institutional memory, route folklore, and black files.

Open canon-review questions:

```text
Was Tenet Nocsid Jr. directly involved in C.A.S.E.?
Did Jr. lead C.A.S.E., witness its aftermath, or only inherit its silence?
Did C.A.S.E. become one of the reasons later Traverse naming culture hardened into acronyms?
Which institution officially authorized C.A.S.E. at the time?
```

Do not answer these questions as canon until explicitly promoted.
