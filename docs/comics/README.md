## Current Headliners

### W.A.S. Traverse: The Road That Came Back Wrong

A 5-issue comic pitch about the first WISSARD team sent into Meridian Prime's Core before the Core was ready for travel.

The team returns changed into the cryptid remnants later known as Rookmask Jack, X, Ol' Jasper, Flagstaff, and Sparky / The Cairn Hound.

The pitch is structured for monthly release with a trade-paperback shape: each 22-page issue gets its own purchase-worthy hook, escalation, midpoint, lowest point, or climax, while the full arc reads as a single tragedy about duty, abandonment, memory, and warnings left on a road that should not reopen.

### Issue 01 Beat Sheet: The Route That Came Back Wrong

A 22-page issue beat sheet for the opening installment of the W.A.S. Traverse comic arc.

The issue follows a present-day Traverse crew through a wrong-route survival incident involving Ol' Jasper, Flagstaff, a dead WISSARD relay, and Rookmask Jack before revealing that the cryptids may be Core-returned WISSARD personnel.

The document protects page budget, page-turn reveals, splash usage, A/B/C plot weaving, and the Page 22 cliffhanger: `PHASE TWO AUTHORIZED`.
