## Continuity and handoff

- Page 8 ends with the Rookie's hard correction, convoy hitch strain, a returning legacy-load warning, and Jasper's red light in front of the windshield. Page 9 reveals the hidden ground hazard in the direction of that correction.
- Keep Jasper human-sized and shaggy, with lynx-like tufted ears, goggles, salvage straps, a scrap tool and red headlamp. The approved Page 7 fog treatment is a continuity cue, though this page makes his warning gesture more readable. Do not identify his earlier human self or turn him into an attacker.
- The cat-shaped charm is a tiny intimate clue on Jasper's harness. Do not explain its origin or treat it as a large emblem.
- The opening frost crust conceals a deep fracture; the bottom-left close-up dramatizes depth without establishing a huge open chasm across the route.
- Preserve the practical tracked convoy, black flags, cold blue-black terrain, amber machine lighting and selective red signal. No rails or spaceship designs.
- No Saurasapien appears here. On later pages, the Traverse Lead remains a Saurasapien with flush/internal ears and no external pinnae.
- Page 10 returns to the Galley module: the Saurasapien Traverse Lead takes the all-channel command, orders no reversing or improvising and nobody to exit, then holds the convoy in formation until a stable stop point.

This is a 1024 × 1536 visual working master; high-resolution trim/bleed and final lettering review are still needed for print.
