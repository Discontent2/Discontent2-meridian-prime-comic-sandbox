## Continuity and reveal limits

- This is a **tracked crawler convoy**. The top panel looks backward along its own tracks; the flags remain the misleading physical route. The approved GPS route bends left. Do not reverse this geography on later pages.
- The survey drone is an old route-service machine and the first suggestive Flagstaff clue. Its yellow-white shell, torn covering, bent staff and damaged antenna can recur. The head movement remains ambiguous because of camera interference. Do not identify a W.A.S. person or explain the machine's origin here.
- The rear tractor driver has a weathered face, dark hair and work glasses; the Rookie is a separate younger human in the lead cab, with goggles raised and a red coat. Keep their cab positions and roles distinct.
- Preserve Pages 1–2's heavy black spot shadows, cold slate/cobalt snow, dirty amber instrument lights, limited cyan screens and small red clothing accents. The machine and terrain are industrial and lived in, with no spaceship or rail-train design.
- The Page 2 anatomy correction remains binding for future appearances: Saurasapiens have flush/internal ears and no projecting human-style ear flaps (pinnae). No Saurasapien appears on this page.
- The script's present-day incident stays legible. C.A.S.E., readable W.A.S. personnel names, a full flashback, and Rookmask Jack's explanation remain unrevealed.
