## Saurasapien anatomy correction — carry forward

Saurasapiens have internal or flush ears, **without external ear flaps (pinnae)**. The Traverse Lead's profile must stay smoothly reptilian. If an ear is visible, show only a small flush opening or scale-covered slit integrated into the head behind the eye and jaw. Do not depict a rounded human ear or projecting cartilaginous flap on this character or other Saurasapiens. This correction applies to all subsequent panels and character references, not just this page. The issue-wide art bible repeats this lock.

## Continuity and palette

- The Lead is a competent Saurasapien route authority, not a monster or Rookmask Jack. Routine travel gear, no helmet.
- The Robot Load Tech is a functional industrial worker; the Antisapien Signal Tech has blue skin and amber work glasses; the Rookie matches Page 1's dark hair, goggles pushed up, weathered face, red thermal coat and collar mic.
- Use large flat black shadow masses, slate/cobalt blue shadows, sparse dirty amber practical lights, and cyan instruments. Keep red as a small garment or warning accent. No broad painterly glow.
- Each character is introduced through a job and radio voice rather than an exposition caption. The convoy remains in motion; crew work from their modules and the cab.
- The present approved route already bends left while the misleading physical flags continue straight. Page 2's last panel preserves the Rookie's belief that he is holding a clean route.
- The `ROUTE STATUS: LEGACY...` underlay is a transient equipment anomaly. It does not identify W.A.S. personnel or explain the old disaster.
