## Goal

Turn the creature silhouette plate from a field-guide concept into an actual page composition.

This page should read like:

```text
old scientific plate
damaged evidence file
ecological warning document
comic-book promise page
```

Not a clean bestiary. Not a trading-card page.

A beautiful little bureaucratic curse.

---

## Recommended Thumbnail Direction

Use this layout:

```text
Damaged 3 x 3 Field Plate
```

This is the strongest option because it keeps the nine-organism motif readable while still letting the page feel corrupted, censored, and alive.

```text
┌─────────────────────────────────────┐
│ APOCRYPHAL PLATE I                  │
```

## Visual Hierarchy

The artist's eye path should be:

```text
1. Girasol absence / antler glint
2. Buckle's pale solid body
3. Index Orchid pollen clue
4. Gift-Pod at center
5. Bottom stamp: LOCATION WITHHELD
```

That gives the page a trapdoor: wonder first, comedy second, danger third, ethics last.

---

## Cell Size Weighting

Do not make every box emotionally equal.

The page should preserve the readable 3 x 3 structure, but the line weight, black mass, redactions, and damage should subtly privilege the Girasol, Buckle, Index Orchid, and Gift-Pod.

---

## Top Row

### Top Left: Index Orchid

Small, precise, botanical.

```text
Thin silhouette
Redacted root system
Tiny Girasol Gold pollen flecks
```

Composition note:

The orchid should have more white space around it than expected, making it feel like a key in a specimen box.

---

### Top Center: Buckle

Slightly larger and more grounded than the other silhouettes.

```text
Pale body against black ink
Sale-tag scrap overlapping the cell
Tiny chew mark in corner
```

Composition note:

Buckle should be the most emotionally readable form on the plate. Not cartoonish, but solid, stubborn, and weirdly innocent.

Visible text scrap:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

---

### Top Right: Girasol

Most mysterious cell.

```text
Do not show full body
Show partial antlers and neck
Let black foliage swallow the form
Use the best Girasol Gold flecks here
```

Composition note:

The Girasol cell should feel like the page failed to capture the animal. Use negative space, torn edge, and black foliage to hide more than is shown.

Label fragment:

```text
FULL FORM UNCONFIRMED
```

---

## Middle Row

### Middle Left: Crimson Glass Salamander

Elegant false lead.

```text
Low body
Frilled gills
Stamp: FALSE MATCH?
```

Composition note:

The silhouette should look seductive enough to pull the eye, but it should be stamped down and visually demoted.

---

### Center: Gift-Pod

The page's quiet bomb.

```text
Slightly larger than its cell
Root threads creep into neighboring borders
Looks like a present, seed, egg, and threat
```

Composition note:

The Gift-Pod should occupy the exact center of the grid and subtly violate the grid. Its root filaments should creep across the ruled borders.

Visible text:

```text
DO NOT ACCEPT AS GIFT
```

---

### Middle Right: Nightwake

Most ambiguous silhouette.

```text
Ghosted wing positions
Moth-bird shape
Bad-registration effect
```

Composition note:

The Nightwake should look like it was sketched from several impossible sightings laid over one another.

---

## Bottom Row

### Bottom Left: Lung Lichen

Survival warning organism.

```text
Branching lung/coral pattern
Tiny air-gauge doodle
Optional Lichen Green only in variant
```

Composition note:

Keep it legible in black and white. It can glow only in a Lichen Green variant, not in the main Girasol Gold plate.

Visible text:

```text
GLOWS WHEN AIR FAILS
```

---

### Bottom Center: Dwarf Craton Sauropod

Sacred, old, restricted.

```text
Partly hidden in reeds
Stamp: CULTURAL RESTRICTION
No flashy pose
```

Composition note:

This should feel solemn, old, and protected. Do not make it a dinosaur spectacle.

---

### Bottom Right: Snow-Dire Leafcat

Predator shape, herbivore truth.

```text
Dangerous silhouette
Mouth full of leaves
Crossed-out MANEATER note
```

Composition note:

It should read dangerous on first glance and oddly gentle on second glance.

Visible correction:

```text
MISFEAR COMMON
```

---

## Page Texture

Add these over the whole page:

```text
hand-ruled grid lines
water stains
old tape ghosts
redacted coordinate blocks
stamped plate numbers
smudged fingerprints
field-note arrows
tiny Buckle snout smear
```

The page should look like it has been copied, hidden, recovered, annotated, censored, and then folded into Thor's satchel while still damp.

---

## Spot Color Pass

Main version:

```text
Black ink: 95%
White paper: 4%
Girasol Gold: 1%
```

Gold goes only on:

```text
Index Orchid pollen
Girasol antler glints
one broken manuscript line crossing the plate
tiny edge of final stamp
```

Do not use green on the main plate unless making the Lung Lichen variant.

---

## Artist Notes

The page should remain readable in black and white if the spot color is removed.

Buckle should be the only figure with immediate emotional warmth.

The Girasol should be the only figure that feels too large or too rare for the page to contain.

The Gift-Pod should be the only figure that visibly crosses its cell border.

The bottom stamp should look like a moral correction, not a decorative slogan.

---

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
clean encyclopedia layout
monster trading-card layout
full-color bestiary
fully revealed Girasol
cute-only Buckle
fantasy flower glow
generic dinosaur/lizard silhouettes
too many readable labels
perfectly even emotional weighting
```
