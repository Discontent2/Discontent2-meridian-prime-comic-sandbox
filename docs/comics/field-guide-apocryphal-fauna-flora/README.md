## Core Identity

`Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora` is a non-Traverse-centered Meridian Prime comic concept following **Thor Fortner**, a young field-naturalist from **Ledger Falls**, as he attempts to create a modern illustrated field guide to rumored, rare, misclassified, sacred, and officially disputed Meridian Prime fauna and flora.

The project is designed as a **modern ashcan explorer comic** with heavy black inks, mostly black-and-white interiors, and selective spot color.

Core principle:

```text
The story is not about Thor conquering Meridian Prime.
It is about Meridian Prime teaching Thor how not to become another kind of predator.
```

## Traverse Limitation

The story may include Thor's solo field vehicle, supply travel, camps, meal scenes, repairs, and route transitions, but the Traverse-like material should remain background infrastructure and must not become the story spine.

Target limit:

```text
Traverse / vehicle travel storyline: 12 percent maximum
```

The vehicle is Thor's moving home, not a route-operation plot machine.
