# Buckle Pose / Expression Sheet
## Buckle "Buckley"
### Pygmy Pale Burdenback

**Project:** `Observed, Not Owned` / `Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora`  
**Status:** Sandbox comic development / production design sheet  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Function:** Emotional anchor, White Elephant, environmental warning animal, domestic chaos engine

---

# 1. Core Visual Reminder

Buckle is:

- a small pale undergrown Pale Burdenback
- a pygmy hippo / rhino calf / tapir blend
- compact, heavy, low to the ground
- built for wetland terrain, mud, and stubbornness
- a failed pack animal who became a pet and companion
- visually strongest as a white shape moving through black ink

He should feel:

```text
solid
soft-looking but durable
slightly too heavy for his size
earnest
food-motivated
stubborn
emotionally readable
never fully domesticated
```

---

# 2. Silhouette Rules

Every Buckle pose should preserve at least three of these reads:

- barrel body
- blunt flexible snout
- tiny rounded ears
- thick short legs
- underdeveloped burden ridge
- low center of gravity
- tiny tail
- pale body mass against dark environments

Core shape phrase:

```text
A little wetland tank with feelings.
```

---

# 3. Expression Philosophy

Buckle is not a talking cartoon mascot.

His expressions come from:

- ear angle
- eye openness
- nostril tension
- snout curl
- head tilt
- body lean
- foot placement
- ridge tension
- whole-body freeze or slump

The more Buckle feels, the more his entire body should participate.

---

# 4. Must-Have Pose Set

## Pose 1: Neutral Standing / Base Model

### Purpose
Default reference pose.

### Description
- side view
- four feet planted
- head slightly forward
- snout relaxed
- ears neutral
- burden ridge visible
- tail resting
- body mass evenly distributed

### Read
```text
Healthy baseline
calm
curious
ready to move if food appears
```

### Use
Main scale reference for all future Buckle drawings.

---

## Pose 2: Curious Sniff

### Purpose
Buckle investigating something suspicious or edible.

### Description
- neck extended forward
- nose lifted or pressed close
- snout flexed
- one forefoot slightly raised
- ears tipped forward
- eyes focused
- body still heavy and grounded

### Read
```text
What is this?
Can I eat it?
Should Thor have stopped me already?
```

### Best uses
- manuscript wrapper
- field tags
- Gift-Pod jar
- strange plants
- Lung Lichen tin
- Thor's pocket

---

## Pose 3: Refusal / Braced Stubbornness

### Purpose
Buckle refuses to move, enter a place, or cooperate.

### Description
- feet planted wide
- neck pulled back
- chin tucked
- snout angled away
- eyes narrowed
- ears flattened slightly outward
- whole body weight shifted backward
- little tail stiff

### Read
```text
No.
Absolutely not.
This is your mistake, not mine.
```

### Best uses
- bad air
- unsafe cave entrance
- steep ramp
- cold puddle
- obviously bad idea Thor insists on

This is one of the most important Buckle poses.

---

## Pose 4: Alarm / Environmental Warning

### Purpose
Buckle senses something before Thor does.

### Description
- head raised sharply
- nostrils flared
- ears high and tight
- eyes wide
- body frozen
- one foot slightly lifted or set hard
- ridge tense
- mouth closed
- snout angled into air current

### Read
```text
Something is wrong.
Stop moving.
Pay attention.
```

### Optional sound
```text
whff
kweeh
snrk
```

### Best uses
- bad air
- oxygen drop
- pressure shift
- predator presence
- silence in wrong place
- Gift-Pod reaction

This is Buckle's signal animal posture.

---

## Pose 5: Startled Hop / Panic Pivot

### Purpose
Quick comic or danger reaction.

### Description
- body twisting unexpectedly
- front feet lifting slightly
- rear legs driving
- head jerking sideways
- ears thrown back
- mouth open or snort shape
- tail flicked up

### Read
```text
Absolutely not.
Retreat now.
```

### Use
- sudden noise
- steam vent
- jar cracking
- Thor dropping something
- surprise spore puff

Should be funny and believable, not cartoon physics.

---

## Pose 6: Food Crime / Chewing Contraband

### Purpose
Domestic chaos and comedy.

### Description
- seated or half-curled
- object in mouth
- eyes innocent
- one ear crooked
- jaw working calmly
- body fully relaxed
- front foot pinning item if needed

### Read
```text
This was unguarded.
Therefore it was food.
```

### Best props
- field tags
- paper corner
- moss packet
- feed sack tie
- glove
- map edge
- blanket fringe

### Key acting note
Buckle should look sincerely unconvinced he has done anything wrong.

---

## Pose 7: Sleep Curl / Stove Nest

### Purpose
Emotional warmth pose.

### Description
- body curled into oval
- snout tucked or resting on floor
- little legs folded beneath
- ears soft
- eyes closed
- ridge relaxed
- tiny tail loose
- often half inside cloth pile

### Read
```text
Safe.
Warm.
Home is currently acceptable.
```

### Best uses
- Mallow interior night scenes
- after stress scene
- emotional reset beat
- page-end warmth contrast

This pose is extremely important. It makes the hab feel alive.

---

## Pose 8: Leaning for Comfort

### Purpose
Buckle seeks reassurance from Thor.

### Description
- body pressed into Thor's shin or knee
- head tucked against leg
- eyes half-closed or worried
- snout relaxed
- one foot angled inward
- posture soft and trusting

### Read
```text
I am scared.
You are mine.
Stand still.
```

### Use
- thunder
- cave pressure change
- after danger
- moment of quiet bonding

This pose sells the relationship without needing words.

---

## Pose 9: Door Block / Do Not Leave Me Outside

### Purpose
Buckle claims the crawler and resists exclusion.

### Description
- body planted in doorway
- feet splayed
- head pushed under door line or frame edge
- eyes stubborn
- snout lifted in protest
- impossible to move without emotional guilt

### Read
```text
I live here now.
Your objection is administrative.
```

### Best uses
- boarding Mallow
- rainy weather
- when Thor says "wait here"
- when Buckle has already decided otherwise

---

## Pose 10: Pack Animal Failure Pose

### Purpose
Visually prove Buckle is not viable expedition stock.

### Description
- tiny pack frame or draped test bundle slipping awkwardly
- Buckle standing with confused annoyance
- weight obviously wrong for his build
- body too round and too low
- burden ridge underdeveloped
- Thor in background realizing the truth

### Read
```text
This was never going to work.
```

### Story use
Critical visual storytelling for the White Elephant motif.

# 5. Must-Have Expression Set

These should be drawn as head-and-shoulder or bust-style reference miniatures.

## Expression A: Neutral Curiosity
- ears relaxed
- eyes open
- snout soft
- nostrils normal

## Expression B: Food Interest
- eyes slightly larger
- snout extended
- lips or snout tip active
- ears tipped forward

## Expression C: Mild Judgment
- eyes half-lidded
- snout angled slightly down
- ears slightly asymmetric

This is his "Thor is being foolish again" look.

## Expression D: Concern / Worry
- eyes widened
- ears angled outward
- nostrils tense
- neck slightly pulled back

## Expression E: Stubborn Refusal
- chin tucked
- eyes narrowed
- ears slightly flattened
- lips or snout set

## Expression F: Alarm
- eyes wide
- ears high
- nostrils flared
- head rigid

## Expression G: Sleepy Safety
- half-lidded eyes
- loose ears
- soft snout
- head droop

## Expression H: Innocent Guilt
- chewing pause
- wide eyes
- object still in mouth
- zero repentance

# 6. Mouth / Snout Action Notes

Because Buckle has tapir-like facial logic, the snout should be expressive.

## Snout actions to include
- sniffing upward
- nudging sideways
- pressing into Thor's coat
- curling around cloth edge
- lifting label corner
- testing air
- touching Lung Lichen tin
- hovering over dangerous object without biting
- full caught-chewing freeze

The snout is one of Buckle's best acting tools.

---

# 7. Ear / Tail Mood Guide

## Ears

### Forward
Interest / food / investigation

### Out to sides
Uncertainty / discomfort

### Back
Refusal / distrust / irritation

### Soft / low
Rest / trust / sleep

## Tail

### Still
Calm or focused

### Tiny flick
Mild agitation / bug annoyance / irritation

### Stiff
Refusal / tension

### Relaxed curl
Rest / comfort

Because Buckle is a compact animal, even small tail movement matters.

# 10. Scale Notes

Buckle should be:

- clearly smaller than true burden stock
- big enough to take up space
- small enough to be physically plausible inside Mallow
- large enough that Thor cannot simply treat him like luggage

Visual comparison:

- top of back around mid-thigh or slightly above Thor's knee
- broad body
- deceptively heavy look

He should read as:

```text
too small to work
too big to ignore
```

---

# 11. Acting Rules

## What Buckle should always feel like
- real animal first
- emotionally readable second
- cute third

## What Buckle should never feel like
- plush toy
- dog stand-in
- fantasy mascot
- joke prop only
- human in animal costume

## Performance rule
Buckle's funniest moments come from sincerity, not goofiness.

He truly means every refusal, every theft, every worried sniff.

# 12. Spot Color Notes for Pose Sheet

Keep most of the sheet black and white.

Optional tiny spot color callouts:

## Girasol Gold
- tiny label markers
- one eye glint
- field-sheet headers only

## Lichen Green
- one warning pose with green reflected on snout

## Rust Red
- `NO RETURNS` tag
- crossed-out `pack utility` note

Avoid over-coloring Buckle himself.

His pale form is the color event.
