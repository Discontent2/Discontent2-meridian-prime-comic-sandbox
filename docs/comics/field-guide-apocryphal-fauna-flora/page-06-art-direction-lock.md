# Page 06 Art Direction Lock
## The Road Takes Notes

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 06  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Mallow leaves Ledger Falls and stops inches before crushing a tiny black-glass orchid in a muddy roadside rut, while the road behind Thor begins to resemble a living map taking notes back.
```

Page 6 should feel like the first time the landscape quietly corrects Thor.

Not a monster reveal. Not a chase. Not a vehicle showcase.

A road omen.

---

## Page Mood

```text
wet road dread
quiet field discovery
first ethical bruise
machine versus fragile life
map versus living world
```

The page should feel restrained. The danger is not loud yet. It is small, dark, and easy to step on.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Mallow leaves Ledger Falls and enters the black road.
2. The manuscript clue becomes a real roadside organism.
3. Thor records the location, and the road seems to record him back.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Departure Road

Wide horizontal establishing panel.

Mallow crawls away from Ledger Falls along an old timber-margin road. The last lights of town should be tiny behind it. The forest ahead should form a heavy black mouth.

Required elements:

```text
Mallow in three-quarter rear or side view
old timber road
rain
Ledger Falls lights behind
black forest wall ahead
front blade riding low but not actively pushing
```

Ink priority:

- black forest mass
- black crawler undercarriage
- pale rain streaks
- white road slash leading forward

---

### Panel 2: Cab Interior

Thor in the cab, seen through the dark geometry of the windshield and dashboard.

Required elements:

```text
Thor's cap and goggles silhouette
wrapped manuscript
folded map
Kuker-Rankin hand level
handwritten objective note
cracked pressure gauge
```

The note must read:

```text
FIRST OBJECTIVE:
BLACK ROOT / GLASS VEIN / LOW WATER
```

The cab should feel cramped and damp, not cinematic or sleek.

---

### Panel 3: Contradictory Road Signs

A cluster of signs at a fork, half-official and half-local.

Required sign text:

```text
ROAD CLOSED
FLOOD CUT
PERMIT ROAD
NO SURVEY BEYOND MARKER 17
LOW WATER TAKES WHAT IT IS OWED
```

The final sign should look unofficial: brush-painted, nailed crooked, rain-blackened.

Visual note:

A black mineral vein should run through the roadside ditch. Rainwater should behave strangely around it, gathering beside it instead of crossing cleanly.

---

### Panel 4: Orchid Near Track

Close low panel.

Mallow's track stops just before a tiny black-glass orchid in a rut.

The orchid must be:

```text
tiny
fragile
nearly black
reflective like wet obsidian
not decorative
part warning system, part living organism
```

Use a tiny amount of **Girasol Gold** for pollen flecks in rainwater.

The track should feel enormous by comparison, like an industrial mistake paused at the last second.

---

### Panel 5: Thor Documents

Thor kneels in mud and rain beside the orchid, sketching on waterproof paper.

Required elements:

```text
Thor kneeling
hand level used as sight glass
waterproof field paper
Mallow looming behind
small cab light / hab light rectangle
orchid untouched
```

Visible field note fragments:

```text
Index Orchid? roadside specimen
black glass petal / low-water margin
approx. 1.7 mi past Marker 17
DO NOT COLLECT
```

Thor should appear careful in action but still careless in implication.

He is not picking the plant. He is recording too much.

---

### Panel 6: Road Takes Notes Back

High overhead panel.

Mallow, Thor, and the tiny orchid become small inside a branching road pattern. The road behind them should look like a crude map, root system, mineral vein system, or nervous system.

Do not make the shape too literal. It should feel like the reader noticed something Thor has not.

Required visual idea:

```text
The road is becoming a page.
```

Use Girasol Gold only for:

- tiny orchid pollen
- one rain bead on Thor's field note

No full Girasol imagery.

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary page spot color:

```text
D Girasol Gold
```

Use very sparingly:

- pollen specks on black-glass orchid
- tiny manuscript/objective-note echo if needed
- one rain bead on Thor's field note

Optional secondary spot color:

```text
B Rust Red
```

Use only if the warning signs need a weathered accent. Do not let Rust Red compete with Girasol Gold.

No Acid Magenta on this page unless a later revision adds signal contamination.

No Lichen Green on this page.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
forest wall
crawler undercarriage
wet mineral vein
roadside ditch
cab interior
high-panel branching road pattern
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
rain streaks
road slash
field paper
tiny flower highlights
Ledger Falls lights behind
```

The page should feel printable in black and white even if the spot color is removed.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor is alert, self-serious, and too pleased to have found evidence.

Visual language:

```text
careful hands
fast notes
muddy knees
head tilted like a surveyor
not enough hesitation
```

He should look morally decent but methodologically dangerous.

### Mallow

Mallow should feel like a big, battered animal that almost stepped on something sacred.

Visual language:

```text
huge track beside tiny flower
front blade as blunt lower jaw
wet panels
black undercarriage
small cab light
```

### Buckle

Buckle is not present on Page 6.

Do not include a pale animal shape, silhouette, or tease that would imply Buckle has already joined Thor.

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
Buckle
Buzz-Mule / e-bike
full Girasol
combat
chase action
Traverse convoy language
clean sci-fi road design
decorative fantasy flower glow
explained manuscript mechanics
```
