## Page Art Job

Page 03 must make **Mallow / The Tin Burrow** feel like a character.

The reader should understand in one page that the crawler is:

- Thor's vehicle
- Thor's shelter
- Thor's mobile lab
- Thor's fragile proof that he can leave Ledger Falls
- a repurposed extraction machine now trying to become an observation machine

The page should feel wet, black, cramped, and intimate.

Core visual sentence:

```text
A little tracked field den waits in the Ledger Falls rain while Thor carries an impossible book into it.
```

## Panel Count and Composition

Recommended layout: **5 panels**

Page rhythm:

1. Wide exterior reveal
2. Thor crossing yard with manuscript
3. Close-up vehicle identity detail
4. Interior hab reveal
5. Manuscript / gold glimmer hook

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: wide horizontal top panel, rain-soaked yard and full crawler silhouette
Panel 2: vertical or medium panel, Thor crossing toward crawler
Panel 3: narrow detail panel, MALLOW name / tracks / permit sticker
Panel 4: medium interior panel, hab reveal
Panel 5: wider bottom hook panel, manuscript opened on desk with Girasol Gold glimmer
```

The page should descend from outside rain into interior secrecy.

---

## Ink and Value Direction

Use heavy black inks.

Black should dominate:

- forest mass
- crawler undercarriage
- track forms
- cab windows
- rain-shadowed yard
- hab interior corners
- manuscript page gaps

White / pale shapes should carry:

- rain streaks
- wet metal highlights
- Thor's face / hands
- oilcloth edges
- paper surfaces
- small hab light shapes

Avoid gray mush. The page must read through strong black and white design.

---

## Spot Color Direction

Primary interior spot color: **Girasol Gold**.

Use gold only in Panel 5:

- a thin thread between manuscript pages
- tiny reflection on the Kuker-Rankin hand level glass
- possibly one speck on Thor's fingertip

No broad gold glow. No magical blast.

The gold should feel like a secret breathing through paper.

Optional rust-red accents are allowed only as nearly invisible print notes, not full rendered color:

- old warning paint on blade
- permit sticker edge
- rust streak on track housing

But the page should still feel mostly black and white.

---

## Mallow Exterior Direction

Mallow must look compact, battered, and beloved.

Silhouette lock:

- Tucker Sno-Cat-like tracked field vehicle
- compact tracked chassis
- scarred front blade
- rounded cab
- rear hab module
- small stove pipe
- roof rack clutter
- side storage boxes
- handmade conversion feeling

Mood:

```text
A beetle wearing a cabin.
```

The vehicle should not look military, sleek, or rich.

It should look rebuilt from Ledger Falls timber-margin surplus by someone with more patience than money.

---

## Mallow Interior Direction

The interior is a cramped mobile research den.

Required interior objects:

- fold-down desk
- narrow bunk edge
- tiny stove
- specimen drawers
- field plates / paper slips
- pencil tins
- hanging mug
- empty floor corner for future Buckle nest
- manuscript space on the desk

Interior mood:

```text
organized enough to be a lab, cluttered enough to be honest.
```

It should feel warm compared to the outside, but not cozy in a polished way. More survival nook than camper showroom.

---

## Thor Direction

Thor should look small against the yard and vehicle but competent enough to belong there.

Visual lock:

- dark flat cap / driving cap
- early motoring goggles on top
- weathered field jacket
- scarf
- boots
- cross-body satchel
- oilcloth-wrapped manuscript under arm

Body language:

- guarded
- focused
- a little guilty
- moving quickly through rain
- not yet triumphant

He should not look like an action hero. He should look like a young naturalist smuggling destiny into a bad camper.

---

## Manuscript Direction

The manuscript should not be fully readable.

Visual ingredients:

- oilcloth wrap
- stained page edges
- strange biological diagrams
- pressure-script fragments
- old page texture
- final gold thread between pages

The final panel should imply that the book has more layers than Thor understands.

Do not reveal the Index Orchid yet unless it is only an unreadable pressed shape or partial botanical mark.

---

## Environmental Direction

Ledger Falls supply / timber-margin yard should show:

- stacked fuel tins
- wet timber skids
- moss on machinery
- rain sheeting off tarp lines
- cloud forest looming close
- muddy ground
- industrial residue becoming forest again

This keeps the page rooted in Ledger Falls without becoming a lore dump.

---

## Lettering / Caption Design

Caption boxes should look like field labels or later guide notes.

Do not overfill the page.

Panel 5 caption is the page-turn hook and should have room to breathe:

```text
The book did not tell me where to go.
It showed me what had survived being disbelieved.
```

Rain SFX should be integrated into the page texture rather than floating cartoon lettering.

---

## Page Turn Purpose

The reader should finish Page 03 thinking:

```text
The crawler is Thor's home.
The book is dangerous.
The journey is about to begin.
```

Page 04 should reveal or deepen the manuscript / Index Orchid clue.
