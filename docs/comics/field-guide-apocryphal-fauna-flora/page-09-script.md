# Page 09 Script
## Buckley Refuses the Air

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 09  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Production Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Continuity Position:** After Page 08, `Growth Expected, No Returns`  

---

## Page Function

Page 9 is where Buckle creates Thor's first practical travel problem, then immediately proves that the problem is information.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor tries to treat Buckle like future pack stock.
Buckle behaves like a stubborn, undergrown animal.
Thor almost misses an environmental warning.
Buckle notices it first.
```

The page should make Buckle funny without making him disposable comic relief. He is inconvenient because he is alive, not because he is stupid.

This page introduces the idea that Buckle's body reads the world before Thor's instruments do.

## Panel Count

Recommended: **6 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: Mallow parked at a low-water culvert / Buckle blocks the ramp
Panel 2: Thor tries to assign Buckle a pack role
Panel 3: Buckle eats the tag and refuses forward movement
Panel 4: Thor spots the route / misses the warning
Panel 5: Buckle freezes and chirps at glowing Lung Lichen
Panel 6: Thor realizes the air meter is wrong too late
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Wide panel.**

Mallow is parked beside a low-water culvert where the timber road dips under black roots and wet stone. The rear hab door is open. A small folding ramp is down.

Buckle stands halfway down the ramp and halfway inside the hab, blocking everything with his compact barrel body. He is pale, damp, stubborn, and perfectly wedged.

Thor stands outside in the rain with a feed sack, rope halter, and an absurdly small pack frame.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Day one with auxiliary pack stock began with a simple loading delay.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
hff
```

**THOR:**
```text
Buckley, the entire purpose of a ramp is that things move down it.
```

---

### Panel 2

**Medium panel.**

Thor kneels beside Buckle, trying to hold the small pack frame against Buckle's underdeveloped burden ridge. It plainly does not fit. Buckle's face is solemn. His flexible snout reaches past Thor toward the dangling paper sale tag.

Visible tag, half-chewed already:

```text
PACK JUVENILE
GROWTH EXPECTED
NO RETURNS
```

**THOR:**
```text
Projected pack utility is not cancelled.
```

**THOR:**
```text
Delayed, perhaps.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
prrp
```

---

### Panel 3

**Close comic panel.**

Buckle chews the sale tag in total concentration. Thor's hand enters frame too late.

The pack frame has slid sideways and hangs from Buckle like a bad thought.

**SFX:**
```text
chmp chmp
```

**THOR:**
```text
That was not a snack. That was a numbered specimen tag.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE, SMALL:**
```text
Projected pack utility: disputed.
```

---

### Panel 4

**Route panel.**

Thor turns away from Buckle and studies the low-water crossing, confident again. He holds the Kuker-Rankin hand level toward the culvert and compares the road dip to his field note from Page 6.

The culvert mouth is black. Thin pale vapor or cold mist lies low to the ground. A faint patch of lichen clings under the culvert lip, but Thor's attention is on the road grade, not the living warning.

**THOR:**
```text
Low water. Black root. Road cut. Good.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I mistook a match for confirmation.
```

---

### Panel 5

**Silent warning panel.**

Buckle freezes.

His ears tilt forward. His nostrils flare. His whole small body becomes a pale wedge pointed at the culvert. The chewed tag dangles from his mouth.

Under the culvert lip, Lung Lichen begins to glow faintly in **Lichen Green**. It should feel biological and practical, not magical: a living warning lamp in wet stone.

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
kweeh
```

**THOR, OFF-PANEL:**
```text
Come on. We are not negotiating with drainage.
```

---

### Panel 6

**Final panel.**

Thor crouches near the culvert with his air meter in hand. The needle finally jitters down. Behind him Buckle has backed completely into Mallow's doorway, refusing the road with every ounce of his small body.

The Lung Lichen glow is now clear but still restrained. It reflects faintly on Thor's hand level and Buckle's wet nose.

Thor's expression shifts from annoyance to understanding.

**AIR METER SFX:**
```text
tik... tik... tik...
```

**THOR:**
```text
Oh.
```

**THOR:**
```text
That is a warning, isn't it?
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Correction: the animal noticed before the instrument did.
```

## Page Turn

The page should turn into Page 10 with a practical consequence:

```text
Thor now has to decide whether to trust Buckle over the route, the map, and his own objective.
```

Page 10 should exploit the cramped hab interior: Thor, Buckle, the manuscript, revised notes, bad air outside, and the first serious realization that the expedition's living companion is not baggage.

---

## Continuity Notes

- Buckle has now joined Thor and Mallow.
- Buckle is not yet accepted as friend, but the emotional turn has begun.
- Buckle should not talk or act human.
- Buckle is animal-smart: he responds to air, pressure, odor, comfort, fear, and routine.
- Do not make Buckle goat-like.
- Do not introduce the e-bike / Buzz-Mule here.
- Do not explain Lung Lichen fully yet.
- Do not make Lung Lichen a magical glow plant. It is a living environmental warning.
- Do not reveal the Girasol.
- Do not turn the page into a battle, chase, or Traverse route sequence.
