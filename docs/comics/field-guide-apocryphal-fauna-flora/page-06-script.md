# Page 06 Script
## The Road Takes Notes

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 06  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Production Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Continuity Position:** After Page 05, `The First Objective`  

---

## Page Function

Page 6 is the first road omen after Thor leaves Ledger Falls.

The page must show that Thor has left the industrial comfort of numbered things and entered a world where the route itself behaves like a living index.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor follows the manuscript's black-glass orchid clue out of Ledger Falls.
The road gives him the first living sign.
Thor documents it too quickly.
```

This page should not become a Traverse sequence. Mallow is moving through the world, but the drama is not route conquest. The drama is Thor's first careless act as a field-naturalist.

## Panel Count

Recommended: **6 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: wide horizontal departure road
Panel 2: cab interior / objective note
Panel 3: warning-sign cluster / road contradiction
Panel 4: track almost crushes the black-glass orchid
Panel 5: Thor kneels and records it
Panel 6: road seen from above, root-lines behind him
```

---

## Script

### Panel 1

**Wide horizontal panel.**

Mallow, the Mirecat field crawler, crawls along an old timber-margin road outside Ledger Falls. The town's last mill lights are small behind it, half-erased by rain. Ahead, the forest tightens into a black wall. Mallow's front blade rides low but is not pushing. The road is mud, gravel, old root, and pale runoff.

The crawler should look small, stubborn, and underprepared.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Ledger Falls ended the way it began: rain, numbers, and men pretending trees were inventory.
```

**SFX:**
```text
KLAK-KLAK-KLAK
```

---

### Panel 2

**Cab interior.**

Thor drives, damp and focused. His dark flat cap and goggles are visible in silhouette. A handwritten field note is taped near the dashboard:

```text
FIRST OBJECTIVE:
BLACK ROOT / GLASS VEIN / LOW WATER
```

Beside it: a folded map, the wrapped Aeonolacertian manuscript, and the Kuker-Rankin hand level. A cracked pressure gauge trembles.

Thor tries to sound reasonable to himself.

**THOR:**
```text
A plant.
```

**THOR:**
```text
A perfectly reasonable first expedition.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
The map did not agree with the book.
```

---

### Panel 3

**Exterior roadside panel.**

Mallow reaches a fork where every sign contradicts the last.

Visible signs:

```text
ROAD CLOSED
FLOOD CUT
PERMIT ROAD
NO SURVEY BEYOND MARKER 17
LOW WATER TAKES WHAT IT IS OWED
```

The last sign is hand-painted and not official.

A thin black mineral vein runs through the ditch like wet glass. Rainwater gathers along it instead of crossing it. No one has marked it properly.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
That should have troubled me more.
```

---

### Panel 4

**Close low panel near Mallow's track.**

The crawler track stops inches before crushing a tiny black-glass orchid growing in a rut. The flower is fragile, dark, reflective, and nearly invisible except for a few specks of Girasol Gold pollen caught in rainwater.

The orchid should not look pretty in a decorative way. It should look like a warning system that happens to be alive.

**SFX:**
```text
CHNK
```

**THOR, OFF-PANEL:**
```text
No.
```

---

### Panel 5

**Medium panel.**

Thor kneels in the rain beside the rut, one knee in mud, sketching quickly on waterproof paper. He sights the flower through the Kuker-Rankin hand level rather than touching it. Mallow looms behind him. The cab light makes a small warm rectangle in the rain.

Thor writes too much: shape, mineral context, water level, route marker, approximate location.

Visible field note fragments:

```text
Index Orchid? roadside specimen
black glass petal / low-water margin
approx. 1.7 mi past Marker 17
DO NOT COLLECT
```

**THOR:**
```text
Observed, not taken.
```

---

### Panel 6

**High overhead final panel.**

Mallow, Thor, and the tiny orchid are seen from above. The road behind them is not just road anymore. Faint black root-lines, mineral veins, and runoff channels form a branching shape like a crude map, or like a thing taking notes back.

Thor does not see it.

Use only the smallest trace of Girasol Gold around the flower and perhaps one rain bead on Thor's field note.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I wrote down where I found it.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
That was the first careless thing I did after leaving home.
```

## Page Turn

The page should turn into Page 7 with a question:

```text
Who else can read a field note?
```

Page 7 should push toward the first consequence of Thor's documentation, not a battle or chase. The story's monster is not a creature yet. It is method, appetite, and attention.
