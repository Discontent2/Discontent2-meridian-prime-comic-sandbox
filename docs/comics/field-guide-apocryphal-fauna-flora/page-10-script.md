## Page Function

Page 10 is the cramped hab interior scene where Thor has to revise his field notes after Buckle's warning at the low-water culvert.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor tries to treat Buckle as a failed pack purchase.
Buckle turns the hab into chaos.
The Lung Lichen tin proves the culvert air was unsafe.
Thor quietly revises Buckle from failed utility to living warning.
```

This page should be funny at the surface and ethically tender underneath.

Buckle should not become magical, talking, or overly human. He remains animal-smart, stubborn, hungry, afraid of bad air, and much better at the world than Thor gives him credit for.

## Panel Count

Recommended: **6 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: Mallow parked at night after the culvert retreat
Panel 2: hab chaos, Buckle blocking the floor

Panel 6: quiet final image, Buckle asleep by the stove, lichen still glowing
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Exterior night panel.**

Mallow is parked off the road beyond the low-water culvert, tucked under black trees. Rain ticks on the roof. The front blade is raised just enough to show the machine retreated instead of forcing through. The culvert mouth sits in the background as a black half-circle, wet and patient.

A faint Lichen Green glow leaks from a sample tin inside the rear hab window.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Retreated from low-water culvert after animal refusal.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Cause unclear.
```

**SFX:**
```text
tik tik tik tik
```

---

### Panel 2

**Interior rear hab.**

Chaos. Buckle has wedged his compact pale body across the narrow floor between the stove and field desk. His rump blocks a drawer. His snout is inside a feed sack. One hoof is on Thor's bedroll. The old sale tag dangles from his neck or harness.

The hab should feel too small for one human and absolutely conquered by one undergrown burdenback.

Thor stands in the doorway, wet, tired, holding a coil of rope and a useless little pack saddle that clearly will not fit.

**THOR:**
```text
Buckley.
```

**THOR:**
```text
You are standing in the entire room.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
hff
```

---

### Panel 3

**Close panel on Thor's desk.**

Thor checks an air meter beside a cracked sample tin of Lung Lichen. The lichen glows faintly green from inside the tin, stronger than it should. His wet glove hovers above the note from Page 9.

Visible notes:

```text
CULVERT AIR: questionable
LUNG LICHEN RESPONSE: active glow
animal refusal precedes meter drop?
```

Thor's confidence begins to wobble.

**THOR:**
```text
That is not nothing.
```

**THOR:**
```text
That is aggressively not nothing.
```

---

### Panel 4

**Medium comic-friction panel.**

Buckle chews the old sale tag:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

The tag is half in his mouth. Thor reaches for it too late. The tiny pack saddle lies on the floor, absurd beside Buckle's round body and underdeveloped burden ridge.

Thor has a field ledger open, tallying costs.

Visible ledger fragments:

```text
feed
rope
wrong saddle
repair latch
animal refund: impossible
```

**THOR:**
```text
That was not a snack.
```

**THOR:**
```text
That was a numbered economic regret.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
prrp
```

---

### Panel 5

**Close panel on field note revision.**

Thor's pencil crosses out a previous line.

Visible field note:

```text
PYGMY PALE BURDENBACK, juvenile
Projected pack utility: delayed
```

The word `delayed` is crossed out.

Thor writes:

```text
Projected pack utility: none
Environmental warning behavior: possible
Keep under observation
Do not overload
```

He hesitates before the final line, then adds it smaller:

```text
Individual name: Buckle
```

A small drip from the roof lands on the margin.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
An honest note is sometimes just an apology written before the person receiving it can read.
```

---

### Panel 6

**Quiet final panel.**

Buckle sleeps by the little stove, enormous in the tiny space, pale against the black interior. His snout rests on Thor's scarf or bedroll. The Lung Lichen tin still glows faintly Lichen Green on the desk. Thor sits beside the field note, looking at Buckle rather than the manuscript.

Outside the rear window, the culvert is invisible in the dark.

**THOR, SMALL:**
```text
Fine.
```

**THOR, SMALL:**
```text
You're coming anyway.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Specimen did not prove useful.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
The word useful had already begun to fail me.
```
