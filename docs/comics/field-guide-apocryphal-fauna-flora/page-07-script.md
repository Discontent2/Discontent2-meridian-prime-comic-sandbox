# Page 07 Script
## A Flag Is Also a Beacon

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 07  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Production Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Continuity Position:** After Page 06, `The Road Takes Notes`  

---

## Page Function

Page 7 is the first consequence of Thor's documentation.

Thor believes he has acted ethically because he did not collect the roadside black-glass orchid. The page reveals that his field method can still endanger what he observes.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor marks the orchid to protect it.
The marker becomes a beacon.
Someone else reads the field note.
```

This page should not reveal a major villain, identify a faction, or turn into a chase. The consequence should feel quiet, practical, and worse because it is plausible.

```text
A Flag Is Also a Beacon
```

## Panel Count

Recommended: **6 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: Thor makes a field flag
Panel 2: Mallow leaves the orchid behind
Panel 3: rain changes what the warning emphasizes
Panel 4: an unseen hand finds the marker
Panel 5: the information is copied elsewhere
Panel 6: Thor continues toward the fringe stop, unaware
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Medium close panel.**

Thor kneels in the mud beside the black-glass orchid. He has not touched the plant. Instead, he ties a small waterproof field flag to a twig several inches away from it.

The field flag is meant to warn future travelers. It is also much more visible than the orchid was.

Visible tag text:

```text
OBSERVED
DO NOT COLLECT
```

In tiny writing near the bottom, Thor adds a location shorthand before thinking better of it:

```text
M17 + 1.7 / LOW RUT
```

He hesitates, then scratches part of it out. Not enough.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I did not pick it.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I did not cut it, jar it, press it, trade it, or carry it home.
```

---

### Panel 2

**Wide panel.**

Mallow crawls away down the wet road. The crawler becomes a blocky black shape in rain and tree shadow.

In the foreground, the field flag remains beside the orchid. The flag is pale against the mud. The orchid is nearly invisible.

Thor's warning has made the place easier to notice.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I left a marker.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
A courtesy.
```

**SFX:**
```text
KLAK-KLAK-KLAK
```

---

### Panel 3

**Close-up of the field flag in rain.**

Water runs over the ink. The scratched-out location code becomes half-legible again. Drops of Girasol Gold pollen from the orchid stick to the wet paper and catch light.

The words distort visually so the page reader notices the danger:

```text
DO NOT COLLECT
```

But rain, folds, and mud briefly isolate:

```text
COLLECT
M17 + 1.7
```

Do not make this supernatural. It is ordinary bad luck sharpened by the page design.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
A warning.
```

---

### Panel 4

**Low silent panel.**

A boot enters the roadside mud near the orchid. We do not see the person's face.

A gloved hand lifts the field flag by its stick.

The orchid remains in the rut, tiny and dark.

Required ambiguity: the person might be a salvager, route worker, plant buyer, curious passerby, or someone else. Do not identify them yet.

No dialogue.

**SFX:**
```text
skk
```

---

### Panel 5

**Interior or sheltered exterior detail panel.**

The field flag is now copied into another wet notebook, ledger scrap, or roadside notice card.

Only hands and objects are visible. No face.

Visible copied text:

```text
BLACK GLASS ORCHID?
M17 + 1.7 / LOW RUT
LIVE ROOT MAY HOLD VALUE
```

A rough sketch of the flower appears beside the note. The sketch is uglier and more useful than Thor's.

A tiny fleck of Girasol Gold pollen has transferred onto the copied page.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I thought the danger began when someone took a living thing.
```

---

### Panel 6

**Final wide panel.**

Mallow rolls toward distant fringe-stop lights. The rain is lighter now. Thor is visible through the cab window, small and intent, writing in his field notebook as he drives slowly.

Behind him, the road disappears into black. Far back near Marker 17, one tiny pale field flag shape is gone.

The page should feel like Thor has already made a mistake he cannot see.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I had not yet learned that a warning is still a sign.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
And signs are made to be followed.
```
