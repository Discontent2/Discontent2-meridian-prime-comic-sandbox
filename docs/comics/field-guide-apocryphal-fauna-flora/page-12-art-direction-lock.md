# Page 12 Art Direction Lock
## Observed, Not Owned

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 12  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Thor redacts the map instead of claiming the discovery, accepts Buckle as companion rather than cargo, and closes the ashcan with the field-guide stamp: OBSERVED. NOT OWNED. LOCATION WITHHELD.
```

Page 12 should feel like a quiet vow, not a victory lap.

---

## Page Mood

```text
morning after consequence
ethical correction
domestic quiet inside the crawler
redaction as protection
wonder withheld
```

The page should carry relief, guilt, and awe all at once. A little ember, a little bruise, a little gold dust at the edge of a black page. 🜂

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Thor processes the harm caused by documentation.
2. Buckle shifts from failed purchase to chosen companion.
3. The field guide becomes protective rather than possessive.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Morning Hab Quiet

Interior of Mallow's rear hab at dawn.

Required elements:

```text
Thor at fold-down desk
cap off or pushed back
manuscript nearby but closed or partly covered
Kuker-Rankin hand level on desk
closed Lung Lichen tin with faint residue
Buckle asleep by stove
bent muddy field flag on floor
```

The hab should look used and humbled, not heroic.

Black shapes should frame the desk and stove. Let pale paper and Buckle's pale body carry the eye.

---

### Panel 2: Redaction Close-Up

Close on Thor's hand redacting the orchid location entry.

Required visible text:

```text
Index Orchid? roadside specimen
approx. 1.7 mi past Marker 17
LOCATION WITHHELD
```

The location line should be struck through or blacked out.

Use thick black ink for redaction. It should feel protective but severe.

---

### Panel 3: Buckle Entry

Medium interior panel with Buckle chewing the old sale tag while Thor writes the first honest entry for him.

Required tag text:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

Required guide entry text:

```text
Pygmy Pale Burdenback
Individual: Buckle
Expected use: pack animal
Actual use: friend
Additional note: notices what I miss
```

Buckle must remain animal-smart, not humanlike. His expression can be stubborn, sleepy, food-motivated, and faintly offended.

---

### Panel 4: Revised Guide Spread

A flat-lay / design-heavy panel of the field guide pages.

Required visuals:

```text
black-glass orchid drawing
Buckle nose / head sketch
Lung Lichen tin or lichen diagram
low-water culvert sketch
Aeonolacertian manuscript fragments
redacted coordinates
```

Required stamps / notes:

```text
LOCATION WITHHELD
DO NOT COLLECT
OBSERVE FROM DISTANCE
DO NOT MAP THE BLOOM
```

This panel should feel like the field plate format from Page 2 has changed morally. It is still beautiful, but now it refuses to become a shopping list.

---

### Panel 5: Mallow Under the Unmapped World

Wide exterior panel.

Mallow is small beneath a huge morning landscape. Thor stands outside with Buckle leaning against his leg. Cloud forest, mist, old road, and distant high shapes surround them.

Required elements:

```text
Mallow small in landscape
Thor no longer posing
Buckle pressed against Thor's leg
morning mist
black forest mass
faint sky-island / ridge silhouettes
```

In the far fog, allow only a subtle negative-space suggestion of crystalline antlers.

No full Girasol reveal.

No creature body.

No direct eye contact from the hidden creature.

---

### Panel 6: Final Stamp

Large closing image or half-page final panel.

The composition should look like a field-guide page swallowed by black negative space.

Required elements:

```text
redacted coordinate block
small black-glass orchid drawing
muddy Buckle print
tiny Girasol Gold glint at page edge
final stamp text
```

Final stamp text must read:

```text
OBSERVED.
NOT OWNED.
LOCATION WITHHELD.
```

Optional tiny teaser text:

```text
The Cave That Breathed waits beyond the erased road.
```

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary closing spot color:

```text
D Girasol Gold
```

Use only for:

- tiny edge glint on the final page
- faint dawn light touching the hand level or page edge
- barely visible antler suggestion in fog, if used

Secondary spot color:

```text
B Rust Red
```

Use sparingly for:

- LOCATION WITHHELD stamp
- one redaction stamp impression

Optional echo:

```text
C Lichen Green
```

Use only as faint dried residue on the closed Lung Lichen tin in Panel 1. Do not let green dominate the ending.

No Acid Magenta on this page.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
redaction marks
hab corners
crawler undercarriage
forest mass
final negative-space field-guide frame
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
field paper
Buckle's pale body
morning mist
redacted page margins
```

The page must still read clearly in pure black and white.

Spot color should feel like a secret caught in the teeth of the paper.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor should look quieter than earlier pages.

Visual language:

```text
less pose
less certainty
cap off or loosened
shoulders lowered
hands careful but no longer hungry
```

He is not cured of ambition. He has received his first correction.

### Buckle

Buckle should be the page's soft anchor.

Visual language:

```text
pale compact body
snout near torn tag
sleepy seriousness
forehead lean against Thor's leg
animal comfort without sentimentality
```

Buckle is not cargo, not mascot, not magical solution. He is a living creature Thor chooses not to measure only by utility.

### Mallow

Mallow should feel like shelter, not machine dominance.

Visual language:

```text
small warm hab rectangle
scarred but still useful
not triumphant
still ugly and brave
```

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
full Girasol reveal
Girasol body
combat
chase action
anonymous collector identity
explained manuscript mechanics
Buckle acting human
clean sci-fi polish
bright heroic sunrise poster
complete map to any protected organism
```
