# Page 09 Art Direction Lock
## Buckley Refuses the Air

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 09  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Buckle creates Thor's first practical travel problem by blocking Mallow's ramp and ruining the pack-stock fantasy, then detects bad air at a low-water culvert before Thor's instrument catches up.
```

Page 9 should turn Buckle from mistake into signal.

He remains inconvenient. He also may be right.

---

## Page Mood

```text
wet roadside comedy
animal stubbornness
instrument failure
living warning
small companion becomes useful by refusing usefulness
```

The tonal trick: the first half is funny and domestic; the second half makes the joke quietly save Thor from doing something stupid.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Buckle is a logistical problem.
2. Thor misreads the route because it matches his note.
3. Buckle reads the air and refuses danger.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Ramp Blockage

Wide panel beside a low-water culvert.

Required elements:

```text
Mallow parked roadside
rear hab door open
folding ramp down
Buckle wedged halfway in / halfway out
Thor outside with feed sack, rope halter, and too-small pack frame
wet culvert in background
rain and black roots
```

Buckle should be the brightest living shape in the panel: pale body against black hab interior.

The ramp should feel comically defeated.

---

### Panel 2: Failed Pack Frame

Medium panel.

Thor tries to fit a small pack frame across Buckle's underdeveloped burden ridge.

Required Buckle details:

```text
pygmy hippo-like body mass
rhino-calf stance
tapir-like flexible snout
short sturdy legs
pale thick skin
small dark eyes
underdeveloped burden ridge
serious concentration
```

Visible tag:

```text
PACK JUVENILE
GROWTH EXPECTED
NO RETURNS
```

The pack frame should visibly not fit. It should not look cruel, just absurd and misguided.

---

### Panel 3: Tag Chewing

Close panel.

Buckle chews the sale tag with total focus while the pack frame slips sideways.

Required expression:

```text
solemn
food-motivated
unbothered by Thor's expectations
```

Do not make Buckle goat-like. No bleating. Use snorts, chirps, huffs, grunts, nasal whistles, and tapir-like peeps.

---

### Panel 4: Route Misread

Thor studies the low-water crossing, temporarily ignoring Buckle.

Required elements:

```text
Thor with Kuker-Rankin hand level
field note / route note visible
black root tangle
culvert mouth
low pale vapor or cold mist
Lung Lichen visible but not yet emphasized
```

Visual priority:

Thor's line of sight should go toward the road grade, not the lichen.

The reader should notice the warning before Thor does.

---

### Panel 5: Buckle Freezes

Silent warning panel.

Buckle freezes and points his whole body toward the culvert.

Required Buckle body language:

```text
ears forward
nostrils flared
front feet planted
body low and heavy
chewed tag dangling from mouth
refusal as instinct, not disobedience
```

Lung Lichen begins to glow under the culvert lip.

Spot color:

```text
C Lichen Green
```

Use it sparingly, as a biological warning signal.

---

### Panel 6: Instrument Catches Up

Final panel.

Thor crouches near the culvert with the air meter. The needle drops late. Buckle has backed into Mallow's doorway, refusing to move forward.

Required elements:

```text
air meter needle dropping
Thor's annoyance turning into comprehension
Buckle in doorway
Lung Lichen glow reflected on Buckle's wet nose
hand level reflection
Mallow as dark shelter behind them
```

The final image should feel like a small truth landing:

```text
The creature Thor thought he bought for future labor has already provided present warning.
```

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary page spot color:

```text
C Lichen Green
```

Use for:

- Lung Lichen under culvert
- faint reflection on Buckle's wet nose
- faint reflection on Thor's hand level or air meter glass

Optional tiny secondary:

```text
B Rust Red
```

Use only on the sale tag stamp or weathered culvert warning mark if needed.

Do not use Girasol Gold prominently on this page. The page belongs to Buckle and Lung Lichen, not the manuscript or Girasol.

No Acid Magenta.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
culvert mouth
Mallow interior
wet root tangle
roadside ditch
Buckle shadow under body
Thor's coat and cap silhouette
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
Buckle's pale body
rain streaks
field paper edge
steam / pale mist
sale tag
air meter face
```

The page must still read clearly if printed in pure black and white, with Lichen Green acting as emphasis rather than explanation.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor begins the page slightly ridiculous, trying to justify the purchase.

Visual language:

```text
practical confidence
forced professionalism
muddy knees
one hand protecting the pack frame
field-note brain overriding animal sense
```

By the last panel, his expression should say: he is still irritated, but learning has entered the room.

### Buckle / Buckley

Buckle is not a mascot gag. He is a small stubborn living instrument with needs and instincts.

Visual language:

```text
compact wetland tank
pale shape in black ink
soft flexible snout
small determined feet
serious eating face
sudden freeze response
forehead wrinkles
wet nose catching lichen glow
```

Buckle should not be cute because he is decorative. He is cute because he is absolutely himself in a world Thor keeps trying to categorize.

### Mallow

Mallow should feel newly crowded by Buckle's presence.

Visual language:

```text
rear hab doorway blocked
ramp bent under small heavy animal
dark warm interior behind Buckle
field clutter pushed around by animal logistics
```

## Buckle Sound Rules

Allowed sounds:

```text
hff
prrp
snrk
whuff
brrp
kweeh
```

Avoid:

```text
bleat
baa
goat noises
human speech
```

---

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
Buckle acting human
Buckle talking
Buckle as goat-like
Buzz-Mule / e-bike
full Girasol
combat
chase action
Traverse convoy language
explained Lung Lichen science
magical glowing flower treatment
```

## One-Sentence Art Prompt

Heavy-black-ink modern ashcan comic page, mostly black and white with restrained Lichen Green spot color, showing Thor Fortner's compact tracked field crawler Mallow parked beside a wet low-water culvert while Buckle, a small pale pygmy hippo-rhino-tapir-like Pale Burdenback, blocks the rear ramp, chews his GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS sale tag, refuses a too-small pack frame, then freezes and chirps at faintly glowing Lung Lichen under the culvert before Thor's air meter finally drops.
