# Page 12 Script
## Observed, Not Owned

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 12  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Production Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Continuity Position:** Final page of the 12-page ashcan, after Page 11, `The Flag Found Someone`

---

## Page Function

Page 12 closes the ashcan proof of concept.

Thor does not solve the manuscript, reveal the Girasol, or master the ecosystem. He changes method.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor chooses redaction over possession.
Buckle is accepted as companion rather than cargo.
The field guide becomes an ethic instead of a trophy case.
```

This is the page where the title becomes a vow.

## Panel Count

Recommended: **6 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: morning inside Mallow, after the loss
Panel 2: Thor revises the guide
Panel 3: Buckle receives the first honest entry
Panel 4: redaction spread / location withheld
Panel 5: exterior wide, Mallow beneath living landscape
Panel 6: final field-guide stamp / distant Girasol hint
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Interior of Mallow's rear hab, early morning.**

The cramped hab is quiet after the previous night's chaos. Thor sits at the fold-down desk. His cap is off. His goggles lie beside the manuscript. The Lung Lichen tin is closed but still faintly marked with dried green residue. Buckle sleeps by the stove with Thor's scarf partly under his chin.

The field flag from Page 11 is on the floor, bent and muddy.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I had thought a field guide was a way to save what people refused to believe in.
```

---

### Panel 2

**Close on Thor's hand over the field notes.**

Thor draws a thick black line through a neat location entry for the black-glass orchid. He does not erase the observation. He erases the usable directions.

Visible text before and after redaction:

```text
Index Orchid? roadside specimen
~~approx. 1.7 mi past Marker 17~~
LOCATION WITHHELD
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
But a name can be a handle.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
A coordinate can be a trap.
```

---

### Panel 3

**Medium domestic panel.**

Buckle wakes, reaches up with his flexible snout, and chews the torn sale tag one last time. The tag reads:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

Thor gently takes it away, not angry this time.

On the page beside him, Thor writes a new guide entry:

```text
Pygmy Pale Burdenback
Individual: Buckle
Expected use: pack animal
Actual use: friend
Additional note: notices what I miss
```

**THOR:**
```text
Fine.
```

**THOR:**
```text
You're coming anyway.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
hff
```

---

### Panel 4

**Field-guide layout panel.**

A spread of Thor's revised guide pages fills the panel. We see sketches of the black-glass orchid, Lung Lichen, Buckle's nose, the low-water culvert, and fragments of the ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript.

Every useful site marker is blacked out, stamped, or replaced by caution language.

Visible stamps / notes:

```text
LOCATION WITHHELD
DO NOT COLLECT
OBSERVE FROM DISTANCE
DO NOT MAP THE BLOOM
```

A small line in Thor's handwriting:

```text
A guide is not a key.
```

---

### Panel 5

**Wide exterior panel.**

Mallow rests tiny beneath a huge morning landscape: cloud forest, wet road, distant rock, and fog lifting into sky-island shapes. The crawler looks less like a machine of conquest and more like a shelter that has learned to stop.

Thor stands outside with Buckle beside his boot. Buckle leans into his leg. Thor looks outward, not upward, as if he has finally stopped posing as an explorer long enough to listen.

In the far fog, branches and cloud shapes form the faintest suggestion of crystalline antlers.

Do not show the full Girasol.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Some things are rare because they are dying.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Some are rare because they are good at surviving us.
```

---

### Panel 6

**Final half-page or large closing image.**

A field-guide page lies open over black negative space. It contains no map. Only a small drawing of the black-glass orchid, a paw/hoof-like muddy Buckle print, a redacted coordinate block, and a tiny restrained glint of Girasol Gold along the page edge.

Final stamp dominates the composition:

```text
OBSERVED.
NOT OWNED.
LOCATION WITHHELD.
```

At the very bottom, optional tiny teaser line:

```text
The Cave That Breathed waits beyond the erased road.
```

---

## Page Turn / Ashcan Close

This page ends the ashcan as a proof of tone, character, ethics, and visual language.

It should invite a longer story without creating a cliffhanger based on danger alone.

The unresolved question is not:

```text
Will Thor find the rare creature?
```

The unresolved question is:

```text
Can Thor learn how to witness without taking?
```

---

## Continuity Notes

- The black-glass orchid remains missing.
- The party responsible is not identified.
- Do not resolve who copied Thor's field note.
- Do not reveal the full Girasol.
- Do not explain the manuscript fully.
- Do not make Buckle magical or talking.
- Buckle is recognized as friend and warning animal, not pack utility.
- The page should complete Thor's first ethical correction, not complete his entire character arc.
