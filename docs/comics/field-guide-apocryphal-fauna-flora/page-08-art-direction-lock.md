# Page 08 Art Direction Lock
## Growth Expected, No Returns

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 08  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Thor reaches a fringe feed-and-supply stop looking for practical expedition supplies and leaves with Buckle, an undergrown pale Pale Burdenback sold as future pack stock under a tag that reads GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS.
```

Page 8 should feel like the story quietly slides from field mission into companionship.

Buckle should be funny because he is specific, inconvenient, and alive, not because he is a mascot pasted into the page.

---

## Page Mood

```text
wet fringe commerce
bad bargain warmth
underfunded expedition comedy
failed utility becoming attachment
soft white animal against black mud
```

The page should be warmer than Pages 6 and 7, but still rain-soaked and slightly predatory. Everything at the feed stop has a price tag. Buckle is the first thing Thor buys that will refuse to remain an object.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Thor enters a crooked fringe supply stop to solve practical problems.
2. Buckle appears as a pale shape among sacks, mud, and cheap tags.
3. Buckle is inside Mallow, already making the crawler less orderly and more alive.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Fringe Stop Establishing Shot

Wide establishing panel.

Mallow parked outside a roadside feed-and-field-supply stop. The building should look improvised from timber-yard boards, tarps, corrugated metal, and old signage.

Required signage:

```text
LOW WATER FEED + FIELD SUPPLY
SALT MOSS / FILTERS / TACK / DRY PAPER
NO MAP REFUNDS
```

Required visual elements:

```text
Mallow parked near awning
muddy yard
feed sacks
rain barrels
animal pens
hanging tarps
crooked porch
wet trees behind
```

Do not show the e-bike here.

Do not make this a machine salvage shop. This is animal/feed/fringe field supply territory.

---

### Panel 2: Counter Scene

Thor at the counter under a leaking awning. He is trying to appear competent while visibly damp, tired, and oversupplied with confidence.

Required elements:

```text
Thor's flat cap and goggles
field jacket and scarf
cross-body satchel
muddy boots
field supply list
vendor behind counter
feed sacks and stamped receipts
```

Visible list fragments:

```text
waterproof paper
filter cloth
salt moss
small field cycle?
```

The vendor should look local, practical, and unimpressed. Do not overdesign them into a major character unless later needed.

---

### Panel 3: Machines Are Elsewhere

Close or medium panel of the vendor crossing out Thor's field-cycle request.

The panel must visually establish:

```text
No e-bike purchase here.
Low Road gets the machines.
This stop sells feed, animals, and mistakes.
```

Suggested visible counter objects:

```text
thick pencil
receipt spike
tack hooks
filter cloth rolls
salt moss sacks
field tags
```

In the background, behind sacks or pen slats, Buckle's pale snout or eye should appear for the first time.

Buckle's first visual should be partial, like a strange soft moon peeking out from behind bad inventory.

---

### Panel 4: Buckle Reveal

Medium reveal panel.

Buckle stands in a muddy little pen under a torn tarp.

Required anatomy lock:

```text
small pale pygmy hippo / rhino calf / tapir blend
compact barrel body
short sturdy legs
broad pad-feet / split keratin toes
thick pale hide with wrinkles at neck and legs
flexible upper lip / mini proboscis
broad rounded muzzle
tiny rounded ears
glossy dark eyes
underdeveloped burden ridge
no goat traits
no horns beyond tiny keratin brow nubs if visible
```

Buckle should be chewing his own sale tag with total focus.

Required tag text:

```text
PALE BURDENBACK
PYGMY / JUVENILE
PACK STOCK
GROWTH EXPECTED
NO RETURNS
```

Buckle should be the brightest living shape on the page.

---

### Panel 5: Professional Inspection / Emotional Trap

Thor crouches outside the pen, trying to inspect Buckle as future field equipment.

Visual contrast:

```text
Thor = categories, field notes, utility logic
Buckle = snout through fence, steals the field list, refuses category
```

Required action:

Buckle takes or chews the corner of Thor's field list.

Required mood:

Thor should look analytical, not yet sentimental. The reader should already know he is doomed.

Suggested visible field note fragments:

```text
Pale Burdenback, pygmy variant
Condition: underfed
Temperament: expensive
Projected pack utility: delayed
```

---

### Panel 6: Buckle Inside Mallow

Interior rear hab panel.

Buckle is now inside Mallow's cramped hab. He occupies the wrong space: where Thor intended to stack feed, specimen crates, or field paper.

Required elements:

```text
Buckle inside Mallow
Thor in doorway with receipt
wrapped manuscript on desk
feed sacks displaced
field crates pushed aside
stove / kettle / cramped hab details
Buckle chewing GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS tag
```

The panel should make the hab feel instantly changed. Less sterile. Less controlled. More alive.

This should be the first time Mallow feels like it could become a home instead of just a mobile lab.

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary page spot color:

```text
B Rust Red
```

Use Rust Red for:

- sale tags
- stamped receipt marks
- old warning paint on the feed stop
- tack labels / field tags
- possibly a tiny red price mark on Buckle's sale tag

Optional tiny Girasol Gold:

- very faint manuscript glint in final panel only

Do not overuse gold. Page 8 belongs to Buckle and the field-stop economy, not the hidden Girasol thread.

No Acid Magenta on this page.

No Lichen Green on this page.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
wet porch shadows
animal pen interiors
Mallow undercarriage
rain-dark tarps
mud below Buckle
rear hab interior corners
```

White / pale shapes should carry:

```text
Buckle's body
Thor's field list
receipt paper
rain streaks
Mallow window highlights
```

Buckle's pale body against mud and black ink is the visual hook.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor should be serious, tired, and self-deceiving.

Visual language:

```text
pinched concentration
field-note posture
pretending the decision is practical
holding receipt like evidence against himself
```

He does not cuddle Buckle. He categorizes him.

That is the joke and the wound.

### Buckle

Buckle should be animal-smart and physically specific.

Visual language:

```text
stubborn little wetland tank
soft snout
tiny ears
muddy feet
serious chewing
concerned dark eyes
compact weight
not graceful
not goat-like
```

Buckle sounds should be snorts, huffs, chirps, nasal whistles, or tapir-like peeps.

Allowed SFX:

```text
snrk
prrp
whuff
brrp
hff
```

Do not use bleats.

### Vendor

The vendor should be functional, local, and dryly practical.

Visual language:

```text
rain hat or work apron
receipt pencil
feed dust on sleeves
unimpressed posture
knows more than they explain
```

Do not make the vendor sinister. Commerce is pressure enough.

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
Buzz-Mule / e-bike purchase
machine salvage shop
Buckle as goat-like
Buckle bleating
full Girasol
combat
chase action
Traverse convoy language
large animal pack saddle already fitted
Buckle instantly useful
Thor instantly sentimental
```

## One-Sentence Art Prompt

Heavy-black-ink modern ashcan comic page, mostly black and white with selective Rust Red tag and receipt accents, showing Thor Fortner stopping at a crooked rainy fringe feed-and-field-supply yard with Mallow parked outside; he asks for waterproof paper, fuel, and a small field cycle, but the vendor says machines are elsewhere, then Thor notices Buckle, a small pale pygmy hippo-rhino-tapir-like Pale Burdenback chewing a tag that reads GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS, and the final panel shows Buckle inside Mallow's cramped rear hab, already disrupting Thor's orderly field system.
