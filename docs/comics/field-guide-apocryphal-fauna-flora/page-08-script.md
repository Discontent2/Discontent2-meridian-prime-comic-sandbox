# Page 08 Script
## Growth Expected, No Returns

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 08  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Production Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Continuity Position:** After Page 07, `A Flag Is Also a Beacon`

## Page Function

Page 8 introduces Buckle without making him cute-only or instantly useful.

Thor reaches a fringe feed-and-supply stop after leaving the roadside orchid marker behind. He is trying to behave like a professional field-naturalist: acquire feed, confirm the road ahead, and ask whether anyone sells a small field cycle.

Instead, he notices an undergrown Pale Burdenback with a sale tag that treats the animal as future utility.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor arrives at a fringe animal/feed stop looking for supplies and practical transport.
He finds Buckle, a pygmy Pale Burdenback sold as a future pack animal.
The vendor calls him delayed stock.
Thor calls him a temporary logistical decision.
The page quietly knows he has just met his friend.
```

Important continuity lock:

```text
The e-bike / Buzz-Mule is not purchased here.
This stop sells feed, animals, field junk, and bad advice.
Machines come later, at a different location.
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Wide establishing panel.**

Mallow is parked beside a crooked fringe feed-and-supply stop at the road edge. The place is half livestock shed, half salvage porch, half rain shelter, despite math objecting. The sign is patched from old timber-yard boards.

Visible signage:

```text
LOW WATER FEED + FIELD SUPPLY
SALT MOSS / FILTERS / TACK / DRY PAPER
NO MAP REFUNDS
```

The road behind Mallow vanishes into wet trees. A few cages, pens, tarped crates, sacks, and rain barrels crowd the yard. The black-glass orchid field flag from Page 7 should not appear here; that moment is behind Thor.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
A responsible expedition requires supplies, restraint, and no additional problems.
```

---

### Panel 2

**Medium panel at the counter.**

Thor stands at a rough counter under a leaking awning. He is muddy from Page 6 and trying to look like someone who has a plan. A fringe vendor sits behind the counter surrounded by feed sacks, field tags, stamped receipts, and drying gloves.

Thor places a short list on the counter.

Visible list fragments:

```text
waterproof paper
filter cloth
salt moss
small field cycle?
```

**THOR:**
```text
I need waterproof paper, clean fuel, and a map that lies less than the last one.
```

**VENDOR:**
```text
Then you need a different planet.
```

---

### Panel 3

**Counter close-up / vendor gesture.**

Thor points to the line about a small field cycle. The vendor scratches it out with a thick pencil.

**VENDOR:**
```text
Machines? Not here.
```

**VENDOR:**
```text
Low Road gets the machines. We get the mistakes.
```

This line establishes that the e-bike / Buzz-Mule belongs later at a different location.

Behind the vendor, partly visible through hanging feed sacks, a small pale animal snuffles at a chewed paper tag.

**SFX, SMALL:**
```text
snrk
```

---

### Panel 4

**Buckle reveal panel.**

Thor turns. Buckle stands in a muddy little pen under a torn tarp: a small pale pygmy hippo-rhino-tapir-like Pale Burdenback with a barrel body, short sturdy legs, thick hide, flexible snout, tiny rounded ears, glossy dark eyes, and an underdeveloped burden ridge.

He is trying, with deep concentration, to chew the corner off his own sale tag.

The tag reads:

```text
PALE BURDENBACK
PYGMY / JUVENILE
PACK STOCK
GROWTH EXPECTED
NO RETURNS
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
prrp
```

**THOR:**
```text
That is not on my list.
```

---

### Panel 5

**Medium close panel.**

Thor crouches outside the pen, trying to inspect Buckle professionally. He does not touch him yet. He uses the same seriousness he used on the orchid, but Buckle is already disrupting the category.

Buckle presses his flexible snout through the fence and steals the corner of Thor's field list.

**THOR:**
```text
Pale Burdenback. Pygmy variant.
```

**THOR:**
```text
Condition: underfed. Temperament: expensive.
```

**VENDOR, OFF-PANEL:**
```text
He'll grow.
```

**VENDOR, OFF-PANEL:**
```text
Probably.
```

**THOR:**
```text
Projected pack utility: delayed.
```

The line should read as Thor lying politely to himself.

---

### Panel 6

**Interior / rear hab panel.**

Inside Mallow's rear hab, later. Buckle is now inside, occupying the exact place where Thor intended to stack neat feed sacks and field crates. He is too small to be useful and already too present to ignore.

Thor stands in the doorway holding a receipt and looking betrayed by his own decision. Buckle chews the edge of a tag that reads:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

The manuscript is safely wrapped on the desk. The field system is immediately less organized than it was on Page 4.

**THOR:**
```text
This is temporary.
```

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
whuff
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
This was the second careless thing I did after leaving home.
```

## Page Turn

The page should turn into Page 9 with a practical problem:

```text
Buckle cannot carry the expedition.
He can, however, change it.
```

Page 9 should show the first travel/camp complication with Buckle and begin revealing that he notices environmental cues Thor misses.

## Continuity Notes

- Buckle enters the story on Page 8.
- Buckle is purchased at a fringe animal/feed stop, not at the later machine salvage stop.
- The e-bike / Buzz-Mule is explicitly not purchased here.
- Buckle should not be goat-like and should not bleat.
- Buckle is animal-smart, not talking.
- The page must not treat Buckle as mere mascot. He is the White Elephant motif made alive.
- Do not explain his final emotional role too directly.
- Do not make Thor instantly sentimental. He should rationalize the purchase as utility first.
