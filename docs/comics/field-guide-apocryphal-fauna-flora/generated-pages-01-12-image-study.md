## Major Structural Finding

The strongest version of the ashcan is **not** the older nine-organism plate dump. The image pass works best when Page 02 functions as a damaged index and Pages 05-12 each carry one reveal.

Buckle should remain separate from the eight organism reveal slots. He is the companion pressure system, the White Elephant, and the first lesson that life does not need usefulness, rarity, or proof-value to deserve care.

The corrected reveal ladder is:

```text
Page 05 - Index Orchid
Page 06 - Lung Lichen
Page 07 - Crimson Glass Salamander
Page 08 - Gift-Pod
Page 09 - Nightwake
Page 10 - Snow-Dire Leafcat
Page 11 - Dwarf Craton Sauropod
Page 12 - Girasol
```

## Visual Identity Lock From the Image Pass

The successful pages share a clear production language:

```text
Heavy black inks
Cream field-guide header band
Tall serif title typography
Rain, snow, wet stone, ruins, and black vertical silhouettes
Muted blue / indigo / violet base
Warm amber windows and lamps
Magenta-violet skies and water reflection
Selective red stamps and warning labels
Creature-specific spot color: gold, green, red, magenta, icy blue
Field-note caption boxes as moral narration
Botanical / zoological plate inserts as evidence, not decoration
```

The page style should feel like:

```text
A field guide smuggled out of a ruined wetland city, printed too dark, annotated too late.
```

---

## Repeating Page Architecture

Most successful pages use a reliable five-part rhythm:

1. **Large atmospheric establishing panel** with Mallow, Thor, Buckle, and the biome.
2. **Behavior / warning panel** where Buckle or the environment resists Thor.
3. **Creature reveal panel** with cinematic scale or beauty.
4. **Field-guide plate / manuscript evidence panel** that reframes the scene.
5. **Moral correction panel** where Thor revises what he thought he knew.

This rhythm should be preserved even if future page layouts are redrawn.

## Character and Object Findings

### Thor Fortner

Thor reads clearly when shown as:

```text
Dark cap with goggles
Weathered coat
Red scarf / neck cloth
Satchel and field notebook
Crouched, studying, writing, overconfident, then corrected
```

His best panels show him small against the environment, not heroic against it.

### Buckle / Buckley

Buckle is now essential on nearly every page after Page 04. His visual role is:

```text
Pale, low, round, wetland-body contrast against black ruins
Animal intuition before human interpretation
Emotional scale marker
Silent moral witness
Comic softness without breaking tone
```

Buckle's most important story functions in the image pass:

- refuses bad air before Thor understands Lung Lichen
- dislikes dangerous organisms before Thor does
- keeps Thor grounded in care rather than proof
- visually softens the harshness of ruins and field-guide violence

### Mallow / The Tin Burrow

Mallow works best as a recurring lit shelter:

```text
Tracked crawler
Boxy hab module
Warm amber windows
Blade and undercarriage in heavy black
Parked at the edge of danger, never conquering it
```

Mallow should remain a home / lab / moral shelter, not a battle vehicle.

The corrected Page 12 image established the final-wonder logic:

```text
Black, deerlike / okapi-like wetland ungulate
Crystalline or petal-like antlers

Do not map

Final vow: We observe. We follow. We do not own.
```

The Girasol should never become a clean trophy shot. Even when visible, it should feel like the page barely held it long enough to draw.

## Corrected Page 10-12 Lessons

The image pass had two near-miss hallucination points that are now important guardrails:

```text
Page 10 must be Snow-Dire Leafcat, not tentacled action or unrelated horror.
Page 11 must be Dwarf Craton Sauropod, not another Snow-Dire Leafcat page.
Page 12 must be Girasol and final redaction, not another sauropod dossier.
```

Future generation prompts should state the page title, reveal creature, and function at the top in blunt terms.

- water / ruins / twilight reflection
