# Page 10 Art Direction Lock
## Projected Pack Utility: None

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 10  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Inside Mallow's cramped rear hab at night, Buckle turns the expedition into domestic chaos while a Lung Lichen tin glows faintly green, forcing Thor to revise Buckle from failed pack utility to possible environmental warning animal.
```

This page is the first time the moving home visibly becomes Buckle's home too.

---

## Page Mood

```text
wet night shelter
cramped comic frustration
small animal trust
ethical revision
warm stove against black road
```

The page should feel intimate and boxy. Mallow is not a vehicle showcase here. It is a tiny room where Thor's assumptions get chewed, slept on, and corrected.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Mallow retreats and parks instead of forcing the route.
2. Buckle overwhelms the hab as a practical inconvenience.
3. The Lung Lichen glow and Thor's revised note reveal Buckle was not useless.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Mallow Parked After Retreat

Exterior night panel.

Mallow should sit low under black trees, rain stippling the roof. The low-water culvert is a dark mouth in the background. The front blade should be raised just enough to imply retreat rather than conquest.

Required elements:

```text
Mallow parked off-road
black trees
rain on roof
culvert mouth in background
small rear hab window
faint Lichen Green glow inside
```

Ink priority:

- black trees
- black culvert opening
- dark crawler undercarriage
- wet white rain marks
- tiny green window glow

---

### Panel 2: Hab Chaos

Interior rear hab, cramped and funny.

Buckle should physically dominate the tiny space without seeming monstrous. He is round, pale, low, stubborn, and accidentally logistical disaster-shaped.

Required elements:

```text
Buckle wedged across narrow floor
feed sack compromised
one hoof on bedroll
snout near stove or supply crate
Thor in wet gear at the door
small wrong pack saddle visible
sale tag still on Buckle
```

Buckle anatomy must remain locked:

```text
pygmy hippo body mass
rhino calf stance
tapir-like flexible snout
pale thick hide
underdeveloped burden ridge
small dark eyes
tiny rounded ears
short sturdy legs
```

Do not make him goat-like.

---

### Panel 3: Lung Lichen Proof

Close desk panel.

The Lung Lichen tin glows faintly Lichen Green beside Thor's air meter and wet notes. The glow should feel biological and practical, not fantasy sparkle.

Required visible note fragments:

```text
CULVERT AIR: questionable
LUNG LICHEN RESPONSE: active glow
animal refusal precedes meter drop?
```

The air meter can be old, cracked, and unreliable. The lichen looks more honest than the instrument.

---

### Panel 4: Sale Tag Chewed

Medium panel with comic bite.

Buckle chews the old sale tag. It must be legible enough to read:

```text
GROWTH EXPECTED / NO RETURNS
```

The wrong pack saddle should be nearby, clearly absurd and unusable. Thor's field ledger should show costs and irritation.

Visual joke:

```text
Buckle is chewing the paperwork that defined him as cargo.
```

---

### Panel 5: Field Note Revision

Close panel on Thor's hand and notebook.

The crossed-out note must be legible:

```text
Projected pack utility: delayed
```

Then the revision:

```text
Projected pack utility: none
Environmental warning behavior: possible
Keep under observation
Do not overload
Individual name: Buckle
```

The addition of `Individual name: Buckle` should feel small, hesitant, and important.

This is the page's emotional hinge.

---

### Panel 6: Stove Nest

Quiet final panel.

Buckle sleeps by the stove, pale against the black interior. His snout rests on Thor's scarf or bedroll. Thor sits nearby, not touching him yet, looking at him differently.

Required elements:

```text
Buckle asleep by stove
Thor seated with notebook
Lung Lichen tin still faintly green
small stove warmth
black windows
cramped hab shelves
```

The page should end warm but uneasy. The outside world is still dark. The ethical lesson is beginning, not complete.

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary page spot color:

```text
C Lichen Green
```

Use only for:

- Lung Lichen tin glow
- faint reflection on Thor's note
- possibly a small green shine on Buckle's pale snout

Optional tiny warm tone:

```text
D Girasol Gold
```

Use only if the stove or manuscript glint needs a warmer counterpoint. Keep it almost invisible.

No Acid Magenta on this page.

No Rust Red unless the old sale tag needs a tiny weathered stamp mark.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
hab corners
window voids
culvert opening
crawler undercarriage
shelf shadows
stove pipe shadow
Buckle's contact shadow on floor
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
Buckle's pale body
field note pages
rain streaks
small interior highlights
```

The page must remain readable in black and white. The Lichen Green should clarify the warning, not carry the entire panel.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor is tired, damp, and still trying to make a ledger of things.

Visual language:

```text
wet sleeves
muddy knees
pencil hesitation
one hand near air meter
one hand protecting notebook from drips
frustration softening into revision
```

He should not look suddenly enlightened. He should look reluctantly honest.

### Buckle

Buckle is not performing. He is eating, blocking, sleeping, and avoiding bad air.

Visual language:

```text
low stubborn body
serious chewing concentration
small ear flick
snout into feed sack
snout on scarf while asleep
pale body as emotional anchor
```

Buckle should be cute because he is real and inconvenient, not because the page begs for cuteness.

### Mallow

Mallow's interior should feel like a moral barometer.

Beginning of page:

```text
organized enough to pretend Thor has a plan
```

End of page:

```text
messier but more honest
```
