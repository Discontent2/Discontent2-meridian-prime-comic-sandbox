# Observed, Not Owned
## 12-Page Modern Ashcan Beat Sheet

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Development / Ashcan Proof of Concept  
**Format:** 12-page modern ashcan  
**Visual Lane:** Heavy black inks, mostly black and white, selective spot color  

---

## Ashcan Purpose

This ashcan is not the full story. It is a proof-of-concept artifact meant to show:

1. Thor Fortner is compelling.
2. Meridian Prime works outside the Traverse spine.
3. The field-guide format is visually unique.
4. Heavy black inks can carry the world.
5. Buckle adds heart and tension.
6. The ancient manuscript and Girasol create a long-form hook.

Core ashcan shape:

```text
A tiny expedition begins badly.
```

## Page 1: Cover

### Function

Mood, logo, premise image.

### Image

Thor stands beside the tracked field crawler at the edge of a black cloud forest.

The crawler's front blade is half-buried in moss, snow-crust, and crystal grit. The rear hab module glows faintly. Buckle's tiny pale shape peers from the open door.

Behind them, the forest forms the shape of crystalline antlers, but only if the reader looks twice.

### Color

Black and white core. Spot color depends on edition:

- Girasol Gold for main edition
- Acid Magenta, Rust Red, or Lichen Green for variants

### Text

```text
OBSERVED, NOT OWNED
A Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora
```

## Page 2: False Field Plate

### Function

Introduce the field-guide format and nine apocryphal motifs.

### Image

A fake scientific plate from Thor's future guide. It shows nine silhouettes:

- Index Orchid
- Pygmy Pale Burdenback
- Girasol
- Crimson Glass Salamander
- Gift-Pod
- Nightwake
- Lung Lichen
- Dwarf Craton sauropod
- Snow-Dire Leafcat

Most are partially labeled, misclassified, or stamped with warnings.

### Labels

```text
UNVERIFIED
MISCLASSIFIED
DO NOT MAP
SPECIMEN REFUSED
LOCATION WITHHELD
```

The Girasol silhouette is labeled only:

```text
OBSERVED.
```

### Color

Gold annotations only, if using the main Girasol Gold interior strategy.

## Pages 3-4: Ledger Falls Cold Open

### Function

Establish Thor's world, theme, and home pressure.

### Image

Thor in Ledger Falls, sketching an animal or plant in the rain while logging crews, permit clerks, and local workers argue over tags and boundaries.

The town should feel busy, wet, industrial, and alive:

- cloud rainforest pressing into mill lights
- worker stilts
- permit boards
- moss on machinery
- steam from haulers
- sawdust rain
- green-black fog
- tags nailed to living trees

Thor finds a weird plant growing from a permit stamp machine, archive wall, or old infrastructure seam. It is not yet identified as Index Orchid, but the visual rhymes with it.

### Suggested Caption

```text
Ledger Falls taught me every living thing becomes easier to kill once someone gives it a number.
```

### Page-Turn Hook

Thor notices a symbol in the plant's petal pattern that matches the ancient manuscript.

---

## Page 5: The Crawler Reveal

### Function

Introduce Thor's vehicle and expedition life.

### Image

Hero shot of Thor's compact Sno-Cat-like field crawler.

It should have:

- front blade
- tracked base
- rounded cab
- rear hab module
- roof rack
- sample poles
- spare filters
- patched panels
- hand-painted field marks
- old dents and stains

Thor loads:

- specimen tins
- sketch rolls
- canned food
- oxygen bottles
- cracked field camera
- old lantern
- moss scraper
- empty creature plates
- forbidden manuscript wrapped in oilcloth

### Suggested Caption

```text
So I built a house that could run.
```

---

## Pages 6-7: The Manuscript

### Function

Introduce the MacGuffin and the Girasol obsession.

### Image

Thor opens the ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript.

The page should be dense and strange:

- Voynich-like biological diagrams
- pressure-script
- saurian marginalia
- impossible botany
- creature plates
- fold-out notes
- old stains

The Index Orchid pollen or related plant residue activates one page. Gold lines crawl through the black.

A partial Girasol drawing appears: legs, antlers, body marks, with the internal glow not yet fully revealed.

### Suggested Captions

```text
The old book did not tell me where to go.
```

```text
It showed me what had survived being disbelieved.
```

### Page-Turn Hook

Thor sees the partial Girasol and is hooked.

---

## Pages 8-9: Fringe Supply Stop

### Function

Move Thor into the shady edge-world. Introduce Buckle and Gift-Pod.

### Image

Thor stops at a fringe port / low-road supply market outside a major route city.

Visual elements:

- maps under glass
- jars of glowing spores
- fuel filters
- black-market weather charms
- animal feed
- preserved wings
- wet boots
- bad coffee
- a vendor with too many names
- creature cages under tarps
- sign reading `NO REFUNDS ON LIVE GOODS`

Thor buys supplies.

He is offered the Gift-Pod.

Then he sees a tiny pale animal chained near a feed bin: the pygmy Pale Burdenback.

Buckle looks pathetic, expensive, and immediately emotionally illegal.

### Page-Turn Hook

Thor tries not to buy Buckle.

He fails.

---

## Page 10: Buckle Joins

### Function

Establish the White Elephant companion and show Thor's bad-good decision-making.

### Image

Buckle is now inside the crawler eating a pencil, sitting on the manuscript, and staring at the Gift-Pod.

The Gift-Pod rests in a jar, beautiful and wrong.

Thor writes field notes while pretending this was a practical choice.

### Suggested Field Note

```text
Pale Burdenback, pygmy variant.
Condition: underfed.
Temperament: expensive.
```

### Small Beat

Buckle bleats at the jar.

Thor ignores it.

---

## Page 11: First Warning

### Function

First horror/ecology warning beat.

### Image

Night. Thor sleeps in the crawler.

Heavy black panel sequence:

1. Gift-Pod shell sweating.
2. Buckle awake.
3. Lung Lichen sample glowing faintly in a tin.
4. Crawler walls creaking.
5. Root-like lines pressing against glass.
6. Thor's field notes fluttering open to the Girasol page.

No exposition.

The ashcan's first warning should be visual, not verbal.

### Color

Possible Lichen Green glow if using secondary survival color.

---

## Page 12: Back Cover / Girasol Omen

### Function

End with wonder, danger, and the White Whale.

### Image

Thor's crawler is tiny at the edge of a vast black landscape.

In the far sky-island clouds, a shape stands where no animal should stand.

Long legs.

Crystalline antlers.

No full reveal.

Just a faint gold scatter inside the fog.

### Text

```text
Some creatures are rare because they are dying.

Some are rare because they are good at surviving us.
```

Back-cover line:

```text
Observed, Not Owned.
```

# Ashcan Ratio Notes

This 12-page ashcan keeps Traverse / vehicle travel low. The crawler appears as:

- home
- preparation space
- supply container
- sleeping place
- visual icon

It does not become a route-operation plot.

Approximate balance:

| Story Material | Pages |
|---|---:|
| Cover / field-guide design | 2 |
| Ledger Falls / Thor setup | 2 |
| Vehicle and manuscript | 3 |
| Fringe supply stop | 2 |
| Buckle / Gift-Pod warning | 2 |
| Girasol omen | 1 |

---

# Ashcan Reader Promise

By the end of the ashcan, the reader should know:

- Thor is curious, capable, and morally unfinished.
- Buckle is trouble and heart.
- The manuscript is dangerous.
- The Gift-Pod is wrong.
- The field-guide format is part of the story.
- Meridian Prime has rare life beyond Traverse plots.
- The Girasol is real enough to haunt the page.
