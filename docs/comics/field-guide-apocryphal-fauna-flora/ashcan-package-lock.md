## Publication Package

This file locks the current proof-of-concept package for the modern ashcan.

```text
Observed, Not Owned
A 12-page modern ashcan explorer comic
Mostly black and white
Heavy black inks
Spot color system: A/B/C/D variants
Primary interior spot color: Girasol Gold
```

The ashcan is designed to prove the visual and emotional identity of a non-Traverse-centered Meridian Prime comic: a field-naturalist explorer story about Thor Fortner, rare ecology, apocryphal creatures, and the moral danger of documenting what survives by remaining hidden.

---

## Format Lock

**Primary format:** 12-page modern ashcan  
**Genre lane:** Explorer comic / ecological weird fiction / field-guide adventure  
**Art lane:** Heavy black inks / mostly black and white / selective spot color  
**Primary audience promise:** A visually bold creature-and-biome proof of concept that makes Meridian Prime feel bigger than the Traverse spine.

The ashcan should feel like:

- a field notebook found in a wet vehicle
- a punk naturalist pamphlet
- an underground explorer comic
- a printed warning
- a creature catalog that does not trust itself

---

## Narrative Lock

The ashcan follows **Thor Fortner**, a young field-naturalist from **Ledger Falls**, before the full expedition begins.

The ashcan should introduce:

- Ledger Falls as Thor's home context
- Thor's instinct to sketch and classify strange ecology
- the pressure between living things and official numbering systems
- the battered Sno-Cat-like field crawler
- the ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript / beast ledger
- the first hint of the Index Orchid
- the pygmy Pale Burdenback companion, Buckle
- the Gift-Pod as a future problem
- the Girasol as a distant omen, not a full reveal

The ashcan should not explain the entire story.

It should create desire.

---

## Traverse Limitation Lock

Thor's solo field vehicle may appear as transport, shelter, field office, kitchen, bunk, and mobile sketch desk.

However:

```text
Traverse / vehicle travel storyline: 12 percent maximum
```

The field crawler is Thor's moving home, not a route-operation plot machine.

The ashcan may include vehicle details for character, texture, and visual identity, but it must not become a Traverse story.

---

## Visual Lock

The ashcan should use:

- thick outer contour lines
- dense black spotting
- graphic silhouettes
- flat, bold limited color
- hard foreground branches and black organic forms
- high-contrast creature shapes
- field-guide annotations
- rough printed texture
- slightly damaged document feeling
- eerie dusk skies and gothic natural forms

Avoid glossy digital polish, cinematic realism, superhero anatomy, and generic monster-manual presentation.

Core visual line:

```text
Black ink does the storytelling. Color tells the truth only when the page earns it.
```

---

## Spot Color System

The ashcan may test four spot color options.

### Option A: Acid Magenta

Use for alien biology, manuscript weirdness, Hydropolis-adjacent signal strangeness, poisonous spores, or false beauty.

### Option B: Rust Red

Use for Ledger Falls industry, old warning paint, Craton mineral spaces, permit stamps, and field corrections.

### Option C: Lichen Green

Use for Lung Lichen, oxygen warning glow, spores, quiet survival cues, and hidden life in darkness.

### Option D: Girasol Gold

Use for Index Orchid pollen, hidden manuscript lines, Girasol foreshadowing, sacred discovery, crystalline antler glow, and final-page wonder.

**Primary interior recommendation:** Girasol Gold.  
**Secondary permitted micro-accent:** Lichen Green only when survival function requires it.

## Core Ashcan Thesis

Thor begins with this belief:

```text
If I document a creature, I save it.
```

The project is built to eventually overturn that belief:

```text
If I document it carelessly, I may be the first predator it could not hide from.
```

The ashcan should plant the seed of that reversal without stating the whole lesson too early.
