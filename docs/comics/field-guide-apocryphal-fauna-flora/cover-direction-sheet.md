# Cover Direction Sheet
## Observed, Not Owned
### A Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora

**Format:** Modern ashcan explorer comic  
**Visual Lane:** Heavy black inks, mostly black and white, selective spot color  
**Primary Spot Color:** Girasol Gold  
**Variant System:** A Acid Magenta / B Rust Red / C Lichen Green / D Girasol Gold  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox comic development only unless promoted through canon review

---

## 1. Cover Job

The cover must sell the ashcan in one image:

```text
A young field-naturalist, his strange pet, and a battered crawler stand at the edge of a living world that should not be fully mapped.
```

The cover should not feel like a polished sci-fi poster. It should feel like an artifact: printed, ink-heavy, field-worn, damp around the edges, and half-swallowed by the black ecology behind it.

Core promise:

```text
Explorer comic.
Creature mystery.
Heavy black ink.
Strange field guide.
Dangerous wonder.
```

---

## 2. Main Cover Concept

### Cover Title: The Tin Burrow at the Forest Edge

Thor Fortner stands beside **Mallow**, his compact Mirecat field crawler, at the edge of a black Ledger Falls cloud forest.

The crawler's scarred front blade is planted in mud, snow-crust, moss, and crystal grit. The rear hab module glows faintly from within. A tiny stove pipe tilts from the roof. The roof rack is cluttered with sample poles, tarps, storage tins, and patched field gear.

**Buckle** peers from the open rear hab doorway as a pale little shape against the black interior.

Thor stands slightly forward of the crawler, not heroic in a superhero way. He is small, wary, stubborn. He wears his dark flat cap with early motoring goggles pushed up on top, weathered jacket, scarf, boots, cross-body satchel, and holds the **Kuker-Rankin hand level** like a sight glass.

Behind them, the cloud forest forms a dense black wall.

At first glance, it is just branches and fog.

On second glance, the black branches form the hidden outline of crystalline antlers: the unseen **Girasol** haunting the composition.

Only the antler hint receives a trace of **Girasol Gold**.

## 3. Cover Composition

### Foreground

- Mud, roots, snow-crust, crystal grit
- Broken permit tag half-buried in muck
- Small Index Orchid bud near one crawler track
- Heavy black weeds crossing the bottom edge like ink scratches

### Midground

- Thor standing beside the front-left corner of the crawler
- Mallow angled three-quarters toward the reader
- Blade down, tracks visible, cab windows dark
- Rear hab door open with Buckle visible inside
- Thor's hand level catching a tiny spot-color glint

### Background

- Massive black tree wall
- Pale fog
- Distant vertical trunks
- Negative-space antler shape hidden in branches
- No full Girasol reveal

### Cover Read

The image should read in three beats:

1. Small explorer and strange vehicle
2. Pale animal in the doorway
3. Something huge implied in the trees

## 5. Thor Cover Direction

Thor should look like a self-made field explorer, not a soldier.

### Silhouette

- Dark flat cap / driving cap
- Early motoring goggles on top
- Short field jacket
- Scarf
- Cross-body satchel
- Tall boots
- Hand level in one hand
- Slight forward lean

### Pose

Thor is not triumphant. He is measuring, listening, and doubting.

Recommended pose:

```text
Thor stands with one boot on a root, hand level raised halfway, head turned slightly toward a sound in the trees.
```

His body language says:

```text
I came to find something.
Something may have found me first.
```

---

## 6. Buckle Cover Direction

Buckle should be visible enough to sell the emotional engine.

### Placement

Best placement: rear hab doorway.

### Shape

- Pale compact body
- Pygmy hippo / tapir / rhino calf blend
- Barrel body
- Flexible snout
- Small dark eyes
- Thick little legs
- Underdeveloped burden ridge

### Expression

Buckle should look concerned, curious, and mildly offended by the entire expedition.

He is not comic relief on the cover. He is the soft white counterweight to the black world.

Visual rule:

```text
Buckle is the brightest living shape on the cover.
```

---

## 7. Mallow / Tin Burrow Cover Direction

The crawler must feel iconic from the first cover.

### Exterior

- Compact Tucker Sno-Cat-inspired tracked vehicle
- Front blade scarred and low
- Rounded cab
- Rear hab module
- Roof rack
- Stove pipe
- Side storage boxes
- Muddy tracks
- Slightly handmade conversion look

### Mood

It should look beloved, battered, underfunded, and functional in exactly the wrong way.

The crawler is not sleek. It is a rolling field den.

Best visual phrase:

```text
A beetle wearing a cabin.
```

## 8. Logo and Typography

### Title

Primary title:

```text
OBSERVED, NOT OWNED
```

Recommended placement:

- Top third of cover
- Large, blocky, slightly distressed
- White or off-white letters against black tree mass
- Some letters lightly interrupted by branch shapes

### Subtitle

```text
A Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora
```

Placement:

- Under title, smaller
- Field-label style
- Could appear as if stamped or typed onto a specimen tag

### Creator / Imprint Space

Bottom edge or lower right:

```text
A Meridian Prime Ashcan
```

Optional false catalog text:

```text
APOCRYPHAL PLATE EDITION
LOCATION WITHHELD
```

## 9. Spot Color System

### Main Edition: D Girasol Gold

Use gold only for:

- hidden antler glint in branches
- hand level glass reflection
- tiny manuscript glow through hab window
- one or two field-note markings
- small Index Orchid pollen fleck

Gold must feel rare.

The cover should remain overwhelmingly black and white.

## 11. Back Cover Direction

### Image

A false field-guide fragment on black background.

A torn page shows:

- partial Girasol leg study
- Buckle hoof print
- redacted coordinates
- tiny Index Orchid diagram
- field stamp: LOCATION WITHHELD
- a broken line of Aeonolacertian manuscript script
- gold antler fragment crossing the corner

### Back Cover Copy

```text
Thor Fortner wanted to prove the old ledger was real.

A young field-naturalist from Ledger Falls, Thor leaves home in a battered crawler called Mallow, chasing impossible animals through cloud forest, crystal cave, swamp, fjord, and sky-island fog.

He carries an ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript, a beloved hand level, too much confidence, and one undergrown Pale Burdenback named Buckle who was supposed to become a pack animal and became something more expensive instead.

Some creatures are rare because they are dying.

Some are rare because they are good at surviving us.
```

Final stamp:

```text
OBSERVED.
NOT OWNED.
LOCATION WITHHELD.
```

## 14. Print Texture

The cover should feel like a boutique ashcan print.

Use:

- rough paper texture
- slightly misregistered spot color
- dry-brush blacks
- distressed title edges
- uneven ink fill
- field-stamp graphics
- fake catalog markings
- small scuffs, stains, and weathering

Avoid:

- glossy digital shine
- smooth airbrush
- cinematic poster lighting
- perfectly clean type
- full-color concept-art polish

## 17. Cover Rule

The cover should make the reader think:

```text
There is something enormous in that forest.
Thor thinks he is looking for it.
The forest already knows he is here.
```
