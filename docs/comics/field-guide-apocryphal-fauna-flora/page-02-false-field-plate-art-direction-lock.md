# Observed, Not Owned
## Page 02 Art Direction Lock: False Field Plate

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Format:** 12-page modern ashcan explorer comic  
**Page:** 02  
**Page Title:** False Field Plate  
**Status:** Sandbox Art Direction / Non-Canon / Requires Review Before Promotion  
**Production Purpose:** Establish the ashcan's field-guide visual language, nine-organism silhouette system, and spot-color logic.

---

## Core Image

A full-page false scientific plate titled:

```text
APOCRYPHAL PLATE I
Nine reported organisms from disputed Meridian Prime field accounts.
```

The page is a damaged field-guide insert: mostly black and white, heavy black inks, rough hand lettering, stamped labels, torn plate borders, redacted coordinates, tape marks, water damage, and selective spot color.

The page should feel like an artifact Thor Fortner will eventually make after the journey, even though it appears near the beginning of the ashcan.

Core mood:

```text
A field guide page that knows classification can become violence.
```

---

## Visual Reference Feel

Use the mood of the provided references through description, not direct copying:

- heavy black silhouettes
- flat graphic color
- gothic ruins and branch shapes
- violet-gray dusk fields
- organisms emerging from black negative space
- strange circular spots and biological marks
- spot colors that feel printed and dangerous
- bold figure-ground contrast
- rough comic ink, not polished concept art
- color behaving like a signal flare in darkness

Page 02 should feel like a naturalist plate drawn by someone who has spent too much time in caves, ports, and rain.

---

## Composition

Use a rough 3 x 3 plate grid.

Do not make it too clean.

The grid should look assembled from:

- old paper
- black gutters
- handwritten labels
- specimen tape
- stamp marks
- old field corrections
- redacted location bars
- torn or water-softened borders

The nine plate cells should not be perfectly equal. Let some silhouettes push against their frames.

The Girasol cell may break the top edge slightly with antlers.

Nightwake may spread across its box like a black stain.

Gift-Pod may look too elegant for the danger it carries.

---

## Ink Rules

Black carries the page.

Use black for:

- plate gutters
- creature silhouettes
- cave voids
- internal shadow masses
- under-leaf shapes
- redaction bars
- the Girasol's withheld-body mystery
- Nightwake's wings
- Snow-Dire Leafcat's false predator posture

Use white for:

- specimen paper
- silhouette cuts
- small eyes
- exposed bone-like linework
- lichen growths
- water damage holes
- annotation spaces

Line quality:

- thick outer contours
- scratchy field-study hatching
- rough white interior marks
- uneven creature outlines
- imperfect stamps
- hand-cut plate edges

Avoid:

- smooth digital polish
- realistic encyclopedia rendering
- clean museum-plate sterility
- shiny gradients
- too much decorative texture

---

## Spot Color Lock

Interior primary spot color:

```text
D: Girasol Gold
```

Use Girasol Gold for:

- Index Orchid pollen
- hidden manuscript-like glints around the plate
- faint Girasol antler scatter
- Thor's most important handwritten emphasis marks
- tiny guide-page registration marks

Optional alternate/variant cues:

- **A Acid Magenta:** manuscript weirdness / false beauty test
- **B Rust Red:** Craton mineral or old warning-stamp variant
- **C Lichen Green:** Lung Lichen survival-glow variant
- **D Girasol Gold:** main edition and preferred interior language

For the main edition, do not use all spot colors at once. If a second color is ever allowed, Lung Lichen Green should be the only candidate and should remain extremely limited.

## Organism Silhouette Directions

### 1. Index Orchid

Black-glass flower silhouette with sharp, layered petals. Gold pollen is the first real color event on the page. The flower should look botanical but slightly weaponlike.

### 2. Pygmy Pale Burdenback

Small pale shape, squat body, soft inconvenient body language, one expressive dark eye. It must read lovable without becoming a cartoon mascot.

### 3. Girasol

Partial okapi-like ungulate silhouette. Long legs, narrow head, crystalline antlers. Do not show full internal glow. Only faint gold scatter in antler edges.

Important rule:

```text
The Girasol reveal is not on Page 02. Page 02 only teaches the reader to look for it.
```

### 4. Crimson Glass Salamander

Soft translucent salamander / nudibranch shape curled on mineral stone. It should read wet, beautiful, and misleading. Keep the body elegant rather than monstrous.

### 5. Gift-Pod

Symmetrical floating seed pod in a jar or tied with string. Beautiful, charm-like, almost gift-shop precious. Tiny root-worm shadows inside should be barely visible.

### 6. Nightwake

Black-winged moth-bird / glider with wings nearly swallowing its box. Use white dots, eyespots, or mineral star-specks. It should look like night learned to migrate.

### 7. Lung Lichen

Pale branching growths like lungs, coral, and cave fungus. Glow nodes should be small and important. The organism should look quiet, not spectacular.

### 8. Dwarf Craton Sauropod

Dwarf Bajadasaurus-like sauropod silhouette, forward-curving neck spines, swamp steam obscuring the body. Treat as sacred animal presence, not monster bait.

### 9. Snow-Dire Leafcat

Large snow leopard / dire wolf-like silhouette with false predator posture. Reveal the herbivore twist visually by showing it with stripped plant matter or crystal bamboo.

## Text and Graphic Marks

Required top title:

```text
APOCRYPHAL PLATE I
Nine reported organisms from disputed Meridian Prime field accounts.
```

Required bottom note:

```text
Observed means seen.
It does not mean owned.
```

Optional footer:

```text
From the private working plates of Thor Fortner, Ledger Falls field-naturalist. Coordinates removed by author.
```

Recommended stamps:

```text
UNVERIFIED
MISCLASSIFIED
DO NOT MAP
LOCATION WITHHELD
SACRED, DO NOT TAG
DO NOT ACCEPT AS GIFT
PAPER TIGER
```

Use stamps sparingly. They should add pressure, not clutter.

## Page Design Rules

The page should be readable as:

1. a creature roster
2. an omen
3. a warning label
4. a design thesis for the ashcan

The reader should finish the page knowing:

- this is an explorer comic
- organisms are the story engine
- knowledge has consequences
- Thor will eventually have to choose what not to reveal

---

## Continuity and Spoiler Restraint

Do not reveal:

- the Girasol's final internal moving glow
- full sky-island location
- exact Craton sacred-site coordinates
- the full manuscript system
- any protected Meridian Prime mystery

This page is a promise, not an answer-box.

## Production Checkpoints

Before final art, confirm:

- all nine organisms are present
- Girasol is teased, not revealed
- Girasol Gold is the primary spot color
- Lung Lichen does not dominate the page
- Gift-Pod looks beautiful before dangerous
- Buckle / Pygmy Pale Burdenback looks animal-real, not plush mascot
- Snow-Dire Leafcat reads threatening first, herbivore second
- the page feels like a printed field artifact
- the final note `Observed means seen. It does not mean owned.` remains legible
