# Page 04 Art Direction Lock
## The Book Blooms Back
### Observed, Not Owned

**Status:** Sandbox Page Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Related Script:** `page-04-script.md`  
**Primary Visual Job:** Show the ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript reacting inside Mallow and giving Thor the first Index Orchid clue without decoding the book.

---

## Core Image

```text
Inside Mallow's cramped rear hab, Thor uses his Kuker-Rankin hand level as a sight glass over the ancient manuscript, causing hidden Girasol Gold lines to bloom around a black-glass orchid drawing while rain streaks down the windows.
```

The page should feel intimate, secretive, and slightly dangerous: a young field-naturalist alone in a tiny vehicle with a book that is less dead than paper should be.

---

## Page Mood

This is not a magic spell scene. It is a field-discovery scene that feels uncanny because the biology refuses to stay in the past.

Mood targets:

- cramped field lab
- rain-soaked isolation
- black ink interior shadows
- old paper and wet metal
- tiny gold reaction, not full illumination
- excitement curdling into unease
- a book that reveals only enough to create a problem

The page should feel like the first time the ashcan whispers back.

---

## Layout Direction

**Recommended Panel Count:** 5 panels  
**Reading Flow:** wide interior, close discovery, macro botanical clue, partial reveal, final field note  
**Visual Rhythm:** quiet / tiny glint / biological detail / hidden marks / decision

Panel architecture should tighten as the page progresses:

1. Hab interior establishes Thor alone with manuscript.
2. Lens / hand level catches the first gold thread.
3. Macro plant-dust detail reveals black-glass flower clue.
4. Partial gold annotation bloom, still unreadable.
5. Thor's field note converts mystery into objective.

## Thor Direction

Thor should look absorbed, damp, and under-rested.

Key visual cues:

- jacket still rain-dark at shoulders
- sleeves pushed back
- pencil behind ear or nearby
- flat cap hanging from hook with goggles on top
- hand level handled like a cherished instrument
- expression moves from skepticism to focus to alarmed curiosity

Thor is not triumphant on this page. He is hooked.

Body language:

```text
leaned too close to the page
one hand bracing paper
one hand holding the hand level
shoulders hunched because the hab is cramped
```

---

## Manuscript Direction

The manuscript should not look like a normal book.

Design qualities:

- thick uneven pages
- old biological diagrams
- saurian marginalia
- pressure-script-like notation
- fold lines and stains
- dense black ink shapes
- botanical geometry that almost becomes animal anatomy
- multiple annotation layers, mostly unreadable

The viewer should believe generations of hands touched this book, but nobody fully owned it.

Important rule:

```text
The manuscript should look smarter than Thor.
```

---

## Index Orchid Clue Direction

The plant clue should be partial and mysterious.

Do not show a fully rendered Index Orchid yet.

Instead show:

- black angular petals
- glass-like petal edges
- root lines that behave like index tabs
- a central aperture / eye-shaped mark
- dust or ancient pollen activating hidden marks

The reader should understand:

```text
This flower matters.
```

The reader should not yet understand:

```text
where it grows
what it fully does
why the manuscript needs it
```

---

## Girasol Gold Spot Color Rules

Gold appears only in tiny, earned marks:

- one hairline through the hand level lens
- pollen/dust flecks in Panel 3
- partial annotation fragments in Panel 4
- a final faint thread dying away near Thor's field note

Gold must not fill the room. It is not warm lamplight. It is hidden information briefly becoming visible.

Recommended visual metaphor:

```text
Gold behaves like a nerve firing inside dead paper.
```

---

## Lighting

Primary lighting:

- hard desk lamp cone
- deep surrounding blacks
- faint rain-gray window wash

Secondary lighting:

- minimal gold manuscript reaction

No broad cinematic glow. Keep the room small and dark.

Panel shadows should come from:

- desk lamp
- Thor's hunched body
- roof rack shadow through window
- hanging gear
- manuscript pages casting weird angular shapes

---

## Hab Interior Continuity

Use Mallow / Tin Burrow rules from `field-crawler-vehicle-sheet.md`:

- cramped fold-down desk
- tiny stove or stove shadow
- sample storage
- tool clutter
- manuscript wrapped in oilcloth
- field slips and pencil tins
- practical mobile lab, not polished sci-fi station

Buckle is not part of the expedition yet, so any Buckle-related space should either be absent or read as an empty low storage area that can later become his nest.

---

## Typography / Page Text Visuals

Visible manuscript fragments may appear in the art, but must remain partial and unreliable.

Permitted visible fragments:

```text
FIRST FIND THE INDEX
DO NOT MAP THE BLOOM
OBSERVED / NOT LOCATED
BLACK ROOT / GLASS VEIN / LOW WATER
```

Thor's field slip should be legible:

```text
FIRST OBJECTIVE:
BLACK-GLASS ORCHID?
```

Additional Thor notes may be semi-legible:

```text
wet archive?
mineral seam?
old permit station?
low-water flora?
```

Do not create a complete decoded paragraph.

---

## Black Ink Treatment

Use black ink heavily for:

- hab corners
- outside rain and window voids
- manuscript ink blocks
- desk shadow
- Thor's jacket folds
- hanging cap and goggles silhouette
- cramped roofline

White space should mostly come from:

- page surfaces
- Thor's hands
- lamp cone
- rain streaks
- field slip

Gold should be the only warm exception.

## Page Exit Image

Final visual beat:

```text
Thor's handwritten field slip sits beside the open manuscript: FIRST OBJECTIVE: BLACK-GLASS ORCHID? The gold thread has almost vanished. Outside the window, rain and branch reflections almost form antlers again.
```

The final panel should make the reader feel that Thor has gained an objective, but the book has kept the upper hand.

---

## Production Rule

The page should not solve anything. It should create motion.

The correct feeling is:

```text
Thor did not read the book.
The book recruited him.
```
