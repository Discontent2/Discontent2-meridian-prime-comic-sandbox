# Page 11 Art Direction Lock
## The Flag Found Someone

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 11  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Thor returns to the roadside orchid site and finds the black-glass orchid gone, his protective field flag bent into a beacon, and his own location note copied onto a more dangerous scrap.
```

Page 11 is the ethical wound page.

The page should not feel explosive. It should feel like a wet paper cut in the conscience.

---

## Page Mood

```text
quiet consequence
fieldwork guilt
missing life
rainy evidence
protection turned into invitation
first moral correction
```

This is not a villain reveal.

This is the moment Thor understands that careful data can still become a weapon.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in three stages:

1. Return to the site.
2. Discover the absence.
3. Redact the self.

The page should feel like Page 6 turned inside out.

Page 6: discovery.  
Page 7: marker.  
Page 11: consequence.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Return to Marker 17

Wide morning panel.

Mallow is parked near the Marker 17 roadside, not heroically but awkwardly, as if the vehicle itself knows this is a bad place to stop.

Required elements:

```text
Mallow near roadside
Marker 17 sign cluster in background
wet mist instead of heavy rain
black forest edge
roadside rut area visible but not yet revealed
```

Visual echo:

Use a similar road angle to Page 6 but colder, emptier, and more suspicious.

---

### Panel 2: Buckle Refuses

Medium rear-ramp panel.

Buckle stands halfway down Mallow's ramp, blocking Thor. He should be small but immovable.

Required Buckle posture:

```text
low body
stiff legs
ears forward
tense snout
head aimed toward the rut
```

Buckle should not look like a mascot. He should look like an animal refusing a bad smell.

Thor should be mid-irritation but visibly listening.

---

### Panel 3: Empty Rut

Low close panel.

The black-glass orchid is gone.

Required details:

```text
small dark hollow
mud disturbed by careful fingers or tool
threadlike root tears
black mineral water smear
one or two reflective petal chips
bent Thor field flag
wet tag reading OBSERVED / DO NOT COLLECT
```

The absence should be the loudest object in the panel.

Do not show gore, body horror, or melodrama. It is a missing plant, and that should be enough.

---

### Panel 4: Copied Scrap

Close-up panel of the copied note.

Required text:

```text
M17 + 1.7 LOW RUT
BLACK GLASS / LIVE ROOT
MARKED
```

Add a crude **Rust Red** price slash, inventory mark, or tally line at the bottom.

The paper should not look like Thor's paper. It should feel rougher, cheaper, and more transactional.

No full culprit.

Optional visual clue only:

```text
partial boot print
partial glove smear
dirty thumb mark
```

Keep all identity withheld.

---

### Panel 5: Compare the Notes

Medium close panel.

Thor kneels in mud with his original note in one hand and the copied scrap in the other.

Required visual:

```text
Thor's note and copied scrap visible together
matching distance detail
Thor's face partly shadowed under flat cap
Buckle behind him, still and watchful
petal fragments near knee
```

Thor should look less shocked than hollowed out.

This is the first time he sees his method as the problem.

---

### Panel 6: Pulling the Flag

Quiet emotional panel.

Thor pulls his own field flag out of the mud. Buckle presses his forehead into Thor's leg.

Required emotional read:

```text
Buckle grounds Thor
Thor's hand grips the wet tag
field flag loses authority
Thor looks smaller than he did on Page 6
```

This panel should be tender but not sentimental.

Buckle is not solving the plot. Buckle is keeping Thor present.

---

### Panel 7: Redaction in the Hab

Interior final panel.

Inside Mallow's cramped rear hab, Thor sits at the field desk. Buckle rests by the stove. The manuscript is closed. The field notebook is open.

Required visible revised note:

```text
INDEX ORCHID
Observed alive near low-water mineral margin.
Location: WITHHELD.
Collection: FORBIDDEN.
Public mark: NONE.
```

Thor should draw a heavy black bar through the location line or coordinate line.

The final panel should feel like the first true page of the ethical field guide.

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary spot color for this page:

```text
B Rust Red
```

Use Rust Red only for:

- the crude mark on the copied scrap
- a faint echo on the wet field flag tag if needed

Secondary optional spot color:

```text
D Girasol Gold
```

Use at most one tiny dead pollen fleck or petal glint. The near-absence of Girasol Gold matters because the living orchid is gone.

Do not use Lichen Green on this page unless a tiny carryover from Page 10 appears inside the hab. If used, keep it extremely faint.

No Acid Magenta.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
forest edge
Mallow undercarriage
muddy rut
Thor's cap shadow
redacted notebook bar
hab corners
empty plant hollow
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
wet paper
field tag
Buckle's pale body
torn root threads
rain mist
notebook page
```

The page must remain readable in pure black and white.

Spot color is punctuation, not decoration.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor should visually move from professional worry to moral recognition.

Visual language:

```text
field bag ready
kneeling in mud
papers compared
hands slower than before
face shadowed
notebook redaction deliberate
```

He is not angry enough yet. He is learning the shape of culpability.

### Buckle

Buckle's role is grounding and warning.

Visual language:

```text
refuses ramp
sniffs rut
holds still
presses forehead into Thor's leg
pale shape near stove in final panel
```

Buckle should be treated as animal-intelligent, not supernatural or humanlike.

### Mallow

Mallow should feel less like expedition confidence and more like shelter after error.

Visual language:

```text
awkward roadside stop
rear ramp down
wet panels
small hab light
cramped interior refuge
```

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
named culprit
full thief reveal
chase sequence
fight sequence
full Girasol
new major faction reveal
Buckle behaving like a human
supernatural map mechanics explained
clean heroic redemption
```

This page is about responsibility, not revenge.
