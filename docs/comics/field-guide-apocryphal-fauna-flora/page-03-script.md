# Script

## Panel 1

**Wide establishing panel.**

Behind a Ledger Falls supply counter / timber-margin yard, **Mallow** waits in heavy rain. The crawler is parked near stacked fuel tins, wet timber skids, mossy machinery, and a sagging tarp line. Its front blade rests in mud and snow-crust. The rear hab module is dark except for a faint interior lamp.

The cloud forest presses close behind the yard. Big black tree shapes lean over the little crawler.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`Mallow was not built for discovery.`

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`Mallow was built to push wet timber out of places it did not want to leave.`

---

## Panel 2

**Medium shot.**

Thor Fortner crosses the rain-slick yard toward Mallow, head down, dark flat cap pulled low, early motoring goggles perched on top. He carries an **oilcloth-wrapped bundle** under one arm: the ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript.

His other hand holds the strap of his satchel closed against the rain. His field jacket is already soaked at the shoulders.

In the background, a worker or supply clerk under shelter watches him go but says nothing.

**SFX:**  
`RAINRAINRAIN`

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`I bought her cheap because cheap is what they call broken things before someone loves them.`

---

## Panel 3

**Close-up detail panel on the crawler.**

The side of Mallow: hand-painted name **MALLOW** partly chipped and rain-streaked. An old Ledger Falls permit sticker is half-scraped off. Rust-red warning paint flakes from the blade bracket. Black mud cakes the tracks.

A small field mark or scratched symbol from Thor's personal system appears near the door.

**NO DIALOGUE.**

**OPTIONAL SMALL FIELD LABEL:**  
`MIRECAT FIELD CRAWLER / TIMBER-MARGIN SURPLUS / MODIFIED HAB UNIT`

---

## Panel 4

**Interior hab glimpse from just inside the open rear door.**

Thor climbs in or leans in with the bundle. We see the cramped interior for the first time:

- fold-down field desk
- tiny stove
- narrow bunk
- specimen drawers
- drying hooks
- empty corner where Buckle's future nest will be
- hanging mug
- pencil tins
- label rolls
- field plates clipped to the wall
- a warning tag: `DO NOT STORE SPORES`

The hab is organized but precarious, like a mind trying to look more orderly than it is.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`She had a desk, a stove, a bunk, and three leaks I considered nonfatal.`

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`That made her a laboratory by Ledger Falls standards.`

---

## Panel 5

**Final hook panel.**

Thor places the oilcloth bundle on the fold-down desk and unwraps it. The ancient manuscript is revealed just enough: strange biological diagrams, unreadable pressure-script, and an old page edge darkened by age.

A faint thread of **Girasol Gold** appears between two pages, not bright enough to explain itself. It feels more like a warning than magic.

Thor's hand hovers over the page. His Kuker-Rankin hand level lies nearby on the desk, catching a tiny reflection.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`The book did not tell me where to go.`

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**  
`It showed me what had survived being disbelieved.`
