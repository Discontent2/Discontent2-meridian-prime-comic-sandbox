# Page 07 Art Direction Lock
## A Flag Is Also a Beacon

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan Title:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 07  
**Status:** Production-Facing Art Direction Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Thor leaves a protective field flag beside the roadside black-glass orchid, but the flag makes the hidden organism easier to find, and an unseen hand copies his careful note into a more dangerous ledger.
```

Page 7 should feel like an ethical trap sprung by paper.

No monster. No chase. No villain reveal.

A field note becoming bait.

---

## Page Mood

```text
quiet consequence
paper dread
wet ink betrayal
good intention becoming exposure
attention as predator
```

The page should feel more unsettling after the reader thinks about it. Thor does the gentle thing. The gentle thing still leaves a trail.

---

## Composition Goals

The visual read should move in four stages:

1. Thor makes a careful protective marker.
2. The marker is more visible than the orchid.
3. Someone else reads and copies the information.
4. Thor moves forward unaware that his note now travels without him.

---

## Panel Direction

### Panel 1: Thor Makes the Flag

Medium close panel.

Thor kneels beside the black-glass orchid in roadside mud. He ties a small waterproof field flag to a twig or thin survey stake near the plant, but not touching it.

Required elements:

```text
Thor kneeling in rain
black-glass orchid in rut
small waterproof flag
waterproof field paper
Kuker-Rankin hand level visible near his knee or satchel
Mallow track nearby but safely stopped away from flower
```

Visible tag text:

```text
OBSERVED
DO NOT COLLECT
```

Tiny location shorthand:

```text
M17 + 1.7 / LOW RUT
```

The shorthand should look partly scratched out.

---

### Panel 2: Marker Left Behind

Wide panel.

Mallow crawls away down the wet road. The field flag remains in the foreground beside the orchid.

Important visual contrast:

```text
field flag = pale, readable, exposed
orchid = dark, tiny, hidden
```

This panel must make the reader understand that the marker is more dangerous than the machine.

Mallow should be a black silhouette fading into rain, not a heroic departure shot.

---

### Panel 3: Rain Rewrites the Warning

Close-up of the field flag.

Rainwater and mud distort the text. The intended warning should still be present, but the composition should isolate the dangerous parts.

Readable fragments:

```text
COLLECT
M17 + 1.7
LOW RUT
```

Use tiny **Girasol Gold** flecks where pollen sticks to the wet paper.

Do not make this look like magic lettering. It should be practical misreading, bad weather, and bad luck.

---

### Panel 4: Unseen Hand

Low silent panel.

A boot enters the mud. A gloved hand lifts the flag.

Do not show the face, body identity, faction insignia, or obvious villain styling.

Required elements:

```text
anonymous boot
anonymous glove
field flag being lifted
orchid still in rut
mud displacement
rain streaks
```

Keep this panel quiet. The violence is informational.

---

### Panel 5: Copied Note

Close detail panel.

The information from Thor's flag is copied into another document. This can be a wet notebook, ledger scrap, sale card, route-board note, or hand-held field slip.

Visible copied text:

```text
BLACK GLASS ORCHID?
M17 + 1.7 / LOW RUT
LIVE ROOT MAY HOLD VALUE
```

Visual requirements:

```text
no face visible
rougher handwriting than Thor's
crude sketch of black-glass orchid
one transferred Girasol Gold pollen fleck
```

The copied page should feel uglier and more predatory than Thor's note.

---

### Panel 6: Thor Unaware

Final wide panel.

Mallow rolls toward distant fringe-stop lights. Thor is visible through the cab window, still writing in his own field notebook.

Behind him, the road falls into black. The original field flag is gone.

Required emotional read:

```text
Thor thinks he is leaving no harm behind.
The reader knows the note has learned to travel.
```

The fringe-stop lights can quietly set up Page 8.

---

## Spot Color Rules

Primary page spot color:

```text
D Girasol Gold
```

Use extremely sparingly:

- pollen flecks on wet field flag
- one transferred fleck on copied note
- maybe a tiny glint on the orchid itself

No Acid Magenta.

No Lichen Green.

Optional Rust Red only if a later revision wants a tiny ledger stamp, but the preferred version uses no Rust Red.

---

## Ink Rules

Heavy black should dominate:

```text
roadside mud
Mallow silhouette
anonymous boot / glove
final road behind Thor
underside of rain clouds
```

White / negative space should carry:

```text
field flag
wet paper
rain streaks
small foreground orchid highlights
fringe-stop lights
```

The field flag should become the visual villain of the page: pale, simple, readable, wrong.

---

## Character Direction

### Thor

Thor should appear careful and morally earnest.

Visual language:

```text
gentle hands
precise field lettering
muddy knees
protective posture
no awareness of downstream consequence
```

Do not make him look foolish. Make him look sincere and incomplete.

### Mallow

Mallow is present as departure silhouette and mobile shelter, not action machine.

Visual language:

```text
black block in rain
track marks leading away
small cab window
low blade
underfunded field rig shape
```

### Anonymous Reader

The person who takes or copies the marker must remain unidentified.

Visual language:

```text
boot only
glove only
hands only
no face
no faction markings
no dramatic villain posture
```

The reader should feel that this could be anyone hungry enough to turn knowledge into trade.

### Buckle

Buckle is not present on Page 7.

Do not include Buckle, a pale animal silhouette, feed sacks, or animal-market imagery yet unless Page 8 is being visually teased in the far distance only.

## Avoid

Do not show:

```text
Buckle
Buzz-Mule / e-bike
full Girasol
combat
chase action
clear villain identity
faction logos
explained manuscript mechanics
magic text transformation
bright full-color botanical glow
```

Heavy-black-ink modern ashcan comic page, mostly black and white with only tiny Girasol Gold pollen flecks, showing Thor Fortner kneeling in rain beside a tiny roadside black-glass orchid and leaving a protective field flag marked OBSERVED / DO NOT COLLECT, then Mallow rolling away as rain exposes the location shorthand on the tag, an anonymous gloved hand lifts the marker, the information is copied into a rougher ledger note reading BLACK GLASS ORCHID / LIVE ROOT MAY HOLD VALUE, and Thor drives toward distant fringe-stop lights unaware that his field note has become bait.
