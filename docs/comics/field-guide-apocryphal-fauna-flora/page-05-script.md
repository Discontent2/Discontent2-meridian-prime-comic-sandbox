## Page Function

Page 5 is the first practical decision page.

Thor has seen the manuscript react. He does not fully understand it, but he now has enough to act: the first objective is the **Index Orchid**, a black-glass orchid connected to hidden manuscript annotations, bad records, and places where classification has gone wrong.

The page should move from interior obsession to exterior departure.

Core page movement:

```text
Hidden clue -> field plan -> vehicle prep -> leaving Ledger Falls
```

---

## Visual / Emotional Goal

The page should feel like Thor is making the wrong kind of right decision.

He is excited. He is scared. He is underprepared. He is trying to make a sacred, strange, half-living manuscript behave like a field assignment.

This is not yet wisdom. This is momentum.

---

# Comic Script

## Panel 1

**Interior, Mallow hab module. Close-up of the manuscript page.**

The black-glass orchid drawing from Page 4 remains visible. A thin **Girasol Gold** thread moves from the flower's root system into a marginal note written in ancient Aeonolacertian pressure-script.

Thor's pencil enters frame, not touching the gold line, circling the orchid symbol on a separate scrap of paper.

Handwritten Thor note on scrap:

```text
INDEX ORCHID?
BLACK-GLASS BLOOM
LOW WATER / BAD RECORDS / NOT FOREST?
```

Small field stamp nearby:

```text
UNVERIFIED
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**

```text
The book did not give me a route.
```

---

## Panel 2

**Interior, hab desk. Medium shot.**

Thor has pinned three things to the wall over the fold-down desk:

1. a crude Ledger Falls map,
2. a copied symbol from the manuscript,
3. a blank field plate labeled `PLATE I: INDEX ORCHID`.

He holds the Kuker-Rankin hand level in one hand and a pencil in the other, trying to align the manuscript clue with a real-world map. The old hand level is being used as an absurd, beloved sight glass.

The hab is still organized. No Buckle yet. There is an empty floor corner where Buckle will eventually nest.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**

```text
It gave me a disagreement.
```

**THOR, muttering:**

```text
Plants do not bloom in paperwork.
```

Beat.

**THOR, smaller:**

```text
Unless the paperwork is where something died.
```

---

## Panel 3

**Exterior, rainy Ledger Falls timber-margin yard. Loading montage panel.**

Thor moves between the supply shed and Mallow. He loads fuel tins, waterproof paper, ration crates, spare filters, field plates, sample tubes, and a wrapped plant press.

Mallow's front blade sits low in the mud. Its tracks are black with Ledger Falls grit. The roof rack is already too full.

A worker or supply-yard voice calls from off-panel.

**OFF-PANEL VOICE:**

```text
Fortner! Road's mud after the mill line.
```

**THOR:**

```text
So is everything honest.
```

---

## Panel 4

**Interior cab of Mallow. Tight panel.**

Thor sits in the driver seat. Rain streaks the windshield. His dark flat cap is damp; the early motoring goggles rest on top. The hand level is tucked safely into an inner pocket or side case.

Dashboard details:

- old pressure gauge,
- heater switch labeled `LIE / WORSE / OFF`,
- route lamp,
- cracked radio,
- hand-painted tag: `MALLOW`,
- glove box sticker: `DO NOT STORE SPORES`.

Thor turns the ignition.

**SFX:**

```text
KRR-KRR-KRR
```

The engine catches badly.

**SFX:**

```text
THOOM. CHUG. CHUG. CHUG.
```

**THOR:**

```text
Come on, Mallow. Be ugly and brave.
```

---

## Panel 5

**Wide exterior. Ledger Falls edge.**

Mallow leaves the timber-margin yard and rolls toward the black cloud forest road. The front blade pushes rainwater aside. Behind the crawler, Ledger Falls is a stack of wet roofs, mill lights, permit boards, logging cables, and fog.

Ahead, the road narrows into dark trees. A tiny hint of **Girasol Gold** marks the route scrap taped inside the cab window, barely visible through rain.

No Buckle yet. No e-bike yet. This is the lonely version of the expedition.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**

```text
I told myself I was leaving for a plant.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**

```text
That was the first lie.
```
