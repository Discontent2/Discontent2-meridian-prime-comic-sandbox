# Observed, Not Owned
## Page 02 Script: False Field Plate

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Format:** 12-page modern ashcan explorer comic  
**Page:** 02  
**Page Title:** False Field Plate  
**Status:** Sandbox Script / Non-Canon / Requires Review Before Promotion  
**Production Purpose:** Establish the field-guide format, reveal the nine apocryphal motif-organisms, and create a visual promise for the full expedition before returning to Thor's grounded Ledger Falls story.

---

## Page Function

Page 02 is not a normal scene.

It is a false scientific plate from Thor Fortner's future field guide, presented as a found page, proof sheet, or damaged insert.

The reader should feel that they are looking at an artifact from a field guide that does not fully trust itself.

This page introduces the creature and plant spine of the ashcan:

1. Index Orchid
2. Pygmy Pale Burdenback
3. Girasol
4. Crimson Glass Salamander
5. Gift-Pod
6. Nightwake
7. Lung Lichen
8. Dwarf Craton Sauropod
9. Snow-Dire Leafcat

---

## Page Layout

**Full-page plate.**

A rough 3 x 3 field-plate grid, but not cleanly mechanical. The grid should feel hand-built, slightly crooked, and partly damaged by ink, moisture, field tape, and black botanical shadows.

The nine organisms appear as silhouettes, partial sketches, or incomplete plates.

Some are fully drawn enough to intrigue the reader. Others are only dark shapes, wrong outlines, or redacted contours.

The page should read as both:

- a naturalist reference plate
- a warning page

## Panel / Plate Breakdown

### Plate 1: Index Orchid

**Image:** A black-glass orchid with too-sharp petals. Its pollen is rendered in the primary spot color, Girasol Gold. Tiny gold dust crawls toward the plate border like hidden text trying to escape.

**Label:**

```text
INDEX ORCHID
Hidden index catalyst / suspected manuscript-reactive pollen
STATUS: UNVERIFIED BUT REPEATED
```

**Margin Note:**

```text
Do not inhale. Do not catalog near open books.
```

---

### Plate 2: Pygmy Pale Burdenback

**Image:** A small pale pack-beast silhouette, squat, soft, inconveniently adorable, standing beside a scale ruler it has knocked sideways. One dark eye looks toward the reader.

**Label:**

```text
PYGMY PALE BURDENBACK
Wet-margin pack-beast variant
STATUS: MISCLASSIFIED COMPANION ANIMAL
```

**Margin Note:**

```text
Expense exceeds carrying capacity.
```

---

### Plate 3: Girasol

**Image:** Only a partial silhouette: long legs, okapi-like body, narrow head, crystalline antlers cut off by the top of the plate. Do not show the internal moving glow yet. Only a faint gold scatter appears inside the antler tips.

**Label:**

```text
GIRASOL
Sky-island ungulate / crystal-antlered light-scatterer
STATUS: OBSERVED
LOCATION: WITHHELD
```

**Margin Note:**

```text
Do not map.
```

---

### Plate 4: Crimson Glass Salamander

**Image:** A translucent salamander / nudibranch form curled around a cave stone. Body should look like wet glass and soft danger. Use black and white first; optional rust-red internal refraction may be noted for later color tests.

**Label:**

```text
CRIMSON GLASS SALAMANDER
Cave-edge amphibious mineral grazer
STATUS: BEAUTIFUL, MISLEADING
```

**Margin Note:**

```text
Not proof of Girasol range.
```

**Image:** A beautiful floating seed pod, elegant and symmetrical, with a faint seam. The pod is shown in a jar or tied with string, as if sold as a charm. Tiny root-worm shapes are barely visible inside.

**Label:**

```text
GIFT-POD
Dormant invasive seed vessel / charm-market vector
STATUS: DO NOT ACCEPT AS GIFT
```

**Margin Note:**

```text
Kindness can travel with teeth.
```

### Plate 6: Nightwake

**Image:** A black-winged moth-bird / glider silhouette spanning its box. Wings contain small white eye spots or star-like mineral dust. The body is almost swallowed by black.

## Lettering Notes

- Use mixed field-guide labels, stamp language, and handwritten marginalia.
- Labels should be readable but not sterile.
- Redaction marks may cross out coordinates and old names.
- Thor's handwriting should feel quick, practical, slightly crowded, and human.
- Do not overload the page with prose. The plate must remain visual-first.

---

## Continuity Notes

- The Girasol's final internal light-tracking reveal is reserved for the end of the story.
- This page may tease the Girasol with faint gold scatter but must not explain it.
- The dwarf Craton sauropod must not be framed as an unevolved Aeonolacertian. It is an animal lineage that never became Aeonolacertian personhood.
- Lung Lichen should be introduced as a warning organism and possible survival aid, not a real-world medical instruction.
- Gift-Pod must read beautiful before it reads dangerous.

---

## Page Turn

Page 02 ends with the reader understanding that this comic is a creature ledger.

Page 03 returns to Ledger Falls, rain, permit tags, and Thor before he knows how much the guide will cost him.
