# Observed, Not Owned - Page 1 Art Direction Lock

**Project:** Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora  
**Ashcan:** Observed, Not Owned  
**Page:** 1  
**Status:** Sandbox Page Art Direction Lock / v0.1  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Production Purpose:** Establish the visual grammar for the ashcan: heavy black inks, Ledger Falls ecology, field-guide captions, and Thor's noticing eye.

---

## Page Title

**The Forest Becomes Cargo**

---

## Page Job

Page 1 must prove the ashcan's identity immediately.

It should communicate:

- this is an explorer comic, not a Traverse comic
- Thor Fortner is a field-naturalist from Ledger Falls
- Ledger Falls turns living forest into tagged resource
- Thor notices small, strange life that others ignore
- the comic's heavy-black-ink style can carry mood, ecology, and danger
- the Girasol exists as omen, not exposition

---

## Reference Feel Translation

The provided visual references suggest the following production feeling:

- heavy black silhouettes
- flat limited color
- gothic branches against violet dusk
- ruined or industrial architecture swallowed by organic forms
- creatures emerging from black fields and negative space
- color used violently and sparingly
- snow, stone, tentacles, windows, and strange organisms reduced to bold graphic shapes
- printed-comic grit rather than smooth rendering

This page should borrow the feel without copying any composition or character.

Core translation:

```text
Ledger Falls should feel like a working town built inside a haunted woodcut.
```

---

## Overall Palette

**Base:** black and white  
**Tonal support:** cool gray / blue-gray / violet-gray dusk  
**Primary spot color:** Girasol Gold, if using the main edition palette  
**Variant spot options:** Acid Magenta, Rust Red, Lichen Green, Girasol Gold

Page 1 should remain mostly black-and-white even in color variants.

Color must appear as a controlled signal, not full atmospheric painting.

---

## Page Composition

Recommended page shape:

1. Wide establishing panel, heavy black forest and town silhouettes
2. Medium work-culture panel with tags and permit marks
3. Low-angle Thor discovery panel
4. Close-up sketchbook / tiny organism panel
5. Bottom omen panel with hidden antler shape in branches

The page should move from public industry to private noticing.

Top of page: the town counts.  
Middle of page: Thor notices.  
Bottom of page: the forest watches back.

---

## Panel 1 Art Direction

**Wide establishing shot of Ledger Falls in rain.**

Visual elements:

- black cloud forest wall
- logging cables through fog
- worker platforms or stilts
- wet boards
- hauler lights
- permit boards
- mill steam
- rain as white scratches through black mass
- trees towering beyond human structures
- town lights rendered as small hard shapes, not soft glow

Composition:

Make the forest larger than the town. The town should feel busy but temporary.

Ink:

Use massive black tree shapes and black roof / gantry silhouettes. Let rain and mist cut white seams through the darkness.

Spot color:

Very minimal. If using Girasol Gold, reserve it for one or two distant windows or field-tag glints. Do not spend the gold too early.

---

## Panel 2 Art Direction

**Workers tagging trees.**

Visual elements:

- close tree bark texture
- wet permit ribbon
- ugly number tag
- clipboard or field board
- work gloves
- rain beads
- tiny moss life ignored by workers

Composition:

Let the permit number dominate the living bark, but keep small organisms visible along the edge.

Ink:

Heavy black bark shadows, thick glove shapes, hard tag silhouette.

Spot color:

Rust Red works especially well for permit ribbon / stamp variants. In the main Girasol Gold edition, keep this mostly monochrome, or use a dead muted mark so gold remains special.

---

## Panel 3 Art Direction

**Thor Fortner crouched near tiny organism.**

Thor should be dwarfed by work culture behind him.

Visual elements:

- Thor crouching in mud
- sketchbook and pencil
- old field lens
- practical field jacket
- boots with mud crust
- workers in silhouette behind him
- old permit-stamp machine / archive outbuilding / field counter
- tiny organism growing from a crack

Thor design notes:

- young but competent
- practical, muddy, under-rested
- no superhero posture
- more comfortable looking at a plant than facing people
- local Ledger Falls field-naturalist energy

Ink:

Use Thor's outline clearly against black background. He should be readable by silhouette: crouched body, satchel, sketchbook, hood/cap, field lens.

Spot color:

The tiny organism may receive the page's first meaningful color mark.

---

## Panel 4 Art Direction

**Close-up of Thor's sketchbook.**

This panel establishes the field-guide design system.

Visual elements:

- pencil drawing of tiny organism
- handwritten notes
- partial labels
- mud thumbprint
- rain drop distortion
- specimen tag corner
- maybe a stamped number bleeding through from a previous page

Style:

The drawing should feel more delicate than the world around it. Thor's sketch line can be finer, scratchier, and more observant.

Spot color:

This is the best controlled color moment on Page 1.

Variant behavior:

- Acid Magenta: makes the organism feel alien and suspect
- Rust Red: ties organism to permit machinery and industrial warning
- Lichen Green: suggests survival biology / oxygen-warning ecology
- Girasol Gold: suggests Index Orchid / hidden manuscript destiny

Main edition recommendation: tiny Girasol Gold pollen dot or petal seam.

---

## Panel 5 Art Direction

**Bottom omen panel.**

Thor looks up from the organism toward the forest.

Visual elements:

- black tree branches
- rain and fog
- permit ribbon across foreground
- distant forest mass
- hidden antler shape made from branches
- Thor small in the frame or seen in partial profile

Composition:

The antler shape must be deniable. It should reward a second look but not read as a literal creature reveal.

Ink:

This should be the blackest panel on the page. Use negative space and branch silhouettes to create unease.

Spot color:

One small accent only. Possible uses:

- Girasol Gold: tiny glint in the antler-shaped branch geometry
- Lichen Green: faint biological speck near Thor's discovered organism
- Rust Red: permit ribbon as a slash across the frame
- Acid Magenta: eerie plant mark in the forest darkness

Do not reveal the Girasol.

---

## Thor Fortner Page 1 Visual Rules

Thor must not look like an action hero.

He should look like:

- a local field kid with good eyes
- a mud-boot naturalist
- a practical illustrator
- someone who has spent more money on pencils, filters, and field tins than clothing
- someone already carrying the emotional flaw of the book: he believes noticing is automatically noble

Avoid:

- heroic cape-like coat
- sleek sci-fi explorer armor
- military posture
- generic fantasy ranger styling
- too-clean outfit

---

## Ledger Falls Visual Rules

Ledger Falls should feel like:

- cloud rainforest becoming cargo
- damp labor politics
- permit tags in living bark
- logging machinery swallowed by moss and rain
- workers who are not villains, just tired people inside a system
- wet platforms, haulers, steam, sawdust, and green-black fog

Avoid making Ledger Falls generic lumber-town wallpaper.

It should feel specific to Meridian Prime: wet, industrial, bureaucratic, ecological, and morally knotted.

---

## Field-Guide Graphic System

Page 1 begins the caption system.

Use captions as private field notes, not omniscient narration.

Suggested styling:

```text
FORTNER FIELD NOTE / LEDGER FALLS / RAIN SHIFT
```

Do not overuse labels on Page 1. Let the reader feel the field-guide mode without drowning in design furniture.

---

## Lettering and Sound

Minimal lettering.

Use rain and distant machinery visually rather than loading the page with sound effects.

Possible small SFX:

- rain tick marks
- distant mill thrum
- wet tag slap

Keep SFX graphic and integrated into black shapes.

---

## Continuity Protection

Do not show:

- ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript
- Buckle
- Gift-Pod
- Girasol full body
- sky islands
- Red Glass Geodeum
- Craton swamp
- any Traverse convoy or major route operation

Page 1 is Ledger Falls, Thor, and the first tiny signal that something apocryphal lives inside ordinary workday mud.

---

## Success Criteria

Page 1 succeeds if the reader understands:

1. Thor sees what others miss.
2. Ledger Falls classifies life before understanding it.
3. The forest is not passive scenery.
4. The comic is visually bold and ink-driven.
5. The story is about discovery becoming morally dangerous.

---

## One-Image Summary

Thor Fortner crouches in Ledger Falls rain, sketching a tiny strange organism growing out of a permit-stamp machine while workers tag trees behind him and black branches form a hidden antler shape in the fog.
