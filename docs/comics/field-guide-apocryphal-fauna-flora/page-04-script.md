# Page 04 Script
## Observed, Not Owned
### A Field Guide to Apocryphal Meridian Prime Fauna and Flora

**Page Title:** The Book Blooms Back  
**Status:** Sandbox Comic Script / Page 04 Draft  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Page Function:** The ancient Aeonolacertian manuscript reacts inside Mallow and gives Thor the first Index Orchid clue without decoding the book or explaining the larger mystery.

---

## Page Intent

Page 4 should turn Page 3's faint Girasol Gold glimmer into the first real story hook:

```text
The manuscript is not readable by study alone.
Something living is required to read it.
```

Thor should not learn a route, a full translation, or a true map. He should learn only enough to pursue the next clue: a black-glass orchid / Index Orchid symbol connected to hidden annotations.

---

## Page Layout

**Recommended Panel Count:** 5 panels  
**Page Shape:** Interior hab sequence, claustrophobic and rain-soaked  
**Color Rule:** Mostly black and white; Girasol Gold appears only as hidden manuscript reaction / pollen-thread glimmer  
**Lettering Load:** Light to moderate. Let the page breathe.

---

# Panel 1

**Wide interior panel.**

Inside Mallow's rear hab module at night. Rain drums on the roof. The tiny stove is dark or barely warm. The fold-down field desk is cluttered with pencil tins, folded field slips, a dented mug, sample paper, and the oilcloth-wrapped ancient manuscript.

Thor sits hunched at the desk, still damp from the Ledger Falls rain. His dark flat cap hangs from a hook with the early motoring goggles perched on top. His cross-body satchel is slung over a chair. The Kuker-Rankin hand level rests beside the manuscript like a sacred little instrument.

The hab should feel too small for ambition.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I owned three translations of old Aeonolacertian field script.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
The book used none of them.
```

---

# Panel 2

**Close panel.**

Thor opens the manuscript under the desk lamp. The page is dense with impossible biological diagrams: vine spirals, creature limbs, saurian marginalia, not-quite-map geometry, and black ink so old it seems bruised into the paper.

Thor holds the Kuker-Rankin hand level over the page, using it like a sight glass. Through the glass, a thin hidden line catches light.

A single hairline thread of **Girasol Gold** appears under the lens.

**THOR, small:**
```text
No.
```

**THOR, smaller:**
```text
Wait.
```

**SFX, tiny:**
```text
tik
```

# Panel 3

**Macro panel / field-detail panel.**

A crease in the manuscript binding holds ancient powder: maybe pollen, maybe mineral dust, maybe something once alive. As Thor tilts the page, the dust slides across a black botanical drawing.

The drawing resolves into a partial black-glass flower shape: angular petals, root-like indexing lines, and a central mark shaped like an eye or aperture.

This is the first Index Orchid clue, but the page should not name it plainly yet.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Not ink.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Not quite pollen.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
Something the page remembered after the plant was gone.
```

---

# Panel 4

**Medium panel.**

Thor presses the page flat with one hand and raises the hand level with the other. Hidden marks bloom in short incomplete gold fragments around the black flower drawing. The marks do not translate cleanly. They look like an index, not directions.

Visible fragments should be partial, damaged, and ambiguous.

Suggested visible fragments:

```text
FIRST FIND THE INDEX
```

```text
DO NOT MAP THE BLOOM
```

```text
OBSERVED / NOT LOCATED
```

```text
BLACK ROOT / GLASS VEIN / LOW WATER
```

Thor's expression changes from triumph to confusion. The book is not giving him the world. It is giving him a locked door and a dare.

**THOR:**
```text
An index.
```

**THOR:**
```text
To a book that won't let itself be read.
```

---

# Panel 5

**Tall final panel.**

Thor has pinned a blank field slip beside the manuscript. He writes quickly, excited and unsettled. On the field slip, in Thor's hand:

```text
FIRST OBJECTIVE:
BLACK-GLASS ORCHID?
```

Below that, messier:

```text
wet archive?
mineral seam?
old permit station?
low-water flora?
```

The manuscript lies open beside the note. The gold thread has faded to almost nothing. Outside the tiny hab window, Ledger Falls rain streaks down black glass. In the far reflection of the window, branches make an almost-antler shape again, deniable and faint.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
The book did not open.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
It pointed.
```

## Page 4 Exit State

By the end of Page 4:

- Thor knows the manuscript requires a living or formerly living botanical key.
- The reader sees the first clue toward the Index Orchid.
- The manuscript remains mostly unreadable.
- No route, hidden formation, Core truth, Dry Port truth, gateway mechanics, or larger cosmology is revealed.
- Page 5 can move into expedition preparation / crawler departure / first supply need.

---

## Canon Safety

This page should not define ancient Aeonolacertian science, explain manuscript origin, reveal Girasol truth, solve protected mysteries, or turn the manuscript into a reliable universal map.

The book is a lure, warning, index, and character-pressure object. It is not an answer-box.
