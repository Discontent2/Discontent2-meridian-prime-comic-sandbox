## Page Function

Page 11 brings back the consequence seeded on Page 7.

Thor did not collect the roadside black-glass orchid. He did something that felt cleaner, safer, and more professional: he marked it, described it, and made it findable.

Core story beat:

```text
Thor returns to the roadside orchid site and discovers that his protective field flag has become a beacon.
The orchid is gone.
Someone copied his note.
Thor understands that observation can become extraction when recorded carelessly.
```

This page should sting quietly. No chase. No fight. No villain reveal.

The damage is already done.

## Panel Count

Recommended: **7 panels**

Suggested layout:

```text
Panel 1: Mallow returns to Marker 17 road in cold morning rain
Panel 2: Buckle refuses to leave the ramp
Panel 3: empty rut where the orchid was
Panel 4: copied field note / dangerous ledger slip
Panel 5: Thor compares the copied note to his own words
Panel 6: Buckle presses against Thor while Thor removes his marker
Panel 7: Thor begins redacting his own map
```

## Script

### Panel 1

**Wide morning panel.**

Mallow sits near the Marker 17 roadside again. The rain has thinned to a cold mist. The forest is still black, but quieter than before. The road signs from Page 6 are visible in the background, softer now, half-swallowed by wet branches.

Thor has returned because Buckle refused the culvert and because the Lung Lichen warning left him uneasy.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I told myself I was returning to verify conditions.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
That is what field men call worry when they want it to sound employed.
```

---

### Panel 2

**Medium panel at Mallow's rear ramp.**

Buckle stands halfway down the ramp and refuses to go farther. His body is low, stubborn, and tense. His tiny ears are forward. He sniffs toward the roadside rut and makes a sharp warning chirp.

Thor stands beside him, field bag over one shoulder, irritated but listening now.

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
kweeh
```

**THOR:**
```text
Buckley, we are not conducting policy by snout.
```

Small beat.

**THOR:**
```text
Although lately the snout has been winning.
```

---

### Panel 3

**Low close panel.**

The muddy rut from Page 6. The black-glass orchid is gone.

Only a small dark hollow remains, with torn threadlike roots, disturbed mud, a few reflective petal chips, and a smear of black mineral water.

Thor's little field flag is still there, bent sideways, too visible.

The tag reads:

```text
OBSERVED / DO NOT COLLECT
```

But the tag now feels like an invitation someone misunderstood on purpose.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I had not taken it.
```

---

### Panel 4

**Close-up panel.**

Thor finds a torn scrap pinned under the field flag stake or caught in the mud. It is not his paper, but it copies his information.

The copied note reads:

```text
M17 + 1.7 LOW RUT
BLACK GLASS / LIVE ROOT
MARKED
```

A crude price mark or inventory slash crosses the bottom in **Rust Red**.

Do not show a full culprit. Only evidence.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
I had shown the world where to put its hand.
```

---

### Panel 5

**Medium close panel.**

Thor kneels in mud with two pieces of paper: his original field note and the copied scrap. His words match too closely. His face is lit by pale rain and the tiny black shine of petal fragments.

Buckle stands behind him, very still.

Visible on Thor's original note:

```text
approx. 1.7 mi past Marker 17
DO NOT COLLECT
```

Visible on copied scrap:

```text
M17 + 1.7 LOW RUT
MARKED
```

**THOR:**
```text
No.
```

**THOR:**
```text
That was a warning.
```

No answer. Just rain.

---

### Panel 6

**Quiet panel.**

Thor pulls up his own field flag. Buckle presses his forehead into Thor's leg. This should not be cute first. It should be grounding.

Thor holds the flag and tag in one hand. The tag is wet and ugly now.

**BUCKLE SFX:**
```text
hff
```

**THOR:**
```text
Fine.
```

**THOR:**
```text
You were right about this road too.
```

---

### Panel 7

**Final panel, interior of Mallow's hab.**

Thor sits at the small fold-down desk. Buckle is a pale lump near the stove, watching him with one dark eye. The manuscript is closed. Thor's field notebook is open.

He draws a heavy black bar over the location line.

Visible revised note:

```text
INDEX ORCHID
Observed alive near low-water mineral margin.
Location: WITHHELD.
Collection: FORBIDDEN.
Public mark: NONE.
```

The final panel should feel like a hinge into Page 12.

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
The first correction was not scientific.
```

**CAPTION / THOR FIELD NOTE:**
```text
It was moral.
```

## Page Turn

Page 12 should resolve the ashcan thesis:

```text
Observed, not owned.
Location withheld.
```

The final page should not solve the Girasol, the manuscript, or the larger ecology. It should show Thor choosing restraint over proof, with Buckle present and Mallow small against the living world.
