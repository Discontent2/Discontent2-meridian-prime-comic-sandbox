# Page 05 Art Direction Lock
## Observed, Not Owned
### Page 05: The First Objective

**Status:** Sandbox Art Direction / Production Lock  
**Canon Status:** Sandbox only unless promoted through explicit canon review  
**Page:** 05  
**Related Script:** `page-05-script.md`  
**Sequence Role:** Index Orchid objective, Mallow preparation, Ledger Falls departure

---

## Core Visual Lock

```text
Thor turns the manuscript's first black-glass orchid clue into a field objective, loads Mallow in the rain, and drives out of Ledger Falls alone.
```

The page should feel practical, wet, and slightly doomed. This is the moment Thor transforms mystery into logistics.

He is not yet wise. He is simply moving.

---

## Page Mood

- field-journal urgency
- rainy Ledger Falls grit
- handmade expedition prep
- damp paper and oily machinery
- romantic ambition under practical weather
- first departure loneliness

The emotional flavor is:

```text
A young explorer leaves home because the book gave him just enough of an answer to be dangerous.
```

---

## Required Visual Beats

Must show:

1. manuscript detail with black-glass orchid clue
2. Thor translating clue into field plan
3. Mallow being loaded in rain
4. cab ignition / Thor talking to Mallow
5. Mallow leaving Ledger Falls

Must not show yet:

- Buckle
- Gift-Pod
- e-bike / Buzz-Mule
- full Girasol
- Low Road Roadhouse
- Hydropolis
- Craton
- any decoded explanation of the manuscript

## Panel Design

### Panel 1: Manuscript Detail

Close-up, heavy black page field.

The manuscript page should be mostly black ink and off-white paper texture, with strange Aeonolacertian pressure-script and a partial black-glass orchid drawing. Use **Girasol Gold** as a thin thread moving from root to marginal note.

The orchid should look like a flower made from black glass, not a normal botanical specimen.

Design notes:

- petals angular and reflective
- roots like thin cracked glass
- gold line should look discovered, not decorative
- Thor's pencil stays on separate note paper, not on the manuscript itself

---

### Panel 2: Planning Wall

Interior hab desk, medium shot.

Thor pins map scraps, copied symbols, and blank field plate to Mallow's wall. The hab should feel cramped but still orderly at this stage.

Visible objects:

- fold-down desk
- manuscript stand
- oilcloth wrap
- pencil tins
- clipped field plates
- rain-streaked small window
- empty floor space where Buckle's nest will later be
- hand level used as sight glass
- route scrap with one gold mark

Thor posture:

- hunched over desk
- intense, sleepless, fascinated
- cap on or nearby
- motoring goggles pushed up if visible

Visual subtext:

```text
Thor is trying to make the impossible behave like a field assignment.
```

---

### Panel 3: Loading Mallow

Exterior, rainy timber-margin yard.

Mallow should dominate the panel as a working vehicle, not a sleek hero machine.

Show:

- compact tracked chassis
- front blade in mud
- rounded cab
- rear hab module
- roof rack clutter
- stove pipe
- storage boxes
- mud-black tracks
- Ledger Falls mill silhouettes in background
- permit boards / logging cables / wet supply shed

Thor carries supplies between shed and rear hab.

This panel should make the reader understand that Thor is underfunded and determined.

---

### Panel 4: Cab Ignition

Tight interior cab panel.

The cab should feel intimate and battered.

Dashboard details to include if possible:

- pressure gauge
- unreliable heater switch
- cracked radio
- route lamp
- hand-painted `MALLOW` tag
- glove box sticker: `DO NOT STORE SPORES`
- rain on windshield
- Thor's gloved hand turning ignition or engaging controls

Thor's face should be half-shadowed, practical, nervous, pretending not to be.

Lighting:

- dirty amber cab lamp
- exterior gray rain light
- no gold except maybe a tiny route scrap glint

---

### Panel 5: Ledger Falls Departure

Wide exterior page-bottom panel.

Mallow drives away from Ledger Falls toward a black cloud forest road.

Composition:

- Mallow small but readable
- town receding behind it
- cloud forest ahead as huge black mass
- road wet and pale
- front blade cutting through shallow water
- mill lights behind
- fog swallowing distance

No triumphant hero shot. This is not victory. It is a little machine entering a very large mouth.

Optional visual echo:

- black branches ahead subtly form a partial antler-like rhythm, but keep it deniable

---

## Ink and Color Rules

### Black Ink

Use heavy blacks for:

- cloud forest road
- Mallow's undercarriage and tracks
- cab shadows
- roof rack silhouettes
- wet tree walls
- manuscript black-glass orchid

### White / Negative Space

Use white for:

- rain streaks
- road water reflections
- paper scraps
- window glare
- wet metal highlights

### Spot Color

Use **Girasol Gold** only for:

- the manuscript's hidden line in Panel 1
- the route mark / clue scrap in Panel 2
- maybe a barely visible glint through the cab window in Panel 5

No other spot colors should dominate this page.

---

## Lettering / Graphic Design Notes

Use field-note captions as if they are being written later in Thor's guide.

Handwritten scraps should be legible but rough.

Potential visible labels:

```text
INDEX ORCHID?
BLACK-GLASS BLOOM
LOW WATER / BAD RECORDS / NOT FOREST?
UNVERIFIED
PLATE I: INDEX ORCHID
DO NOT STORE SPORES
MALLOW
```

Do not overfill the page with tiny text. The image must still breathe.

---

## Continuity Locks

- Thor wears his dark flat cap / driving cap with early motoring goggles visible at least once on the page.
- The Kuker-Rankin hand level appears in Panel 2 or Panel 4.
- Mallow must match the vehicle sheet: compact Tucker Sno-Cat-like crawler, front blade, rounded cab, rear hab, roof rack, stove pipe.
- Buckle is absent from Page 5.
- The manuscript reacts but remains unreadable.
- The Index Orchid is an objective, not an explained plant yet.
- Thor is leaving Ledger Falls alone.

---

## Page Composition Rule

The page should move from **small clue** to **large departure**:

```text
flower mark -> field plan -> vehicle prep -> ignition -> road
```

This visual progression should make Page 5 feel like the expedition's first real step.

---

## Key Image

The strongest image on the page should be Panel 5:

```text
Mallow, a tiny tracked field den, rolling away from Ledger Falls into a black cloud forest road while Thor believes he is only chasing a flower.
```
