# Structural Beat Sheet

## Pages 1-3 - Scene 1: The Red Light in the Fog

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** Cold Open / Survival Hook  

### Beat

A present-day Traverse crew crosses a frozen auxiliary route in heavy fog. Their GPS says the route bends east, but a red light glows ahead through the mist.

The rookie thinks it is a fixed route marker.

The veteran says nothing at first.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 1:** Big eerie opener: red light in fog, black ice, track ruts, dead flags. The crew vehicle or module is tiny against the route.
- **Page 2:** Crew debate inside vehicle or on foot. GPS disagrees with visible route markers.
- **Page 3:** Page-turn reveal: the red light is moving.

### Page 3 Reveal

```text
Not a route marker.
Something shaggy is walking with the red lamp.
```

---

## Pages 4-6 - Scene 2: Ol' Jasper at the Crossed Route

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** First Cryptid Encounter  

### Beat

The crew follows cautiously.

The red light leads toward a broken route where crossed black flags form an X near a crevasse.

Ol' Jasper appears as a huge shaggy silhouette with a red headlamp, goggles, salvage packs, and a dragged micro-hab barely visible in fog.

### Purpose

Make Jasper scary first, then plant that he may be warning them.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 4:** The red light moves between fog banks. Crew tension rises.
- **Page 5:** Odd-page reveal: Jasper's full silhouette at the crevasse edge.
- **Page 6:** Jasper suddenly slams metal, hurls scrap, or starts a dead motor, terrifying the crew backward.

### Micro-Climax

```text
The rookie falls back just as the ice shelf cracks where they would have stepped.
```

---

## Pages 7-9 - Scene 3: The Wrong Flags

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** Investigation Begins  

### Beat

The crew realizes Jasper may have saved them.

They find a line of route flags continuing north, clean and official-looking, but GPS insists no safe road exists there.

The veteran recognizes the old pattern: flags that look too neat.

### Purpose

Introduce Flagstaff's hazard before showing him.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 7:** Crew regroups. Jasper vanishes into fog.
- **Page 8:** They inspect the flags. Holes are freshly drilled into frozen crust.
- **Page 9:** Odd-page reveal: far ahead, an old yellow-white machine kneels in the fog, planting another flag.

### Page 9 Reveal

```text
Flagstaff / The Flagger is active.
```

---

## Pages 10-12 - Scene 4: The Flagger's Dead Map

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** World-Building / Threat Expansion  

### Beat

The crew observes Flagstaff from a distance.

He removes a modern marker, drills into the ice, and replants an old black route flag according to obsolete data.

His machine behavior is patient, official, and wrong.

### Purpose

Clarify the issue's central danger: not monsters attacking, but old systems still functioning after the world changed.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 10:** Flagstaff's process: scan, compare, remove, carry.
- **Page 11:** The crew debates whether to stop him. The veteran says: `If the flags don't match GPS, don't assume GPS is wrong.`
- **Page 12:** The newly planted flag line points toward a field of hidden pressure cracks.

### Micro-Climax

```text
The crew's instruments pick up a buried WISSARD signal under the old flag line.
```

---

## Pages 13-15 - Scene 5: Dead Relay Signal

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** Mid-Issue Twist  

### Beat

The crew follows the WISSARD signal to a dead relay mast.

The signal is not a warning beacon.

It is an old emergency packet, looping beneath decades of static. Someone deliberately buried it in route infrastructure.

### Purpose

Shift from cryptid encounter to conspiracy mystery.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 13:** Odd-page image: dead relay mast rising from fog, cables frozen like black vines.
- **Page 14:** Technician patches into the mast. Names and data fragments begin to surface: W.A.S., Core Traverse, personnel status corrupted.
- **Page 15:** The mast knocks once from above.

### Page 15 Reveal

```text
Rookmask Jack is perched or standing near the mast, watching.
```

---

## Pages 16-18 - Scene 6: Rookmask Jack and the Old Request

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** Escalation / Emotional Unease  

### Beat

Rookmask Jack does not attack.

He watches while the relay plays a damaged recording: Mark Aggressive requesting help after the Core incident.

The message is calm, formal, and hopeless.

He knows no help is coming.

### Purpose

Reveal that Jack is not just a cryptid. He is tied to the transmission.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 16:** Jack's silhouette, staff, old Traverse pack, birdlike cowl. The crew freezes.
- **Page 17:** Odd-page reveal: the recording identifies `Mark Aggressive, Lead Science Officer, W.A.S. Traverse`. Jack reacts almost imperceptibly.
- **Page 18:** The veteran realizes the creature and the name are connected.

### Micro-Climax

```text
Jack taps the mast again.
The relay unlocks a corrupted visual file.
```

---

## Pages 19-21 - Scene 7: The Crew Image

**Page Count:** 3 pages  
**Function:** Climax / Revelation Build  

### Beat

The relay reconstructs fragments of an old WISSARD team image.

At first it looks like an expedition photo: Mark, Richard, Buck, Mara, Flagstaff.

Then corrupted overlays show present cryptid identities: Rookmask Jack, X, Ol' Jasper, Sparky, Flagstaff.

### Purpose

Deliver the issue's core reveal without overexplaining the entire arc.

### Page Rhythm

- **Page 19:** Old team image begins forming from static.
- **Page 20:** Names resolve one by one: Richard Jameson, Buck, Mara "Sparky" Cairn, Mark Aggressive.
- **Page 21:** The crew sees the impossible overlay: the monsters match the personnel file.

### Page 21 Turn Setup

```text
The technician whispers: "They weren't sightings. They were survivors."
```

---

## Page 22 - Scene 8: Cliffhanger Splash

**Page Count:** 1 page  
**Function:** Full Splash / Commercial Hook  

### Beat

Full-page splash of the reconstructed WISSARD file.

The old crew photo is split between human forms and cryptid remnants.

Rookmask Jack stands in the foreground reflection or in the relay glass.

The file header reads:

```text
W.A.S. TRAVERSE
PERSONNEL STATUS: UNRESOLVED
CORE TRANSIT INCIDENT: CLASSIFIED
```

At the bottom, a new system line appears:

```text
PHASE TWO AUTHORIZED
```

### Cliffhanger Effect

The reader now understands this is bigger than one wrong road.

WISSARD may be preparing to repeat the experiment.
