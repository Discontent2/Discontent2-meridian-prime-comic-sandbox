## Approval and GPS correction

The user approved the page after a localized correction to the GPS display in panel 5. The first generated version is superseded. Its curved cyan route, independent flag line, and dotted amber off-route path were incorrect.

The user's navigation rule is authoritative for future artwork:

- Approved routes consist of straight segments; changes of direction occur at waypoints, not along curves.
- Flags follow the approved route, including its turns; they do not form an independent divergent line.
- An off-route vehicle is a single triangle away from the route. Do not invent a route, dotted trail, or connecting path for it.

The corrected display has an angular cyan route, circular waypoint nodes, flags along that same route, and one isolated amber vehicle triangle to the right. It retains `APPROVED ROUTE` and `OFF COURSE`. The duplicate cyan vehicle triangle and amber dotted path are removed. The edit was requested only for the GPS; the rest of the approved page composition, action, and dialogue were retained.

## Continuity and future use

- Follow Page 10's controlled-stop order. This is an overland tracked crawler convoy, never a railway train.
- Crew remain inside on Page 11. Page 12 begins the load/coupling inspection; Page 13 currently deploys the GPR tractor. Do not insert a 409 deployment into this page.
- Rookie is the same human with dark hair, lowered work goggles, red cold-weather coat, black gloves, and collar mic. No worn helmet.
- The reflection remains an unexplained suggestion, not a named ghost or revealed character.
- Preserve heavy black inks, cold blue terrain and fog, dirty amber cab light, cyan instruments, white gutters, and restrained red signals.
- The GPS correction supersedes inconsistent navigation drawing in earlier references for future production. Historical script descriptions of obsolete physical flags remain draft history; this save does not rewrite earlier pages or resolve that wider story discrepancy.
- Print-resolution preparation, trim/bleed, and final lettering review remain outstanding.
