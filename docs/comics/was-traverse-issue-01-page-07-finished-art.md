## Fog revision and continuity lock

- **Keep the fog-revised image as the current Page 7 master.** Dense layered mineral mist obscures most of Jasper in every appearance, including the mirror, the damaged camera view, and the final crossing. Keep the red headlamp visible as a navigational cue; keep partial tufted ears, goggles, straps, and running posture legible enough to follow the action.
- Preserve the initial image's four-panel composition, viewpoints, poses, gloved controls, and exact dialogue/SFX. Do not move the mechanic overlay out of the corrupted camera frame or turn it into a full Buck reveal.
- Preserve the established industrial crawler convoy, practical lead cab, physical black flags, cold slate/cobalt snow, bold black ink masses, dirty amber headlights and instrument lights, and selective cyan camera graphics. The equipment is tracked, not a rail train or spaceship.
- Jasper is human-sized, shaggy and lynx-eared with round goggles, scavenged route gear, salvage straps and a red headlamp. His identity and protective intent remain unexplained until later pages. Avoid attack posing, blood, gore, a cute mascot treatment, or a clear human face beneath the gear.
- The Rookie remains the younger human in a red thermal coat with raised goggles; his hands begin the turn here. The hard correction and convoy load consequences belong to Page 8.
- No Saurasapien appears on this page. The issue-wide anatomy correction still applies elsewhere: flush/internal ears without external human pinnae.
