## Continuity and script discrepancy

- **Keep the corrected Antisapien in panel 3.** She is the same blue-skinned woman introduced as the Antisapien Signal Tech on Page 2. Her amber work glasses and visible blue profile distinguish her from the Saurasapien Traverse Lead. For this approved visual she is also shown at the rear tractor controls; future continuity should account for her location and duties at this moment.
- The v0.2 script and thumbnail call the panel-3 occupant `Rear Driver` without identifying that person as the Antisapien Signal Tech. This record documents the user-approved visual change; the earlier script wording remains as the draft history. The radio order in panel 3 still comes from the Traverse Lead in the galley.
- The Lead appears in panels 1 and 5 only. His Saurasapien head has internal/flush ears, never projecting human pinnae. He does not leave the galley or deploy the GPR scout on this page.
- Page 9 exposed the fracture Jasper was warning about. Page 10 orders the train to maintain formation to a stable stop point. Page 11 depicts the controlled stop; Page 13 currently depicts the GPR tractor rolling out. The decision to deploy that tractor was discussed separately after this page was generated and has **not** been inserted into this finished image or the script by this record. The vehicle is not named `409` on this page.
- Preserve the practical tracked convoy and radio procedure. The apparent older cadence in panel 4 remains only a texture in static, with no readable ghost message.

This 1024 × 1536 PNG is a visual working master. Final lettering review and print-resolution trim/bleed preparation remain outstanding.
