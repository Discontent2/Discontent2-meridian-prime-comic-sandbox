## Core Summary

X is an escaped WISSARD / World Works anti-world crossover experiment, originally part of a crustacean-interface program that tested whether Meridian Prime crustacean life could survive partial Anti-world exposure.

Crustaceans were considered promising candidates because of their shell logic, pressure tolerance, modular body structure, and apparent resilience under extreme environmental stress.

The program was abandoned after the classified **Antidrill Incident**.

X survived.

He escaped.

He now exists as a horror cryptid with anti-world altered awareness and a focused vendetta against World Works.

He is not a random monster.

He is a lab survivor, a failed threshold organism, and a walking consequence.

## Origin Direction

X was created or altered during early WISSARD / World Works anti-world interface testing.

The program investigated whether Meridian crustacean organisms could survive conditions that destroyed or destabilized other biological candidates.

The exact nature of the Antidrill Incident remains unresolved.

Possible interpretations:

- X partially crossed into the Anti-world and returned changed.
- X was exposed to an anti-world interface event during a drilling experiment.
- X was the only surviving crustacean candidate from a failed test series.
- X was not the intended result but became the only remaining proof.
- X remembers the incident in a non-human, anti-world altered way.

Do not overdefine the Antidrill Incident yet.

## Awareness

X has **anti-world altered awareness**.

This does not mean he thinks like a normal human.

It means he recognizes patterns, symbols, machinery, procedures, and institutional harm in a warped but meaningful way.

He may recognize:

- World Works marks
- WISSARD equipment
- bore systems
- drills and augers
- anti-world interface machinery
- containment architecture
- repeating lab procedures
- personnel behavior associated with harm

His hatred should feel like trauma made directional, not ordinary villainy.

## Vendetta

X has a focused vendetta against World Works and WISSARD-linked infrastructure.

Reported or likely behavior:

- sabotages bore equipment
- destroys drills, augers, and core samplers
- scrapes away World Works symbols
- haunts abandoned WISSARD corridors
- appears near sealed test sites
- reacts violently to anti-world interface technology
- avoids or spares unrelated travelers unless they activate dangerous systems
- targets the machinery and procedure-patterns tied to his creation

He does not hunt purely for food.

He follows drilling because he remembers what drilling did to him.

## Anatomy

### Head / Face

- Clearly deep-sea isopod inspired.
- Pale crustacean face.
- Broad blunt shield-like face structure.
- Wide-set eyes.
- Antennae.
- Mouthparts.
- Not a human face.
- Not a simple insect head.
- Emotion should come from tilt, stillness, limb behavior, and attention rather than facial expression.

### Arms

X has at least four visible arms.

**Upper arms:**

- normal-ish proportions
- readable from a distance as almost human arm placement
- each hand has two fingers and one fixed thumb
- hand structure should feel like a warped crawfish manipulator

**Lower arms:**

- slightly smaller than upper arms
- emerge below the upper pair
- each ends in one pincer and one fixed thumb
- more crustacean and more unsettling than the upper arms

**Hidden folded third lower arms:**

- optional and only partly visible
- one pointer-like limb each
- no thumb
- should feel folded, vestigial, or concealed until needed

### Legs

Current simplified visual version uses two main walking legs.

Earlier development considered four legs with longer front legs and smaller rear legs, but the current clean-palette version removes visible rear legs to preserve the almost-human distance read.

If rear/extra legs return later, they should remain folded, hidden, or disturbing rather than making X read like an ordinary arthropod quadruped.

### Clothing

Current lock:

- black hoodie
- gray Carhartt-style cargo pants
- practical found clothing, not chitin clothing yet
- hoodie first, carapace second
- clothing should make him almost readable as a person from far away

Future variations may explore fabricated plated chitin clothing or a biological hoodie, but this file preserves the current design reset.
