## Purpose

This file records a new development direction for Rookmask Jack in the W.A.S. Traverse comic lane.

The original Rookmask Jack file treats him as a living hybrid-adjacent threshold cryptid. This companion file does not delete that material. It adds a stronger W.A.S.-specific revision candidate:

```text
Rookmask Jack may not be Mark Aggressive's transformed body.
Rookmask Jack may be a machine body Mark Aggressive can still reach from the Core.
```

Until canon review, keep both as sandbox layers. For Issue #1 v0.2, use this infrastructure-body interpretation as the preferred comic direction.

---

# Core Revision

Preferred principle:

```text
Rookmask Jack is not Mark Aggressive's body.
Rookmask Jack is what Mark Aggressive can still reach.
```

Working premise:

```text
Mark Aggressive never fully left the Core.
His WISSARD authority, credentials, guilt, and infrastructure access survived as signal behavior.
Rookmask Jack is a route-service body, relay robot, scavenged machine shell, or WISSARD-linked infrastructure unit that Mark can operate, haunt, or authenticate through.
```

This makes Jack less like a standard monster transformation and more like a dead route system wearing a guilty man's authority.

---

## Why This Works

### It preserves Mark's tragedy

Mark does not get the same return as the rest of the team. He does not get to simply become a creature on the road.

Instead:

```text
Buck returns through Jasper.
Richard returns through X.
Mara returns through Sparky.
Flagstaff returns through damaged route logic.
Mark remains trapped in the Core but leaks into infrastructure.
```

Mark's punishment is access without arrival.

### It strengthens WISSARD logic

Mark was WISSARD-appointed and had access to mission systems, route authority, relay infrastructure, and classified procedure.

Rookmask Jack as a machine-body witness makes his presence at dead relay masts feel operational instead of random.

He can knock masts because the masts still answer him.

### It makes the folklore stranger

Field crews can still call him Rookmask Jack because they see the hood, cowl, staff, mast-knocking, and witness behavior.

But the truth under the folklore is colder:

```text
The route worker saw a cryptid.
The relay saw valid credentials.
The Core saw Mark.
```

# Visual Direction

Rookmask Jack should retain his folklore silhouette:

```text
hooded black robe
birdlike plague-mask-like cowl
crooked staff
old Traverse pack or scavenged field debris
stillness at relay masts
watcher posture
```

But Issue #1 v0.2 and later art may introduce machine-body clues:

```text
servo-stiff posture
metal foot under robe hem
mechanical ankle or knee glimpsed briefly
cowl as service housing rather than organic face
optic glint rather than animal eye shine
cable or contact port under the robe
staff with contact node or relay key
old WISSARD tag hidden among route debris
relay mast responding to Jack's tap like an authenticated command
```

Do not make him a sleek robot.

He should feel like:

```text
obsolete route-service machinery wearing folklore
```

---

# Behavior Direction

Rookmask Jack remains primarily a witness, not an attacker.

His behavior should now read as infrastructure behavior translated into folklore:

```text
stands at relay masts
knocks metal before route failures
appears where files, routes, and testimony disagree
causes dead systems to wake
uses staff like a credential key
stares at crews as if auditing them
responds to old WISSARD signal paths
moves with machine patience
```

Field saying reinterpretation:

```text
If Jack knocks the mast, shut the rig down.
```

Possible hidden truth:

```text
If Jack knocks the mast, Mark Aggressive found a way to issue one more warning.
```

# Issue #1 Reveal Discipline

Do not explain the infrastructure-body truth in Issue #1.

Issue #1 should only imply that the figure at the mast is not a normal biological cryptid.

Allowed clues:

```text
Jack stands too still.
Jack's staff interfaces with the mast.
A mechanical foot or joint is visible for one panel.
The relay wakes after Jack taps it.
Jack's cowl catches light like blackened ceramic or old machine housing.
Jack's reflection overlays Mark Aggressive's personnel file.
```

Forbidden in Issue #1:

```text
no dialogue explaining Mark controls Jack
no narration saying Mark never left the Core
no full reveal that Jack is a robot body
no C.A.S.E. mention
no early Mark Aggressive identity before Page 22
```

Reader question at the end of Issue #1:

```text
Why does that machine have a personnel file?
```

Not:

```text
Mark Aggressive is controlling a robot from the Core.
```
