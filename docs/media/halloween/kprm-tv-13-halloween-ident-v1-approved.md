## Approved Visual Identity

An original nostalgic **1980s local broadcast ident**, informed by the general language of analog optical effects and electronic wireframe television animation without reproducing existing television-network logos.

### Permanent Station Mark

- **Meridian Beacon:** luminous geometric transmission symbol built around a long vertical copper-gold spire and interlocking elliptical orbital rays.
- A centered, bright, blue-violet **eight-point signal star** is the initiation image.
- Warm gold / copper filaments progressively form the station's geometric orbital emblem.
- Transmission lines stretch into an amber, ruby, magenta, and electric-blue **triangular signal corridor**.
- Exact station title: **KPRM-TV 13**, set in gold, readable against the electronic geometry.
- Seasonal Halloween variant ends with the signal corridor transforming into a handmade, moonlit graveyard and crooked woodland.

### Palette and Construction

Deep broadcast black; phosphor blue and violet; rich copper and gold; magenta and ruby highlight flares; analog optical light trails; modest CRT texture; faint registration softness. The destination forest uses violet moonlight, leafless black branches, low blue fog, tiny amber points of light, wooden grave markers, and physical-looking miniature scenery.

The calm, dignified network presentation must contrast with the progressively unsettling final transformation. Avoid modern aggressive glitch aesthetics, smooth generic CGI, and any recognizable proprietary station logo.
