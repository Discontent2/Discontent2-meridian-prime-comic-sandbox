# Meridian Prime — 80-Page Coffee Table Book Spread Plan

**Status:** Sandbox Editorial Development / Non-Canon Until Promoted  
**Repository:** `Discontent2/Discontent2-meridian-prime-comic-sandbox`  
**Working Title:** *MERIDIAN PRIME: The Road Remembers*  
**Format Assumption:** 80 interior pages, cover and endpapers separate  
**Structure:** 40 editorial spreads  
**Primary Use:** Coffee-table picture book planning, Midjourney production planning, captions, visual consistency, canon-safe image sequencing  
**Date:** 2026-09-28  

## Editorial Principle

This book should feel less like a conventional lore encyclopedia and more like a photographic expedition through Meridian Prime.

The reader moves from the official world into the field world, then into places where the record begins to fray.

The strongest visual grammar in the repositories is:

- route
- season
- threshold
- memory
- machinery as culture
- official maps versus field truth
- physical borders as wounds
- beauty under lethal conditions

The first half should say:

> Look at this world.

The second half should increasingly ask:

> Are you sure you understand what you're looking at?

## Canon Handling Rule

Main-canon material may be presented directly.

Planning-canon and visual-development material may be used carefully and should retain its status in production notes.

Sandbox ecology, W.A.S., C.A.S.E., cryptids, and other non-promoted material must not be silently presented as settled canon.

W.A.S. material in particular should be framed as disputed, recovered, apocryphal, archival, folkloric, or otherwise uncertain unless promoted later.

# Production Observations

## Hydropolis and the Craton Need Major Space

Both locations possess distinctive visual mechanics strong enough to carry multiple spreads.

Hydropolis physically changes between wet-season canal city and dry-season exposed infrastructure.

The Craton provides one of the strongest threshold sequences in the project:

cold mineral exterior  
→ dead corporate borer  
→ sovereign gate  
→ humid living saurian interior

These should not be compressed into one-page location entries.

## MITE II Should Be Photographed Rather Than Explained

409, Red Umbrielor, Discovery, the Mods, fuel tails, Aquifer Drill, and Traverse Town already form a coherent machine culture.

Their power comes from use, maintenance, ritual, fatigue, and procedure.

Treat them the way an expedition photographer treats snowcats, bush aircraft, ships, camps, field kitchens, and worn tools.

## Seasonality Is a Signature Feature

The same place can generate radically different photographs.

Strong paired-spread opportunities include:

- Hydropolis wet / Hydropolis dry
- Eschar dry / Eschar wet
- Great Cut road / Great Cut cataract
- Blackstep exposed / Blackstep maritime
- any later promoted ecology that changes state by season

## W.A.S. Should Not Be Accidentally Canonized

The W.A.S. sequence works best as uncertain evidence.

Production possibilities:

- different paper texture
- contact sheet borders
- damaged archive numbers
- partial redactions
- misregistered type
- stamped uncertainty labels
- recovered relay-frame stills
- captions written in a different editorial voice

The visual distinction should signal that the book itself no longer knows exactly what category of evidence it is presenting.

## Final Structural Effect

The book begins with The Lodestar as a magnificent founding image.

It ends with the same object after the reader has crossed:

- Bought Nation labor geography
- seasonal route systems
- Hydropolis
- Contact Frame diplomacy
- Craton sovereignty
- Eschar transformation
- contradictory maps
- Traverse field life
- disputed W.A.S. material

The object remains unchanged.

Its meaning does not.
