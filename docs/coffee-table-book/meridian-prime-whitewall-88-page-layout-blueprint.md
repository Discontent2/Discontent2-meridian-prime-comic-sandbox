# 1. Pagination Lock

The 88-page interior is designed according to physical book pagination:

- odd-numbered pages are **right-hand / recto**
- even-numbered pages are **left-hand / verso**
- intended double-page photographs therefore live on **even–odd page pairs**
- the forty narrative spreads begin on **pages 4–5**, not pages 5–6

Final architecture:

- **Page 1:** half-title, right-hand opening page
- **Pages 2–3:** title / expedition statement
- **Pages 4–83:** 40 narrative spreads
- **Pages 84–85:** coda
- **Pages 86–87:** colophon / production note
- **Page 88:** final black page

The cover and endpapers are separate WhiteWall production elements and are not counted inside these 88 pages.

---

# 2. Design Grammar

## A. Cinema Bleed

A hero photograph crosses the gutter and fills the complete 58 × 29 cm visual field.

Use only when the source survives a 2:1 crop without damaging:
- faces
- small vehicles
- critical anatomy
- route markers
- important silhouettes

Captions live in the **lower outer corner**, never on the gutter.

## B. Photograph + Black

One image fills or nearly fills one square page. The facing page is near-black and carries:
- spread title, if needed
- caption
- sparse location / archive notation

This is the preferred treatment for portraits, vertical scenes, and images whose original composition should not be mutilated into 2:1.

## C. Hero + Inserts

One page or most of the spread belongs to the LOCK image. The facing page becomes an editorial field for one or more INSERT photographs.

Use black space aggressively. Inserts should never become a tiled social-media gallery.

## D. Evidence / Archive

Beginning with W.A.S., the book’s clean photographic grammar deliberately degrades:
- contact-sheet borders
- photographed prints
- uneven alignment
- partial cropping
- typewriter / archive-style notation added in layout
- uncertain provenance

Do not allow generated text inside source imagery to become authoritative typography.

## E. Black Space

Black is an active design element.

Use empty or nearly empty black pages to:
- protect a portrait
- slow pacing
- create scale
- isolate a caption
- signal uncertainty
- prevent the book from becoming 88 pages of continuous spectacle

---

# 3. Caption System

Main captions use the production-board language already approved.

General placement:
- **Cinema Bleed:** 8–9 pt caption in lower outside corner where image is darkest.
- **Photograph + Black:** caption on the black page, lower outside quadrant.
- **Hero + Inserts:** caption sits in a dedicated black cell, never over a small insert.
- **Archive:** caption is treated as file notation or recovered-description text.
- **Coda:** no ordinary spread caption.

Keep a minimum safety distance from trim and gutter. No critical type crosses the center seam.

Running folios:
- small
- outer bottom corner
- suppressed on pp.1–3, pp.84–88
- section title may replace folio text on the first spread of each section
